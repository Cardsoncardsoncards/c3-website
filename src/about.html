About C3

Who runs this site, what we publish, where the prices come from, and how any of it gets paid for.

What C3 is

Cards on Cards on Cards, or C3, is an independent trading card game price and card intelligence site. We track card prices across 32 trading card games, convert them to Australian dollars, and publish set listings, price history, release dates and guides on top of that data.

We are not a marketplace and we do not hold your cards. Everything on the site is information, plus links out to where a card is actually for sale.

Who runs it

C3 is a small, independently owned Australian operation. There is no newsroom behind it and no outside investor. Everything published here is written, checked and signed off by the same small team, which is why every post and every email is signed The C3 Team rather than by an individual byline.

Cards on Cards on Cards is a registered business name of Voxsanity Pty Ltd, ABN 82 700 348 867, Sydney, NSW, Australia. The full company and contact details are on our legal page.

Where the numbers come from

Card catalogue data and base pricing come from licensed and community data providers, are converted to AUD daily, and are stamped with the time they were last updated. Not every game gets the same depth of coverage, and rather than hide that, each page carries a confidence badge saying how much to trust the number in front of you.

The full list of sources, update frequencies and what each confidence level means is on how we price cards. If a number looks wrong to you, it might be, and we would rather hear about it.

How the site is funded

C3 earns a commission on qualifying purchases made through eBay and Amazon links on this site, through the eBay Partner Network and Amazon Associates. This does not change the price you pay. We also run a card store on eBay AU.

Affiliate links pay for the site, so it matters that we say plainly what they do and do not affect. They do not change the prices we display, which come from our data sources and not from any seller, and they do not decide which cards or sets get a page. The full disclosure sits on our legal page, and a short version appears in the footer of every page here.

How we handle corrections

Two things on a site like this go wrong regularly: a price goes stale, and a card gets attached to the wrong set. Both are worth telling us about. Every page carries a Report a Bug button in the bottom corner, and you can also contact us directly.

Where a published guide turns out to be wrong rather than just out of date, we correct the page itself rather than quietly deleting it.
