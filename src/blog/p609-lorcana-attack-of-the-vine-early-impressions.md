Disney Lorcana's thirteenth main set, Attack of the Vine!, released worldwide on 24 July 2026. Five days in, packs are open, decks are built, and the second Pixar-featuring set in Lorcana's history is already reshaping what a summer set can look like.

## Monsters Inc joins the roster

Attack of the Vine! follows Sulley, Mike Wazowski and Boo from Pixar's Monsters Inc, alongside Turning Red glimmers, as the Illumineers team up to stop an unstoppable vine growing out of control. This is only the second time Pixar characters have appeared in Lorcana, after Wilds Unknown brought Toy Story, Brave and The Incredibles into the game back in May.

Two Pixar sets in three months is a genuine shift in direction for a game that spent its first two years leaning almost entirely on classic Disney animation. It also signals that Ravensburger sees enough crossover appeal in Pixar's audience to keep drawing from it rather than treating Wilds Unknown as a one-off experiment.

## What rotated out

Attack of the Vine!'s arrival also triggers Lorcana's set rotation policy, and four Year 2 sets, Shimmering Skies, Azurite Sea, Archazia's Island and Reign of Jafar, have rotated out of Core Constructed play. They remain fully legal in other formats, so nothing in your existing collection has become unplayable, but if you build exclusively for Core Constructed, this is the moment to check your decks against the new legal pool.

## The rarity ladder, and where the real chases sit

Lorcana runs an eight-tier rarity system: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Super Rare, Legendary, Epic, Enchanted and Iconic. Enchanted cards are the classic chase, pulling at roughly 1 in 100 packs, while Iconic sits above even Epic as the rarest tier in the game, with only two Iconic cards printed per set. Five days into a set's life is early for firm secondary market signals, but these are the two tiers worth watching first as more packs get opened.

## Where this fits in Lorcana's 2026 run

Ravensburger has stuck to its usual three to four month cadence this year, and Attack of the Vine! is the third main set of 2026 after Winterspell in February and Wilds Unknown in May. The next stop is Hyperia City, with a prerelease on 16 October and a general release on 23 October, bringing Coco characters like Miguel and Hector Rivera into the game.

That is a way off yet, so Attack of the Vine! has a genuine window as the newest set on shelves. Three months is long enough for a set's real chase cards to be identified and for prices to settle, without a newer release pulling collector attention away in the meantime.

## The bigger story arc this set feeds into

Attack of the Vine! also sits ahead of Illumineer's Quest: The Great Hunny Rescue, a self-contained cooperative multiplayer experience due in October and built around Hunny Wizard Pooh across four difficulty levels. Ravensburger has described it as the conclusion to the game's current story arc, which puts Attack of the Vine! in the position of an active middle chapter rather than a standalone release. Worth knowing if you are the kind of collector who tracks a game's overarching narrative alongside its individual sets.

## What this means for Australian collectors

With Pixar now a confirmed recurring theme rather than a one-off crossover, demand for both Wilds Unknown and Attack of the Vine! is likely to be shaped by how the wider Pixar fanbase responds, not just the existing Lorcana player base. That is a different demand signal to earlier Disney-only sets, and worth watching over the next few weeks as pricing settles.
