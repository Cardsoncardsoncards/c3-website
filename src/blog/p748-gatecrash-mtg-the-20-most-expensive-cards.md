Gatecrash is the second set in the Return to Ravnica block, released on 1 February 2013, and it remains best known for its guild shocklands and a run of hybrid and multicoloured spells built around ten of the plane's guilds. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest at number 1 down to number 20.

Every card in this set carries a single priced printing, so there are no reprint variants to weigh against each other and no ties to note this time round. Where a future update to this list does find two printings sharing a price exactly, that tie will be flagged rather than quietly broken. The ranking method itself stays constant: each card's highest priced printing determines its spot on the list.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a few cents, sometimes more, so treat every figure here as current rather than fixed.
