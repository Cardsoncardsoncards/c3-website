Return to Ravnica landed on the 5th of October 2012, returning players to the ten-guild city plane and reintroducing shocklands to Magic for the first time since the original Ravnica block. It is a set that punches well above its weight in Commander, thanks to a run of guild-fixing lands and efficient spells that have stayed in decks for over a decade. This list ranks the 20 most played Return to Ravnica cards in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower rank number means the card shows up in more decklists.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card had more than one printing within the set. Where that happens, we rank by the card's best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and we price it by whichever printing carries the higher tag. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. For this particular set, every card here has just a single priced printing, so there is no splitting to do this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as accurate to the moment it was pulled rather than a fixed number. Readers on the hunt for these cards can look through our own card pages or check eBay Australia, where availability and pricing will vary from what is listed below.
