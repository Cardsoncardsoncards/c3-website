Betrayers of Kamigawa is the second set in the Kamigawa block, released on 4 February 2005. It sits between Champions of Kamigawa and Saviors of Kamigawa, and it introduced a run of cards that have held collector interest for years afterwards. This post looks at the 20 cards from the set with the highest Australian dollar prices right now, ordered from number 1, the most expensive, down to number 20.

Every card on this list appears once, even where a card has more than one printing within the set. Where a card carries several printings, the ranking uses whichever printing is priced highest, and any printing that shares a price with another is noted rather than silently merged into one figure. In this particular set, every card here has only a single priced printing, so there is no tie-breaking to speak of, but the methodology stays the same across every set we cover.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot at the time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes by a fair amount, so the figures below should be read as a point in time rather than a fixed value. The most expensive card in the set by this measure is Higure, the Still Wind, at AU$27.83.
