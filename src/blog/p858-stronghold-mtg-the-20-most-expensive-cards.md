Stronghold is the second set in the Tempest block, released on the 2nd of March 1998, and it remains one of the more tightly designed sets from that era of Magic. This post looks at the 20 most expensive cards from Stronghold by current Australian dollar price, drawing on singles pricing rather than any claim about play patterns or long term value. It's a snapshot of a market, not a recommendation.

Every card on this list appears once. Where a card has more than one printing within Stronghold itself, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out plainly rather than being broken by some arbitrary tiebreaker. As it happens, every card in this particular top twenty has just a single priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or multi-printing gaps to untangle this time round, but the methodology stays the same regardless.

Prices below are quoted as they stand at the time of writing, in Australian dollars, and Magic singles pricing shifts constantly with supply, demand and the broader state of the secondary market. What you see here is accurate for now but should be treated as a point in time rather than a fixed number. For readers looking to buy, singles can be found through the card pages on our own site as well as through eBay Australia, and it's worth checking both before settling on a purchase.

The list runs from number 1, the single most expensive card in the set, down to number 20. Mox Diamond sits well clear of everything else on this list, and the drop-off from there down to the rest of the top twenty is worth paying attention to as you work through the entries below.
