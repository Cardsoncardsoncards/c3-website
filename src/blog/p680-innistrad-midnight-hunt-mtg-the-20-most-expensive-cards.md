Innistrad: Midnight Hunt returned Magic: The Gathering to its gothic horror roots when it released on 2021-09-24, bringing werewolves, vampires, spirits and a whole village of double-faced cards back into the game. It sits alongside its companion set, Crimson Vow, as one of two Innistrad-themed releases from that stretch of 2021, and it has settled into the kind of collector interest that comes with a well-liked, mechanically dense set. This post looks purely at the numbers behind that interest, ranking the twenty priciest cards in the set by their current value in Australian dollars.

The methodology is simple to state but worth spelling out. Each card appears once on this list, even if Wizards printed it more than once within Midnight Hunt itself, such as through showcase frames or borderless treatments. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked according to whichever printing currently commands the highest price, and the price of the other printing (or printings) is noted alongside it rather than folded into the ranking. If two printings happen to land on the exact same figure, that tie is called out plainly rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Every card here carries at least two priced printings in the set, so the gap between a card's headline price and its other printing's price becomes part of the story. Some of these gaps are narrow, just a handful of cents; others are wide enough that the second printing is worth well under half the headline figure. Reading that gap tells you something about how the market treats different frame treatments or rarities of the same card, without needing to guess at demand or reasons behind it.

One card stands clearly above the rest of the field. The Meathook Massacre tops this list at AU$46.93, and its distance from the number two card is the single largest gap anywhere in this ranking. Keep in mind throughout that every figure here is a snapshot. Prices for Magic singles move constantly, and a card's position on this list today is not a guarantee of where it will sit next month or next year.
