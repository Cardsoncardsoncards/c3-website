Mirrodin Besieged is the 2011 Magic: The Gathering set that dropped players into the middle of the Phyrexian invasion of Mirrodin, and it remains one of the more densely collected small sets from that era thanks to a run of cards that saw serious tournament play. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by their price in Australian dollars, running from number 1 at the top down to number 20 at the bottom.

Where a card has more than one printing within Mirrodin Besieged, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the higher price, and any case where two printings land on exactly the same figure is called out rather than quietly smoothed over. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just one priced printing in the set, so there is no splitting of a single card across multiple rows.

All figures here are in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot in time. Singles prices on Magic cards move constantly with supply, demand and the broader market, so treat these numbers as a point of reference rather than a fixed value. For current pricing, readers can check the individual card pages on our own site or compare against listings on eBay Australia.
