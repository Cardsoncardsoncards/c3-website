Alara Reborn closed out the Shards of Alara block back on 2009-04-30, gluing together the five-colour and wedge themes that the previous two sets had spent building. It is a small set by design, packed with gold cards, but a surprising number of them found lasting homes in Commander decks. This list ranks the twenty most played Alara Reborn cards in that format, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decks.

Every card here appears once on the list. Where a card had more than one printing within the set, we took its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, and priced it using whichever printing carries the highest price. If two printings happened to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it turns out, none of the cards below have more than one priced printing in this set, so the maths stays simple this time around.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. The rank itself is a separate thing again, based on deckbuilding data rather than price, and the two do not always move together.
