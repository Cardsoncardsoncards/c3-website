Ninth Edition landed on the 29th of July, 2005, one of the core sets that reprinted staples for a wide audience rather than introducing new mechanics. It's a set built entirely from reprints, which means its Commander relevance today comes almost entirely from which older cards it happened to carry forward, not from anything new it added to the game. That makes it a useful lens for looking at what still gets played, decades on.

This list ranks the twenty most played Ninth Edition cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks. Each card appears once even if Ninth Edition printed it more than once. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, the card is ranked by whichever printing has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of those printings is currently most expensive. Every card here happens to have two printings in the set, but only one of the two carries a tracked price, so there's no tie to note this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure below as current at time of writing rather than fixed.
