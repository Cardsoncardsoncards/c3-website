Alliances landed back on the 10th of June 1996, a small expansion to Ice Age that has aged into one of the more tightly watched sets from Magic's early years. It is a compact list of cards, but the spread between the top and bottom of this particular ranking is wide, running from Lake of the Dead's AU$165.33 down to Soldevi Adnate at AU$6.93. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20.

Each card appears once on this list. Where a card has more than one printing within Alliances, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the higher price, and any other printings are noted alongside it rather than folded away. Two cards on this list, Kjeldoran Outpost and Thought Lash, share an identical headline price, and that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved by some arbitrary tiebreak. Soldevi Adnate is the only entry with a second printing carrying its own listed price, sitting well below its headline figure.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at the time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes slowly and sometimes not, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value.
