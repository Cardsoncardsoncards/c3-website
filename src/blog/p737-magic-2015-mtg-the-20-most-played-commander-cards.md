Magic 2015 was a core set released on the 18th of July, 2014, built as a broad, accessible entry point that nonetheless left a real mark on Commander tables. This list ranks its twenty most played cards in that format using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. It is not a price list and not a power ranking, it is a play frequency ranking, and the prices attached to each entry are simply what the market happens to be doing at the same time.

Every card here appears once, even where a card had more than one printing within the set. Our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank for that card and pairs it with the highest priced printing available, so if a card had two versions in the set the number you see reflects whichever printing carries the higher tag. Where two printings land on exactly the same price we say so rather than picking one arbitrarily. In this particular set, though, every card on the list happens to have only one priced printing, so the arithmetic stays simple throughout.

The spread from top to bottom is wide. Evolving Wilds sits at EDHREC rank 19 as the most played card from the set by a comfortable margin, while Satyr Wayfinder closes the list at rank 1736. Prices run from a few cents up past AU$80.00 for the set's priciest entry, and that range says more about supply and printing history than it does about how often a card is actually run.

Keep in mind that every figure quoted below is a snapshot. Prices on both our own card pages and on eBay Australia move constantly, driven by supply, demand and reprint news, so a number that is accurate today may drift within weeks. Use this list to understand where these cards sit relative to each other in Commander play, not as a permanent price guide.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Magic 2015 release?**
Magic 2015 released on the 18th of July, 2014, and it is a core set that has already fully released, so every card on this list has had years of Commander play to accumulate its current EDHREC rank.

**What does this list actually measure?**
It ranks the twenty most played Magic 2015 cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower rank number means the card appears in more decks. It is a play frequency measure, not a measure of price or power.

**How are cards with more than one printing in the set handled?**
Each card appears only once on the list regardless of how many printings it had within Magic 2015. The card is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing, with any exact price ties between printings noted rather than resolved silently.

**Which card is the most played from this set in Commander?**
Evolving Wilds is the most played card from Magic 2015 in Commander, sitting at EDHREC rank 19, well ahead of every other card on this list.

**Where can Australian readers buy these singles?**
Singles can be bought through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which are referenced as the buying options for this list.

**Are the prices on this list fixed?**
No, every price here is a snapshot taken at the time of writing. Prices on both our site and eBay Australia move over time with supply and demand, so figures can shift in either direction after publication.

**Why do some lower ranked cards cost more than higher ranked ones?**
Play rank and price are measuring different things, one tracks how often a card appears in decks and the other tracks market supply and demand. That is why a card like Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth can carry a high price despite sitting outside the very top of the play rank order.
