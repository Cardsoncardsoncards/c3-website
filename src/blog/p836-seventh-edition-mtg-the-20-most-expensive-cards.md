Seventh Edition landed on 11 April 2001 as one of Magic: The Gathering's core set releases, drawing cards forward from earlier blocks into a fresh black-bordered printing. Because core sets exist to reprint staples for a new audience, the set mixes format staples, cycle lands and the odd oddball rare, which shows up clearly once you sort the checklist by price. This list runs through the twenty most expensive cards in the set by current Australian dollar price, from number one down to number twenty.

Where a card has more than one printing within Seventh Edition, it appears once on this list, ranked by whichever of its printings carries the higher price. If two printings happened to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. In this particular set, every card below has only one of its two printings carrying a listed price, so the comparison is simple, but the method stays the same for any set where it isn't.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot rather than a fixed reading. Singles markets shift with supply, so the gap between the top of this list and the bottom, or between any two neighbouring cards, is worth reading as a moment in time rather than a permanent ranking.

Each card appears once, ranked by whichever of its printings within Seventh Edition carries the higher price. Where two printings share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than silently broken.

**Do all the printings of these cards carry a listed price?**
No. Each of the twenty cards on this list has two printings in Seventh Edition, but only one printing of each carries a price, which is the figure used for ranking.
