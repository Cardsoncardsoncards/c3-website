Guildpact landed on the third of February, 2006, as the second chapter of the Ravnica block, and it is remembered as one of the tighter, more efficient sets to come out of that era. Nearly two decades later, it still turns up in Commander decks with surprising regularity, largely on the strength of its guild lands, signets and bounce lands, all of which remain functional pieces of manabase design.

This list ranks the twenty most played Guildpact cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means more decks are running that card. Godless Shrine tops the list at rank 62, a considerable distance ahead of everything else here, and the numbers fall away steeply from there, with the bottom half of the list sitting in the thousands rather than the hundreds.

Where a card has more than one printing within Guildpact itself, our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank to decide its position, and prices it using the highest priced of those printings. If two printings happen to share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than being broken arbitrarily. In this particular set every card here only carries a single priced printing, so no such ties come into play, but the rule stands for sets where they do.

Every price below is a snapshot taken at time of writing. Card prices in Australian dollars move constantly with supply, demand and the broader market, so treat these figures as a point of reference rather than a permanent quote.
