Theros Beyond Death arrived on the 24th of January, 2020, returning players to a plane built on Greek myth, gods, and the underworld beneath it. It gave Commander players a dense run of enemy-coloured temples, a handful of gods that double as enchantment creatures, and a late-game combo piece or two that has stuck around in cube and constructed conversation ever since. This post looks at the twenty cards from that set with the most play in Commander decks today, using EDHREC rank as the yardstick, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decklists.

Some of these cards were printed more than once within Theros Beyond Death itself, whether through a showcase treatment, a promotional version, or a second finish. Where that happens, the card is only listed once, and it is placed by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank. Its headline price is the highest priced printing among its versions, with the other printings' prices noted separately so the spread between them is visible at a glance. Where two printings land on the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or another.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week and sometimes day to day, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which will reflect more current pricing than this list.

The order below runs from number 1, the most played card in the set by EDHREC rank, down to number 20. Read on for how the field shakes out.
