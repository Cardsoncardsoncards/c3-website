Tarkir: Dragonstorm returned to the wedge-coloured plane of Tarkir, released on 2025-04-11, and it did what Tarkir sets tend to do: it filled Commander decks with fixing before anyone even had a chance to open a dragon. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower rank means a card shows up in more decks. The most played card in the whole set sits at rank 19, which is a startlingly low number for anything that isn't a format staple, and that honour goes to a piece of mana fixing rather than a dragon or a planeswalker.

Some of these cards have more than one printing within the set itself. Where that happens, the card is ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and priced by whichever printing costs the most, with the other printing's price noted separately. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over. This means the headline price you see is not always the only price in play, and the gap between a card's printings can say something on its own about how the market has treated foil, borderless or other alternate versions differently from the base version.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly, and a figure that's accurate today may not be accurate in a month. **What does this list actually measure?**
It ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower rank means the card appears in more decks.
