Eighth Edition is one of the old core sets, released on 28 July 2003 as a straight refresh of the Magic: The Gathering foundation, drawing cards from across the game's history rather than debuting new mechanics. It followed the design template of earlier core sets, mixing reprints of long standing staples with a handful of cards making their first core set appearance. This list looks at the 20 most expensive cards from that set, ranked by price in Australian dollars.

Where a card has more than one printing inside Eighth Edition, only the highest priced printing sets its position on the list, and any other printing is noted separately rather than added in. When two printings land on the exact same price, that tie is stated plainly rather than quietly broken by some other factor. In this particular set almost every card carries a price on a single tracked printing, with a second printing recorded but not priced, so the ranking is close to a simple ordering of the priced cards.

Grave Pact sits at the top of the list, at AU$59.52, well clear of the next entry. From there the list runs down through a mix of rares and a single uncommon, ending just above the six dollar mark. As always with singles pricing, these figures are a snapshot only. Prices for Magic cards move over time with supply, so treat the numbers here as a point in time rather than a fixed value.
