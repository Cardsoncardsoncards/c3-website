Innistrad landed on the 30th of September 2011 and remains one of the most fondly remembered sets Wizards of the Coast has put out, a gothic horror block built around werewolves, vampires, spirits and a graveyard theme that reshaped how double-faced cards work. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by current price in Australian dollars, drawing on the single highest priced printing where a card has more than one version available. It runs from number 1, the most expensive, down to number 20.

Where a card has several printings in the set, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved in one direction. As it happens, none of the twenty cards below have multiple priced printings within Innistrad itself, so each entry reflects a single line of pricing data rather than a comparison between versions.

Every price quoted here is a snapshot rather than a fixed value. Magic singles move constantly with supply, demand and the broader market, so the figures attached to each card reflect where things stood at the time of writing rather than a permanent number. Readers can track singles through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which will reflect more current pricing than any static list.
