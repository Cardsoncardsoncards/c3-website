Lorwyn Eclipsed released on 23 January 2026, and this list looks at the twenty most expensive cards from the set by Australian dollar price. It is a snapshot, not a permanent ranking, since singles prices shift week to week as supply and listings change on the secondary market.

Where a card has several printings within Lorwyn Eclipsed, this list uses only the highest priced version to set its rank, with any other priced printings noted alongside it. A handful of cards on this list carry only a single printing with a price attached, which is stated plainly rather than left ambiguous. Where two printings of the same card land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over.

Rarity is mixed across the field, with mythics and rares sitting close together for much of the list, and no uncommons or commons appearing in this top twenty. The gap between a card's headline price and its cheaper printing varies a lot from entry to entry, and that spread is often more telling than the headline figure alone.

Prices below are quoted exactly as recorded at the time of writing. Readers looking to buy can check card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia, keeping in mind that any figure quoted here will drift over time.
