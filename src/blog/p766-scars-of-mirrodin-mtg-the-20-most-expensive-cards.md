Scars of Mirrodin arrived on 1 October 2010, opening the story of Mirrodin's corruption by the Phyrexian invasion and introducing infect, poison counters and a run of artifact-heavy mechanics that still get played today. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to number twenty.

Every card in Scars of Mirrodin has a single printing here, so the ranking is simple to follow: each entry's headline price is the price of its one and only version in this set. Where the data notes a tie between printings, that tie is called out rather than resolved arbitrarily, though in this particular set no card has more than one priced printing to tie in the first place.

Mox Opal sits well clear of the rest of the field at the top of the list, and the drop from first to second place is the widest gap of any two positions here. Prices below that point settle into a steadier decline, mythic and rare cards mixing together rather than sorting cleanly by rarity.

Prices shown are a snapshot and will shift over time as supply and buyer interest change. Anyone using this list to shop should treat the figures as a starting point rather than a fixed number.
