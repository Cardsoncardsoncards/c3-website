The Hobbit lands as Magic's latest venture into Middle-earth, and this list looks at the twenty cards from the set most played in Commander according to EDHREC rank, with a lower rank meaning a card turns up in more decks. The set has not released yet, so every price you see here is a preorder price, and preorder prices move around a fair bit before and after a set actually hits shelves. This is a ranking of play, not of price, so the order comes entirely from EDHREC's numbers rather than from anything to do with dollars.

Several of these cards have more than one printing within The Hobbit itself, which is common for a set built with showcase or bonus treatments. Where that happens, the card is ranked once, using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across all its printings, and priced using whichever printing carries the highest price. Where two printings happen to share the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly folded into the number, so you can see when a card's alternate versions are trading at parity.

Because everything below is a preorder figure, treat these prices as a snapshot rather than a settled market. Singles can be picked up through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and both venues will show movement as release day approaches and the set finds its actual supply. What follows is the countdown from twenty up to the single most played card in the set.

## Frequently asked questions

**When does The Hobbit release?**
The Hobbit is set to release on 2026-08-14. It has not released yet, so every price quoted in this list is a preorder price.

**Which card is the most played in this list?**
Wood Elves is the most played card, sitting at EDHREC rank 694, well ahead of every other card on the countdown.

**How does the ranking handle cards with multiple printings?**
Each card appears once on the list, ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across all its printings in the set. It is then priced using whichever of its printings carries the highest price.

**What happens when two printings of a card are priced exactly the same?**
That tie is noted rather than resolved silently. Gleaming Splendor is an example on this list, with two of its three priced printings sharing an identical figure.

**Are these prices final?**
No. These are preorder prices for a set that has not yet released, and prices move over time as supply and demand settle after launch.

**Does a higher price mean a card is more played?**
Not necessarily. Price and EDHREC rank are separate measures, and several cheaper cards on this list, such as Wood Elves and Settle the Wreckage, outrank far more expensive mythics like Smaug the Magnificent.
