Edge of Eternities is Magic: The Gathering's latest set, released on 1 August 2025, and this list tracks its 20 most expensive cards by Australian dollar price at time of writing. The set brought a mix of new mythics and returning staples, including dual lands that have been reprinted across dozens of Magic sets over the years, and both show up here alongside the set's own headline mythics.

Where a card has more than one printing within Edge of Eternities, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, with the other printing's price noted alongside it. Some cards in the set have printings that carry no price data at all, and those are excluded from the "carry a price" count without affecting the ranking. Where two printings land on the exact same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or another.

Every price below is a snapshot. Magic singles move constantly as supply, print runs and demand shift over time, so the numbers here reflect one point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy or sell can check current listings through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, where prices will naturally track the broader secondary market rather than sit still.
