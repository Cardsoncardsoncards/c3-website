Magic 2011 was released on the 16th of July, 2010, and it turned out to be one of the more influential core sets in Commander's history. It reintroduced or newly printed a run of mana fixing lands, cheap interaction and ramp pieces that ended up in decks for years afterwards, and that legacy shows up clearly in EDHREC's play data today. This list ranks the 20 most played Magic 2011 cards in Commander, ordered from number 1 down to number 20 by EDHREC rank, where a lower rank means the card sees more play.

Every card here appears once in the list even where a card had more than one printing within the set. Our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank a card achieves, and prices it using its highest priced printing. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than silently broken in either direction. As it happens, every card in this particular top 20 has just a single priced printing in Magic 2011, so there are no ties or printing spreads to untangle this time around.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure as accurate at the time it was pulled rather than a permanent number. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which will reflect more current pricing than what is printed here.
