Guilds of Ravnica landed on 5 October 2018, bringing back the ten guilds of Ravnica two at a time across its Standard-legal run, and along with them a batch of shock lands, guildgates and guild-themed spells that have since found long lives in Commander decks. This list takes those cards and ranks them by EDHREC rank, from the most played down to the least, across the top twenty.

Where a card has more than one printing within Guilds of Ravnica itself, the methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank for its position on the list, and the highest priced printing for its headline figure. Any other printings in the set are listed alongside it, and where two printings happen to share the same price exactly, that tie is noted rather than smoothed over. Most cards here have a single printing in the set, so this mostly comes into play with the guildgates.

All prices are in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot in time. Singles markets move, sometimes by a few cents, sometimes by more, so treat the figures here as a point of reference rather than something fixed. Readers can find current listings for these cards through our own card pages and through eBay Australia.

The list runs from rank 1, Watery Grave, the most played card from the set in Commander at EDHREC rank 51, down through the shock lands, spells and guildgates, to Murmuring Mystic at number 20.
