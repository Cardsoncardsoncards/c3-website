Urza's Saga is a 1998 expansion from Magic: The Gathering's earliest years, remembered as much for its artefact and land support as for the sheer density of playable cards packed into one small set. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower rank number means a card sees more play across decks. The order runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20.

Each card in this list appears once, even where Urza's Saga printed it more than once. Where a card has several printings in the set, the rank shown comes from its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and the headline price is drawn from whichever of those printings costs the most. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than resolved arbitrarily.

Every price below is a snapshot. Singles markets move week to week, and a figure quoted today can look quite different in a month, in either direction. Treat the prices here as a point of reference rather than a fixed number.

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Urza's Saga released?**
Urza's Saga released on 1998-10-12, making it one of the older sets covered on this site.

**What does this list actually measure?**
It ranks the twenty Urza's Saga cards most played in Commander decks, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means more play across tracked decklists.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears only once on this list. Where it has several printings within Urza's Saga, its rank is taken from the best (lowest) EDHREC rank among those printings, and its price is taken from whichever printing costs the most, with any exact price ties between printings noted rather than resolved silently.
