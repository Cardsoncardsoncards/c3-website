Ixalan sent Magic players hunting for dinosaurs, pirates, vampires and merfolk across a sun soaked archipelago, and it landed back on 2017-09-29. The set is best remembered for its treasure token mechanic and its run of double faced cards, several of which show up in the list below. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by current price in Australian dollars, from number 1 down to number 20.

Each card on this list appears once, even if it has more than one printing within Ixalan. Where a card has multiple printings, we rank it by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it turns out, none of the 20 cards here have more than a single priced printing in this set, so the ranking is a plain sort by that single number.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at time of writing. Singles markets move constantly, so a card sitting at a given spot today may drift up or down in the weeks ahead. Treat the figures below as a point in time reading rather than a fixed value.
