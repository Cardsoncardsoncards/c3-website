Guildpact was released on 2006-02-03, the middle set of the original Ravnica block, and it remains one of the more collected pieces of that era thanks to its guild-themed structure and its handful of enduringly playable rares. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current price in Australian dollars, counting down from number one, the priciest card, to number twenty. It is a snapshot of the secondary market as it stands, not a permanent ranking, and it exists to give collectors and players a clear read on where value currently sits within the set.

The methodology is simple but worth stating plainly. Each card appears exactly once on this list, even if it has multiple printings within Guildpact itself. Where a card does have more than one printing in the set, it is ranked according to whichever of those printings currently carries the higher price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out explicitly rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just a single priced printing in Guildpact, so there are no internal ties to report this time round, though the note above the price for each entry always states this clearly.

Prices themselves are pulled at a point in time and will drift, sometimes gently and sometimes not, as supply, condition and general market sentiment shift. A price you see today reading a certain figure may look different in a month, so treat the numbers here as a dated snapshot rather than a fixed fact. Singles can be sourced through our own card pages here on Cards on Cards on Cards, as well as through eBay Australia, and checking both is generally the sensible way to compare condition and postage before buying.

What follows is a rundown of all twenty cards, each with its rank, price and printing information listed directly above the commentary, so the write-up below focuses on how each card sits relative to its neighbours rather than repeating the raw numbers.
