Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty landed on 18 February 2022, mixing traditional Kamigawa flavour with a neon-lit future timeline, and it has since produced a steady supply of Commander staples in the form of channel lands, cycling duals and a handful of efficient rares. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks. It is a played list, not a value list, so a common land that sees heavy play can sit above a rare that most tables never touch.

Several of these cards have more than one printing within Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty itself, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other variants. Where that happens, the card is ranked once, using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across all its printings, and priced using whichever printing carries the highest price. Any other printings with a price are listed alongside it, and where two printings happen to share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than smoothed over.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly, so the figures here reflect a moment in time rather than a fixed value, and readers should expect movement up or down by the time they check a card page themselves.
