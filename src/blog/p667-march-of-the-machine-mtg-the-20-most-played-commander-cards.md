March of the Machine landed on 21 April 2023, closing out the Phyrexian invasion arc with a set that mixed battle-focused mechanics with a deep bench of value cards for Commander tables. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander decks, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower rank means the card shows up in more decklists.

Some of these cards have more than one printing within March of the Machine itself, whether through borderless treatments, extended art or other variants. Where that happens, the card is ranked once, using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. Any other printings with a price are listed alongside it, and where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly, so a figure that holds today can shift within weeks.
