Ravnica Allegiance released on 25 January 2019 as the second half of Magic's return to the plane of Ravnica, and it remains one of the most heavily played sets in Commander thanks to its guild-focused mechanics and a stacked cycle of shock lands. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set according to EDHREC rank, from number 1 down to number 20, with a lower rank meaning the card shows up in more decklists.

Where a card has multiple printings within Ravnica Allegiance itself, the ranking uses that card's best (lowest) EDHREC rank and prices it at the highest of its printings, so a card is never counted twice. Where two printings happen to share a price exactly, that tie is noted rather than smoothed over. Most cards on this list only ever appeared once in the set, so this mainly matters for the final entry.

All prices below are in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at time of writing. Singles prices move constantly with supply, demand and reprints elsewhere, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers can track down copies of any of these cards through the card pages on our own site or via eBay Australia.
