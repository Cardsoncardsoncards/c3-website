Champions of Kamigawa arrived on 2004-10-01, opening the block that took Magic's design team deep into a world built on Japanese folklore and spirit-versus-mortal conflict. Two decades on, the set still contributes a steady stream of cards to Commander decks, from mana-fixing staples to legendary creatures built for specific tribal or combo shells. This list ranks the twenty most played Champions of Kamigawa cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists.

Every card on this list appears once, even where a card had more than one printing within the set. Where multiple printings exist, the card is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than resolved arbitrarily. As it happens, every card here has a single priced printing within the set, so there is no splitting to untangle this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Singles markets move constantly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value.
