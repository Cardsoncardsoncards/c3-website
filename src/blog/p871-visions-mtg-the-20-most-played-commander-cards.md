Visions arrived back in February 1997 as one of the smaller expansions of Magic's early years, but its checklist has proven unusually durable in Commander decades on. This list ranks the twenty Visions cards most played in the format according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decklists relative to the rest of the pool. It is a measure of play frequency, not of price or of raw power.

Each card on this list appears once, even where Visions printed it more than once. Where a card has several printings in the set, we take its best (lowest) EDHREC rank to place it, and its highest priced printing to set the headline figure shown above each entry. Any case where two printings land on exactly the same price is noted rather than quietly smoothed over. For this particular set, every card here has just the one printing carrying a price, so the headline figures are simple.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent fact. Readers can check current listings and buy through the card pages on our own site, or through eBay Australia, where Visions singles change hands regularly.

The list below runs from number 1, the most played Visions card in Commander, down to number 20.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Visions release?**
Visions released on 3 February 1997.

**Which Visions card is the most played in Commander?**
Vampiric Tutor is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 111, well ahead of every other card in the set.

**How does the ranking handle cards with multiple printings in the set?**
Each card appears once on the list. Where a card has several printings within Visions, it is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. Any two printings sharing an identical price are noted rather than silently resolved.

**Do any of the cards on this list have multiple priced printings within Visions?**
No. Every card on this particular list has only one printing in the set carrying a price, so the headline figures are drawn from a single source each.

**Where can Australian collectors buy these singles?**

**Are the prices shown fixed?**
No. Every price in this post is a snapshot taken at a point in time, and singles markets move, so current listings may differ from the figures quoted here.

**What does EDHREC rank actually measure?**
It measures how often a card appears in Commander decklists relative to other cards, with a lower number indicating more frequent play. It is not a measure of price or power level.
