Pokemon TCG turns 30 this year, and The Pokemon Company International has officially confirmed its anniversary set, currently referred to as 30th Celebration, for a worldwide release on 16 September 2026. This is worth flagging early, because it is set to be the first ever global same-day launch in the Pokemon TCG's history.

## A genuine first for the franchise

Every prior Pokemon TCG release, including this year's own Mega Evolution era sets, has followed a staggered regional rollout in some form. The 30th Celebration set breaks that pattern entirely, launching in every participating market on the same day. For Australian players and collectors, that means no waiting behind other regions for stock to arrive, a meaningful change from how nearly every major Pokemon release has worked until now, and one worth remembering when comparing this launch to any previous set you have shopped for locally.

## What has actually been teased

Early teaser material has shown thirteen cards so far, including Pikachu, Arceus, Raikou and Palkia, widely expected to be reprints of iconic cards in new anniversary treatments, echoing how the game's 25th anniversary Celebrations set worked back in 2021. Additional teasers under the tagline The Future Awaits have hinted at new Mew and Mewtwo cards as well, though full details have not yet been confirmed by The Pokemon Company, and more official reveals are expected as the release date draws closer.

## Why this matters more than a typical anniversary set

Pokemon TCG originally released in Japan in October 1996, making 2026 the genuine 30 year mark rather than a rounded marketing anniversary. Anniversary sets in trading card games tend to draw collector interest well beyond a franchise's active player base, and the simultaneous global launch format is a strong signal of how significant The Pokemon Company considers this particular milestone. Expect coverage and speculation to ramp up considerably in the weeks leading into September as more official details are confirmed.

## Looking back at the 25th anniversary for a guide

The 2021 Celebrations set, released for Pokemon TCG's 25th anniversary, gives a useful reference point for what a milestone set like this can mean for the market. That set combined classic card reprints in fresh anniversary treatments with a run of special products, and demand for it extended well beyond players actively competing at the time, driven largely by collectors wanting a piece of a genuine milestone rather than a competitively relevant card. If the 30th Celebration set follows a similar pattern, expect interest here to behave more like a collector event than a typical set release.

## Why the simultaneous launch changes the usual pattern

Because every other major release this year, including the Mega Evolution era's main expansions, has followed some form of staggered regional rollout, a same-day global launch removes a dynamic that has shaped Pokemon TCG pricing for years. There will be no window where import copies from an earlier-releasing region trade at a premium while other markets wait, which means demand and pricing should reflect genuine, immediate global interest rather than an artificial early-access gap. For a milestone set specifically, that is likely to make launch week itself unusually significant.
