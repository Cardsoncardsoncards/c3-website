Fallen Empires arrived back on the 1st of November 1994, one of the earliest expansion sets Magic: The Gathering ever printed, and it remains a fixture of vintage collections thanks to its small print run and unusual mix of rarities. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, counting down from number one.

Some cards in Fallen Empires were printed more than once within the set itself, so where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked here by whichever printing carries the highest price. Any other printings and their prices are noted alongside the headline figure. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over.

Rainbow Vale sits at the top of the list at AU$12.45, well clear of the rest of the field. From there the list runs down through a mix of rares, uncommons and a couple of commons that have held value despite their lower rarity, ending at Soul Exchange to round out the top twenty.

Prices shown are a snapshot only. Singles prices for a set this old move around over time, so figures quoted here reflect the data at the time of writing rather than a fixed value.
