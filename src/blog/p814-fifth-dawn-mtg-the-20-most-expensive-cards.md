Fifth Dawn closed out the Mirrodin block when it released on 2004-06-04, bringing a run of artifact-driven cards that has stayed relevant to collectors long after the block itself wrapped up. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price, from the priciest single card down to the twentieth spot on the list.

Each card appears once on this list. Where a card has more than one printing within Fifth Dawn, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the higher price, and any case where two printings land on the exact same figure is noted rather than quietly smoothed over. As it happens, every card in this particular top twenty has just a single priced printing in the set, so there are no internal printing comparisons to make this time around.

Sitting at the top is Mycosynth Golem at AU$67.60, the clear leader of the pack. From there the list runs down through a mix of rares and uncommons, with the gap between neighbouring spots varying quite a bit depending on where you look.

Keep in mind that all of these figures are a snapshot. Card prices shift over time with market conditions, so treat the numbers here as accurate as of when this list was compiled rather than fixed forever.
