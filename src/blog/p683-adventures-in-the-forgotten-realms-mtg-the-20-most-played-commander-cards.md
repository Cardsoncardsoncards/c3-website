Adventures in the Forgotten Realms brought Dungeons & Dragons flavour to Magic: The Gathering when it released on 23 July 2021, and it left behind a Commander card pool that still gets played today. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set, using EDHREC rank as the measure, with number 1 being the card that shows up in the most decks and number 20 the least played of this particular top twenty. It is worth remembering that a lower EDHREC rank means a card is more played, so Evolving Wilds sitting at rank 19 puts it ahead of everything else here despite its low price.

Several cards in this set were printed more than once, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other variants. Where that happens, the card is ranked once using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of its printings costs the most. Any other printings are listed alongside it for comparison, and where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than glossed over.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and were accurate at time of writing, but singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure as a snapshot rather than a guarantee. Readers in Australia can check current listings on our own card pages or through eBay Australia if they want to compare against what is shown here.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Adventures in the Forgotten Realms release?**
The set released on 23 July 2021, bringing a Dungeons & Dragons themed card pool to Magic: The Gathering.

**Which card is the most played from this set in Commander?**
Evolving Wilds is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 19, well ahead of every other card on this list.

**How does the methodology handle cards with more than one printing in the set?**
Each card is ranked once using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across its printings, and priced using whichever printing is most expensive. Other printings are listed for comparison, and identical prices between two printings are noted as a tie rather than silently resolved.

**Does a lower or higher EDHREC rank mean a card is more played?**
A lower EDHREC rank means a card appears in more decks, which is why Evolving Wilds at rank 19 tops this list despite ranking below many other cards numerically.
