Marvel Super Heroes landed on 2026-06-26, folding a heavy dose of capes and cosmic cubes into Magic's card pool. This list looks at the twenty cards from that set with the most Commander play, using EDHREC rank as the measuring stick, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decks. It is a play list, not a price list, though price and rarity are noted alongside for anyone tracking either.

Some cards in this set were printed more than once, in different treatments or supplemental products, so the methodology folds those together. Each card appears exactly once on the list, ranked by whichever of its printings has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever printing costs the most. Where two printings land on the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars, correct at the time of writing and quoted to the cent from the data we pulled. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so treat the figures as a point of reference rather than a guarantee of what you will pay today. For actual purchases, singles are available through our own card pages and through eBay Australia, and both are worth checking before buying.

Thirst for Knowledge sits at the top of the list, the single most played Marvel Super Heroes card in Commander by this measure. From there the list runs down through mythic legends and equipment, commons and uncommons, showing how played rate and price do not always move together.
