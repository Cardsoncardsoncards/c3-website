Shards of Alara arrived on 2008-10-03, splitting its five colours across five distinct shard worlds and giving Magic one of its most visually striking blocks. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current price in Australian dollars, from the single priciest card down to the twentieth. It is a snapshot of the secondary market rather than a fixed record, and every figure here should be read as a moment in time rather than a permanent value.

For the methodology, each card appears once on this list, even if it has more than one printing within Shards of Alara. Where a card does carry multiple printings, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or another. As it happens, every card in this particular top twenty has just a single priced printing in the set, so there are no internal ties to untangle this time round, but the rule stands for any future update to this list.

Prices move. Singles markets shift with reprints, tournament relevance, and simple supply over time, so what appears here is accurate to when this post was written and not a promise for next month or next year. Readers in Australia can pick up singles through the card pages on this site, and through eBay Australia, and comparing listings across both is a reasonable way to see how the numbers here sit against the wider market.

The list itself runs from Tezzeret the Seeker at the top through to Knight of the White Orchid rounding out the twentieth spot, taking in a spread of mythics, rares and one common along the way. Rarity does not track price cleanly here, and that gap between a card's rarity and its market position is worth watching as you work down the list.
