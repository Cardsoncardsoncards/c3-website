Cyberpunk TCG is the biggest new trading card game arriving this year, and the numbers behind it are not subtle. WeirdCo raised USD 28,353,088 from 50,773 backers on Kickstarter, making it the most funded trading card game campaign in the platform's history. It is licensed directly from CD PROJEKT RED and built around the world of Cyberpunk 2077 and Cyberpunk: Edgerunners.

If you are in Australia and trying to work out when you can actually buy it, the answer is more complicated than a single date. There are three separate waves, and only one of them has started.

## The three waves, in order

**Wave 1, Kickstarter fulfilment, from early September 2026.** WeirdCo began shipping Welcome to Night City product to backers around 1 September, with a two week delivery window to allow for regional shipping variation. If you backed the campaign, your cards are either in transit or close to it. Late pledges have closed, so this window is shut to anyone who missed the campaign.

**Wave 2, in-store Beta events, 10 to 20 September 2026.** These run exclusively at stores that registered for Cyberpunk TCG Organised Play before 1 May. They use Beta booster packs rather than retail packs, and carry their own promo cards. Format is sealed deck: open your kit, build 30 cards plus 3 Legends, and play.

**Wave 3, retail.** Retail pre-release events are scheduled for the weekend of 30 October, using retail boosters rather than Beta ones. The retail set itself, Welcome to Night City, is listed by most retailers for 6 November 2026. Be careful with this date. Some coverage published in June quoted 1 October instead, and WeirdCo has not restated it publicly since. Treat 6 November as the working date and expect it to move.

## What is actually in the product

Retail configuration, as listed by stockists:

1. Booster box: 24 packs per box, 12 cards per pack.
2. Sealed case: 6 booster boxes.
3. Starter decks: two of them, The Heist and Embracing Power. Each contains a playable 40 card deck, 3 Legends and a full set of six dice.

The Heist is built around V, Jackie Welles and Viktor Vektor. Embracing Power runs Saburo Arasaka, Yorinobu Arasaka and Goro Takemura.

Rarity structure matters if you are buying to hold. Iconic Rares are the main chase cards inside retail boosters, using alternate artwork built for collectors. Nova Rares sit outside the retail booster rarity list entirely. Every Nova Rare revealed so far has been a promotional variant distributed through Kickstarter rewards, conventions or exclusive partnerships, including Nova versions of Lucy, Rebecca and Adam Smasher. That distinction is the whole ballgame for anyone thinking about long term value: retail chases get printed again, promo distribution does not.

## How the game actually plays

Cyberpunk TCG abandons the health pool most card games are built on. You are the leader of a crew, competing against a Rival to become a Night City Legend. You need six dice (d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20) alongside your deck.

The win condition is control of Gig dice. You take a die from your fixer area, roll it, and add it to your Gig area. Hold the majority for a full turn and you win. You get there by playing Units into the field, equipping them with Gear, and deploying Programs at the right moment. Cards can also be sold for Eddies, one per turn, and a sold card is worth 1 Eddie regardless of what it costs in hand.

Rising Street Cred unlocks more powerful tools as the game goes on, and it does the same for your opponent. It is a tempo and denial game rather than a damage race, which is a genuinely different shape from Magic, Pokemon or One Piece.

## Australian pricing and availability

No Australian RRP has been confirmed by WeirdCo or any Australian distributor at time of writing. Local stores taking pre-orders are pricing independently, and several of the widely circulated listings are from overseas retailers who may or may not ship to Australia at a sensible freight cost.

Two practical points for Australian buyers:

1. **Check the ship date, not the release date.** A pre-order listing showing November is a shipping estimate, not a guarantee, and several stockists explicitly state that contents and dates can change without notice.
2. **Marketplace stock will be thin until November.** TCGplayer only opened the category on 31 August 2026, and sellers are permitted to list ahead of launch. Most listings you find right now are Kickstarter product or pre-orders, not stock sitting on a shelf.

## Frequently asked questions

**When does Cyberpunk TCG release in Australia?**
Retailers are listing the first set, Welcome to Night City, for 6 November 2026. Retail pre-release events are scheduled for the weekend of 30 October. Some earlier coverage listed 1 October, so the date is not fully settled.

**Can I still back the Kickstarter?**
No. The campaign has ended and late pledges have closed. The only routes now are retail pre-orders or the secondary market.

**What is the difference between a Beta event and a pre-release?**
Both are sealed deck events at a local store. Beta events run 10 to 20 September using Beta booster packs, and are limited to stores that signed up for Organised Play before 1 May. Pre-releases run on the weekend of 30 October using retail boosters. Each carries different promo cards.

**Are Kickstarter exclusive cards worth more than retail chases?**
They are structurally scarcer. Nova Rares sit outside the retail booster rarity list and have only appeared through Kickstarter rewards, conventions and partnerships. Iconic Rares come out of retail boosters, so supply grows with every box opened. Neither has established Australian sold pricing yet.

**Do I need anything besides cards to play?**
Yes. You need a set of six dice: d4, d6, d8, d10, d12 and d20. Both starter decks include a full set.
