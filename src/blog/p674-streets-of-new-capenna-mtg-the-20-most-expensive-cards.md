Streets of New Capenna arrived on 29 April 2022, dropping players into a plane of towering art deco skylines and five crime family factions. It gave the game a fresh cycle of triome lands, a run of angels and demons in the New Capenna colour identities, and the sort of top end mythic slate that tends to hold collector attention long after a set's initial print run has moved through shops. This list runs down the 20 most expensive cards from that set, priced in Australian dollars.

The ranking works from a single figure per card: where a card has more than one printing in the set, we use whichever printing carries the highest price and rank on that number alone. The other printings aren't ignored, though. They're listed alongside the headline figure so you can see the full spread for that card, and where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over.

Triomes dominate the top of the list, which isn't a surprise given how many of them exist across multiple printings with real gaps between the cheapest and priciest version. Further down, the spread narrows and some cards settle with only a small difference between their printings, or with only one printing carrying a listed price at all.

Keep in mind that every figure here is a snapshot. Card prices move constantly with supply, so treat this list as a picture of where things stood at time of writing rather than a fixed ranking.
