Oath of the Gatewatch launched on 22 January 2016 as a smaller companion to Battle for Zendikar, and despite that lighter card count it has left a lasting mark on Commander tables through a handful of efficient spells and Zendikar-flavoured permanents. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. The order runs from number 1, the most played card, down to number 20, the least played of this particular group.

Every card here has a single printing within Oath of the Gatewatch, so there is no need to weigh one version against another this time. Where a set does carry several printings for a card, our method takes the lowest EDHREC rank to decide placement and the highest price among those printings for the headline figure, noting explicitly on the page whenever two printings land on exactly the same price rather than quietly picking one.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at time of writing. Singles markets move constantly, so treat every figure as a point of reference rather than a fixed number, and check current listings before buying.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Oath of the Gatewatch release?**
Oath of the Gatewatch released on 22 January 2016, as a follow-up set to Battle for Zendikar.

**Which card from Oath of the Gatewatch is the most played in Commander?**
Negate is the most played card from the set according to EDHREC rank, sitting at rank 55 with a price of AU$0.30.

**How does this list handle cards with more than one printing in the set?**
Cards with several printings are ranked using their lowest, meaning best, EDHREC rank, and priced using the highest price among those printings. Where two printings share exactly the same price, that tie is noted on the page rather than resolved silently.

**Where can Australian readers buy these singles?**

**Are the prices listed fixed?**
No, every price shown is a snapshot taken at time of writing. Card markets move constantly, so current listings should be checked before buying.

**Does a card's rarity determine its price on this list?**
Not always. Several rares and mythics here are priced below commons or uncommons that rank higher for play, showing that rarity and price do not move together in lockstep.

**What does EDHREC rank actually measure?**
EDHREC rank reflects how often a card appears in Commander decklists submitted to EDHREC, with a lower number indicating more frequent inclusion.
