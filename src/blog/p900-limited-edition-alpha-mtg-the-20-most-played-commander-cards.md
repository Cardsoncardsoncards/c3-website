Limited Edition Alpha is where Magic: The Gathering began, released on 1993-08-05, and it remains the set every subsequent Magic product traces back to. This list ranks the twenty Alpha cards most played in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decklists. It is a strange collision of eras: cards designed decades before the format existed, now filtered through what modern Commander players actually build with.

Every card here appears once on the list even if it has multiple printings within Alpha itself. Where a card has more than one printing in the set, the rank shown is its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, and the price shown is the highest priced of those printings. Any case where two printings land on exactly the same price is noted as a tie rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Prices are listed in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Alpha is thirty years old, low in print quantity, and its market moves according to condition, grading and general collector interest, so the figures here will shift over time. Treat every price in this piece as a moment captured, not a promise of what a card will cost tomorrow or what it cost yesterday.

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Limited Edition Alpha released?**
Limited Edition Alpha released on 1993-08-05, making it the first Magic: The Gathering set ever printed.

**Which card is the most played Alpha card in Commander?**
Counterspell is the most played, holding the top EDHREC rank of 16 among all Alpha cards on this list.

**How does the ranking handle cards with multiple printings in the set?**
Each card appears once. Its rank is taken from its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across its printings in the set, and its price is taken from its highest priced printing, with any exact price ties between printings noted rather than silently resolved.

**Where can Australian collectors buy these singles?**
Readers can buy singles through the card pages on Cards on Cards on Cards, as well as through eBay Australia.

**Are the prices in this list fixed?**
No, every price is a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at a point in time. Alpha card prices move with the market, so figures shown here will change.

**What is EDHREC rank measuring?**
EDHREC rank reflects how often a card is played in Commander decks, with a lower number meaning the card appears more frequently across recorded decklists.

**Why do some low-ranked cards cost less than higher-ranked cards on this list?**
Play frequency and price are measured independently. A card's rarity within Alpha and its overall collector market can push its price well above or below what its EDHREC rank alone would suggest.
