Rivals of Ixalan is the smaller follow-up set to Ixalan, released on 2018-01-19, and it carries forward the block's dinosaurs, pirates, vampires and merfolk into a tighter card pool. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from that set by their current price in Australian dollars, running from number 1 down to number 20 as stated.

Each card on this list appears only once, even if it has more than one printing within Rivals of Ixalan. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and any other printings are noted alongside it. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

As it happens, every card in this particular top 20 has just a single priced printing in the set, so there are no internal ties or secondary printings to weigh up here. That's worth keeping in mind when comparing this list to sets with more varied print runs.

Prices shown are a snapshot in Australian dollars and will shift over time with the market, so treat the figures as a point-in-time reading rather than a fixed value. Singles can be bought through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia."
