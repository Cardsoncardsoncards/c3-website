Odyssey arrived on 1 October 2001, the second set in the block that carried its name and a card pool remembered for pushing graveyard strategies into the mainstream of constructed play. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, drawing on singles pricing as it stands today rather than at any point in the set's history.

Every card here has exactly one printing within Odyssey itself, so this is a simple headline-price ranking rather than an exercise in reconciling multiple versions. Where a future update introduces a card with more than one printing in the set, the methodology carries a card by its highest priced printing, and any exact price tie between printings is noted rather than resolved arbitrarily.

Tainted Pact sits at the top of the list, and from there the prices step down fairly evenly rather than dropping in one sharp cliff. Keep in mind that singles prices shift with the market, so the figures below are a snapshot rather than a fixed record, and the gap between any two cards on this list can narrow or widen over time.
