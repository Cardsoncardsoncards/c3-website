Summer Magic, also catalogued as Edgar, is one of the strangest footnotes in Magic's early history. Printed and then largely pulled from release in June 1994 over card frame and rarity errors, it exists in far smaller numbers than a typical set from that era, which shapes almost everything about the price list below. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from number one down to number twenty.

Where a card has several printings inside the set, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and any other printings are noted rather than folded into the total. When two cards land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out plainly rather than being broken by some arbitrary secondary factor. A handful of basic lands sit high on this list precisely because of that rule, their scarcity as Summer Magic prints outweighing what would normally be a nominal value.

Every figure here is a snapshot. Prices for a set this old and this limited can shift as supply and grading population changes, so treat the numbers as a point in time rather than a fixed reference.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Summer Magic / Edgar release?**
The set released on 21 June 1994, and it has remained available to collectors ever since despite its unusual and limited print run.

**Which card is the most expensive in the set?**
Ornithopter is the most expensive card in Summer Magic / Edgar, priced at AU$715.00, well ahead of the basic land trio ranked just below it.

**How does the ranking handle cards with multiple printings?**
Each card appears once on the list, ranked by whichever of its printings carries the highest price. Any other printings and their prices are noted alongside the headline figure rather than being averaged or added in.

**How are tied prices handled?**
When two or more printings share exactly the same price, the tie is stated plainly rather than being broken by an arbitrary tiebreaker. This list includes ties among the basic lands near the top and among Fireball, Fog and Rock Hydra in the middle.

**Are these prices fixed?**

**Why do some basic lands rank above rares and uncommons?**
