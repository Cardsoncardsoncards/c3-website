Dominaria arrived on 27 April 2018, bringing plane-hopping nostalgia, legendary creatures for every colour, and a story steeped in the history of the game's oldest setting. It remains one of the more fondly discussed sets from that era, and its secondary market still throws up a wide spread of prices across mythics, rares, uncommons and even a couple of commons. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from Dominaria by their current Australian dollar price, from the priciest single card down to the twentieth.

Each card on this list appears only once, even if it has more than one printing within the set. Where a card has multiple printings, the highest priced version is what determines its position on the list, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card here has a single priced printing, so no such tie appears in this particular list, but the methodology stands for any future update.

Prices are not fixed. They shift with market activity, so treat every figure below as a snapshot rather than a permanent value. Readers looking to buy or sell can check singles through the card pages on our own site, or compare against listings on eBay Australia.
