Invasion arrived on the 2nd of October, 2000, one of the sets that helped define the block era of Magic: The Gathering, and it remains a source of Commander staples two decades on. This list ranks the twenty most played Invasion cards in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure. A lower EDHREC rank means a card is more played, so the countdown below runs from number 1, the single most played card in the set, down to number 20.

Every card here appears once, even where Invasion printed it more than once. When a card has multiple printings in the set, the ranking uses its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and the price shown is the highest priced of those printings. Where two printings land on the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or another. As it happens, none of these twenty cards have more than a single priced printing in this set, so the headline figures below stand alone.

Prices are listed in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles pricing on cards this old moves with supply, demand and general market drift, so treat every figure here as accurate to the moment it was recorded rather than a permanent number. For current stock and pricing, readers can check the card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia.
