Morningtide is the small, tribal-themed Lorwyn follow-up set that released on the 1st of February, 2008. It never had the tournament spotlight some other sets enjoyed, but a handful of its cards have found long-term homes in Commander decks, which is what this list tracks. We have pulled the 20 Morningtide cards with the most play in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists.

Every card here appears only once on the list. For the handful of Morningtide cards that have more than one printing within the set itself, we use the lowest (best) EDHREC rank to place the card, and the highest priced of its printings for the headline figure quoted above each entry. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

As it happens, every card in this particular top 20 has only a single priced printing within Morningtide, so the printing count sits at one throughout. That keeps the numbers simple this time round, though the methodology stands regardless.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move day to day, so treat every figure below as accurate at the time of writing rather than a fixed number.
