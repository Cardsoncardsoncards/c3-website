Zendikar Rising returned to the plane of Zendikar in September 2020, bringing back the modal double-faced land cards first seen in the block's earlier visits and pairing them with a fresh set of creatures and spells built around landfall and party mechanics. This post ranks the twenty most played cards from that set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure of play, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decks.

Several cards in Zendikar Rising received more than one printing within the set itself, whether through borderless treatments, promotional versions or other variant frames. Where that happens, the card is ranked once using its best, that is lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of its printings carries the highest price. Any other printings are listed separately alongside it, and where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly folded into one number.

The list runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20. Play rank and market price are two different things entirely, and this list makes no claim about which cards are worth owning, only about how often they appear in decks and what the data shows for pricing across the set's printings.

Prices quoted throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets shift constantly, so treat every figure here as accurate at the time of writing rather than a permanent value.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Zendikar Rising release?**
Zendikar Rising released on 25 September 2020, and it has been available for purchase since that date.

**Which card is the most played card from Zendikar Rising in Commander?**
Negate is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 55, the lowest (and therefore best) rank of any card in this set.

**How does this list handle cards with more than one printing in the set?**
Each card appears once, ranked by its best, that is lowest, EDHREC rank across all its printings, and priced using its highest priced printing. Any other printing prices are listed alongside it.

**What happens when two printings of the same card share the exact same price?**
That tie is noted explicitly rather than resolved silently, so readers can see when two versions of a card are priced identically.

**Where can I buy these singles in Australia?**

**Are the prices in this post fixed?**
No. Prices are a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at the time of writing, and singles markets move over time, so figures will drift from what is shown here.

**Does a card's play rank tell you anything about its price?**
Not directly. Play rank measures how often a card appears in decks according to EDHREC, while price reflects the singles market, and the two do not move in lockstep across this list.
