The Brothers' War landed on 18 November 2022, sending players back to the Argive and Phyrexian sides of the Weatherlight saga's deep prehistory, with a card frame full of Powerstones, prototype spells and a cast of Urza, Mishra and their allies. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to the twentieth. It is a snapshot of the secondary market rather than a claim about what any card does at the table.

Where a card has more than one printing in the set, the ranking uses its highest priced printing to decide placement, and the other printing's price is noted alongside it rather than folded in or ignored. If two printings happen to land on the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. Cards with only a single priced printing in the set are marked as such, since there is nothing to compare.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are exact to the cent at the time this list was put together. Card markets move, sometimes within days, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value.
