Outlaws of Thunder Junction is the desert heist set released on 19 April 2024, and this list rounds up the 20 cards from that set currently carrying the highest price tags in Australian dollars. Every card appears once, and where a card has more than one printing in the set, we rank it by whichever printing costs the most. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than glossed over.

The prices below are a snapshot rather than a fixed record. Singles markets shift week to week as supply and demand change, so the figures quoted here, both the headline price and any other printing mentioned, reflect the data pulled at time of writing. Every price quoted is exact to the cent.

Every card on this list has two priced printings in the set, which makes the gap between those two printings a useful thing to watch. In some cases that gap is small, in others the highest printing is worth several times the other. Read on for the full order, from the most expensive card in the set down to the twentieth.
