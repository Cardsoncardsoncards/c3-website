Darksteel is the second set in the Mirrodin block, released back on the 6th of February 2004. It leaned hard into the block's artifact theme, and that legacy still shows up in the prices its cards command today. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from the most costly down to the twentieth.

Every card here has a single tracked printing in Darksteel, so there are no internal ties to note and no secondary printings to weigh against a headline figure. Where a set has multiple printings of the same card, our methodology takes the highest priced version as the ranking figure and flags any exact price ties between printings rather than quietly picking a winner, but that situation simply doesn't arise across this particular twenty.

Prices are shown in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a little and sometimes by a lot, so a price checked today may differ from one checked in a month's time. Keep that in mind as you read through the rankings below, and treat the figures as a point in time rather than a fixed value.
