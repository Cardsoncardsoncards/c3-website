Dragon's Maze rounded out the Return to Ravnica block back in 2013, closing out the ten guild story with a small but memorable set. It gave Commander players a mix of guild-specific tools, a full cycle of Guildgates, and a handful of rares and mythics that still see play in decks built around two-colour identities. This list ranks the 20 most played Dragon's Maze cards in Commander today, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks.

Every card here appears once on the list even if it had more than one printing within the set. Where a card carries several printings, we use its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank for placement, and its highest priced printing for the headline figure. Any other printings are noted alongside, and where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over. In this particular list every card happens to have just a single priced printing in the set, so there is no tie-breaking to do this time around.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot in time. Card prices move, sometimes a fair bit, so treat the figures here as a point of reference rather than a permanent fact. Singles can be bought through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking current listings before assuming the numbers below still hold.
