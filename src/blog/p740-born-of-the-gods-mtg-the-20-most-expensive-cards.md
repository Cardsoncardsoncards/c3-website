Born of the Gods is the second set in the Theros block, released on 7 February 2014, and it leans hard on the block's gods and their devotion mechanics. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, drawing on our own card page data. It's a single point in time rather than a permanent ranking, since singles prices shift with supply and demand long after a set stops being printed.

Where a card has more than one printing within Born of the Gods, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the higher price, and any printings that share an identical price are noted as a tie rather than arbitrarily split. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just one priced printing in the set, so there's no tie-breaking or printing comparison to walk through this time around, just a straight ordering from the top price down.

The five gods of the set, Phenax, Xenagos, Ephara, Karametra and Mogis, dominate the upper half of the list, all sitting at mythic rarity, before the list moves into a run of uncommons and rares that make up the Archetype cycle and a scattering of other rares and mythics. The gap between the top of the list and the bottom is wide, and it narrows steadily rather than dropping off sharply anywhere in particular.
