Onslaught arrived on 7 October 2002, kicking off the block of the same name and introducing tribal mechanics that would echo through Magic design for years afterwards. It is also home to one of the most valuable fetchland cycles ever printed, and that cycle dominates the top of this list. This post ranks the 20 most expensive Onslaught cards by Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20.

Each card on this list appears only once, even where a card had more than one printing in the set. In those cases the card is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card in this particular list has just a single priced printing in Onslaught, so there is no tie-breaking to report here, only a straight run of individual prices.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. Readers looking to buy can check card pages on our own site or search eBay Australia for current listings.
