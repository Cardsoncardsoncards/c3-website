Fallen Empires released on 1994-11-01, one of the earliest expansion sets ever printed for Magic: The Gathering, and it still turns up in Commander decks today in surprising numbers. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card sees more play across logged Commander decklists. It is a play-frequency ranking, not a price ranking, so the order below has nothing to do with what any of these cards cost.

Several Fallen Empires cards were printed more than once within the set itself, so the methodology needs a rule for that. Each card appears once on this list, keyed to its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever of its printings carries the highest price. Where two printings of the same card land on the exact same price, that tie is noted plainly above the entry rather than being broken arbitrarily.

Every price quoted here is a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at the time of writing. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so treat the figures as a point of reference rather than a guarantee of what you will pay when you go looking for a copy. Singles can be sourced through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and the numbers above each entry are the ones to check first before buying.

The list runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20. Rarity, printing counts and price gaps are all given directly above each blurb, so the write-up below each card focuses on what that data implies rather than restating it.
