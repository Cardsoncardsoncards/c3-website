Murders at Karlov Manor arrived on 2024-02-09, bringing Ravnica's guild politics back into focus with a murder-mystery twist. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, from the most played card at number 1 down to the twentieth most played entry. A lower EDHREC rank means a card shows up in more decks, so the top of this list is where the format's actual habits are visible.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Murders at Karlov Manor, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other alternate versions. Where that happens, the card is ranked once, using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across all of its printings, and priced using whichever printing costs the most. Any other printings and their prices are listed separately above each entry, and where two printings happen to share an identical price, that coincidence is called out rather than smoothed over.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on Cards on Cards on Cards, or through eBay Australia.
