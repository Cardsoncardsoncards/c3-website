Ninth Edition is one of the old core sets, released back on 2005-07-29, and it still sits in a lot of collections as the last few printings before the core set format changed shape. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, running from number 1 down to number 20 as stated once here and not repeated through the list.

Where a card has more than one printing inside Ninth Edition, we take its highest priced printing as the figure that ranks it. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved, though as it happens every card on this particular list only has one printing carrying a price, with the second printing untracked. That keeps the maths simple this time round, but the rule stands for any set where it matters.

Biorhythm sits at the top of the list, and from there the prices step down fairly steadily rather than in one or two big jumps, though the gap between the top handful of cards and the rest of the field is still sizeable. Rarity is mixed in among the twenty, mostly rares with a single common breaking up the pattern partway down.

Keep in mind these are snapshot prices. Magic singles move with the market, so what is listed here reflects prices at time of writing, not a fixed value. Readers looking to buy or check current pricing can do so through the card pages on our own site, or through eBay Australia.
