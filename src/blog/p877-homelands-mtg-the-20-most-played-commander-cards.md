Homelands landed on the 1st of October 1995, one of the more obscure entries in Magic's early history, yet its cards still turn up in Commander decks today. This post ranks the twenty most played cards from the set as measured by EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card sees more play across the decks that EDHREC tracks. The list runs from the single most played card down to the twentieth, and every entry below is placed strictly by that rank rather than by price.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Homelands itself, so a note on method is worth stating plainly. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, it is ranked once, by whichever printing carries the lowest EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever printing carries the highest price. Where two printings happen to share the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. This means the headline price you see for a card is not always attached to the same printing that earned its rank, and the gap between the headline price and any other printing's price can tell you something about how the market treats the different versions.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars, sourced at a single point in time, and they will move. A price quoted here today may already be out of date by the time you read this, so treat every figure as a snapshot rather than a guarantee. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which will reflect current pricing rather than what is printed below.

With that groundwork laid, here is how the twenty most played cards from Homelands stack up against one another in Commander.
