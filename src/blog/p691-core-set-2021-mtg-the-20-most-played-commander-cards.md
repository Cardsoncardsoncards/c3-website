Core Set 2021 landed back on 2020-07-03, and even years later it keeps a foothold in Commander decks thanks to a run of efficient ramp spells, cycling dual lands and a handful of creatures that slot into almost any deck. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. Cultivate sits at the top of the pile, and from there the list works its way down through counterspell fodder, scry lands and a few pricier mythics.

Several cards in this set were printed more than once within Core Set 2021 itself, most obviously the five scry temples, so the methodology treats each card as a single entry. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked by its best EDHREC placement and priced at its highest priced printing, with the other printing listed alongside it. When two printings land on the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a permanent figure. Singles markets shift with supply, demand and reprints, so treat every number here as accurate to the moment it was recorded rather than a fixed value. You can shop these cards through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking both before settling on a copy.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Core Set 2021 release?**
Core Set 2021 released on 2020-07-03, and it has been available for purchase since that date.

**Which card is the most played in Commander from this set?**
Cultivate is the most played card from Core Set 2021 in Commander, sitting at EDHREC rank 20, ahead of every other card in the set.

**How does the ranking handle cards with more than one printing?**
Each card appears once on the list even if it has several printings in the set. It is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing, with any other printing listed alongside it.

**What happens when two printings of the same card share an identical price?**
When that happens, the tie is noted explicitly rather than being broken silently or hidden by rounding.

**Where can I buy these singles in Australia?**

**Are the prices listed fixed?**
No, every price is a snapshot taken at one point in time. Singles prices move with market conditions, so figures will drift after this list is published.

**Does EDHREC rank measure price or popularity?**
EDHREC rank measures how often a card appears in Commander decklists, not its price. A lower rank means the card is used more, and price is tracked separately alongside it.
