Hour of Devastation rounded out the Amonkhet block back in July 2017, and despite being the smaller of the two Egyptian-themed sets, it has left a surprisingly durable footprint on the Commander format. This list ranks the twenty most played Hour of Devastation cards in EDHREC's data, ordered from the single most included card down to the twentieth, using EDHREC rank as the measure of play rather than price or rarity. A lower rank number means more decks are running that card, so the card sitting at rank 144 is played far more often than the card sitting at rank 3715.

Every card here has only one printing within Hour of Devastation itself, so the methodology note about tie-breaking and multiple printings is mostly a formality for this particular set. Where a card does happen to have more than one priced printing, the rule is to rank by the lowest (best) EDHREC rank available across those printings, and to price by whichever printing carries the highest price tag. Any printings that land on exactly the same price are called out as a tie rather than arbitrarily separated, though as it happens every entry below carries just the one price.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a permanent fact. Singles markets shift with reprints, tournament results and general demand, so treat every figure here as accurate to the moment this list was compiled rather than a fixed number. Readers looking to buy into any of these can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking both before settling on a copy.

The spread across this list is wide. At one end sits a common removal spell played in enough decks to sit at rank 144, and at the other end sits a boardwipe with a rank past 3700, a reminder that "played in Commander" covers a huge range of actual play frequency once you get outside the top handful of cards.

**When did Hour of Devastation release?**
Hour of Devastation released on 14 July 2017 as part of the Amonkhet block.

**Which card from Hour of Devastation is the most played in Commander?**
Abrade is the most played card from the set, sitting at EDHREC rank 144 and priced at AU$0.36.

**How is this list ranked?**
Cards are ordered by EDHREC rank, with a lower rank meaning the card appears in more Commander decks, from number one down to number twenty.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
A card with several printings in Hour of Devastation is ranked by whichever printing has the lowest, or best, EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever printing has the highest price. Where two printings share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than resolved arbitrarily.
