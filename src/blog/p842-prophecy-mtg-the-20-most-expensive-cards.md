Prophecy is a small expansion released on 2000-06-05, the final set of the Rath Cycle era before Invasion arrived and changed the shape of Magic's card pool. It is a compact set, but it carries a handful of cards that still command attention on the singles market two decades on. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from Prophecy by current Australian dollar price, from number one down to number twenty.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card has more than one printing within Prophecy. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked according to whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out directly rather than quietly resolved. In this particular set, every card on the list happens to have just a single priced printing, so there are no internal ties to untangle this time around.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes by a few cents and sometimes by more, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. Readers looking to buy into Prophecy can find singles through the card pages on our own site as well as through eBay Australia.
