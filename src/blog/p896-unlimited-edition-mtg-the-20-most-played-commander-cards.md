Unlimited Edition is one of the foundational Magic: The Gathering sets, released on the 1st of December 1993 as the second print run of the game's original card pool. It has no set symbol, but it holds a permanent place in Commander circles because so many of its cards remain legal and played today. This list ranks the 20 most played Unlimited Edition cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card shows up in more decks.

Each card on this list appears once, even if a card was printed more than once within Unlimited Edition. Where that happens, the card is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and priced using the highest priced printing among them. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved in one direction.

As it happens, every card on this particular list has only a single tracked printing with a price attached, so there are no internal ties to report this time around. That is a feature of this specific set and list, not a rule that will hold everywhere.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Card prices move over time with supply, condition and market activity, so treat every figure here as accurate at the time of writing rather than fixed.
