Limited Edition Alpha is where Magic: The Gathering began. Released on 1993-08-05, it was the very first print run of the game, distinguished from its immediate successor Beta by rounded corners and a smaller overall card pool. That founding status is a big part of why so many of its cards carry the prices they do today.

This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from Alpha by current price in Australian dollars, from the single priciest card down to the twentieth. Each card appears only once. Where a card has more than one priced printing in the set, its highest priced printing sets its position on the list, and any printings that share an identical price are noted as a tie rather than arbitrarily separated.

As it happens, every card on this particular list has exactly one printing in the set, so there are no internal ties or multi-printing gaps to work through here. That is a feature of Alpha itself: it was a single, unified print run with no separate collector or foil variants to muddy the picture. What you see is a straight ranking of one printing per card.

Prices are snapshots. Magic singles move constantly with market conditions, so the figures below reflect a point in time rather than a fixed value. Treat the ordering and the relative gaps as the useful takeaway, not a promise that these exact numbers will hold.
