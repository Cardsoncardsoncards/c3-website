Strixhaven: School of Mages arrived on 23 April 2021, bringing five magical colleges, a run of cycle lands, and a batch of instants and sorceries that found their way into Commander decks almost immediately. This list ranks the twenty most played Strixhaven cards in that format using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decks. Storm-Kiln Artist sits at the top of the pile.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Strixhaven itself, most obviously the five "Snarl" cycle lands, so the methodology treats each card as a single entry. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, its rank comes from the best (lowest) EDHREC figure across those printings, and its listed price is the highest of them. Any other printings are noted separately, and if two printings happen to land on the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot in time. Singles markets move week to week, so treat every figure here as a point of reference rather than a fixed number. You can find current listings for these cards through our own card pages or on eBay Australia.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Strixhaven: School of Mages release?**
The set released on 23 April 2021, and this list covers cards from that set as played in Commander since then.

**Which card is the most played Strixhaven card in Commander?**
Storm-Kiln Artist is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 205, well ahead of the rest of the list.

**How does the ranking handle cards with multiple printings in the set?**
Each card is listed once. Its rank is taken from the best (lowest) EDHREC figure across its printings in the set, and its listed price is the highest of those printings, with any other printing price noted alongside it.

**What happens if two printings of a card share the same price?**
The methodology notes that tie explicitly rather than resolving it silently, so readers can see when two printings happen to land on the same figure.

**Where can I buy these singles in Australia?**

**Are the prices in this list fixed?**
No, the figures are a snapshot in Australian dollars at the time of writing, and singles prices move over time as the market shifts.

**Does EDHREC rank measure price or popularity?**
It measures how often a card appears in Commander decks tracked by EDHREC, a lower number meaning more decks, and it is not a measure of price at all.
