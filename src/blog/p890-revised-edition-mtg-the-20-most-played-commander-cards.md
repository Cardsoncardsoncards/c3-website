Revised Edition landed in April 1994 as one of the earliest reprint sets in Magic's history, and it remains a foundational point of reference for older Commander decks. This list ranks the twenty most played Revised cards in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks. Because EDHREC tracks a card by name rather than by individual printing, any card with several printings within Revised Edition is credited with its best (lowest) rank across those printings, and priced using whichever printing carries the highest price. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Every card below appears once, even if it happened to be printed more than once within this set. The rank tells you how often a card turns up in Commander decks relative to the rest of Magic's card pool, not how it performed within Revised alone, so a card at rank 60 is more broadly played than one at rank 438, regardless of what either costs. Prices are shown in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Card markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number.

The list runs from the single most played card in the set down to the twentieth, and the gap between neighbours on the list is often far more informative than any one price on its own. Some of the cheapest cards here sit closer to the top of the rankings than several of the most expensive ones, a reminder that play frequency and price follow their own separate logic.

Revised Edition released on 11 April 1994, making it one of the earliest sets in Magic's history.

**What does this ranking measure?**
It orders the twenty most played Revised Edition cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower rank means the card appears in more decks.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears only once on the list. If it has several printings within Revised Edition, it is ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and priced using its highest priced printing, with any exact price tie between printings noted rather than resolved silently.
