Secrets of Strixhaven landed on the 24th of April, 2026, and this list takes a first proper look at how its cards are actually being used at Commander tables, rather than how they were priced on release day. The ranking runs on EDHREC rank, from the most played card in the set down to the twentieth, which means it is a measure of play frequency, not a measure of value. A low EDHREC rank simply means more decks on EDHREC include that card.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Secrets of Strixhaven itself, whether as a bonus treatment, a showcase frame or some other variant. Where that happens, the card is only listed once, ranked by whichever printing has the better (lower) EDHREC standing, and priced using its highest priced printing. Any other printings and their prices are noted separately above each entry, and where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Rarity ranges right across the list, from commons sitting near the very top all the way to mythics further down, which is a reminder that play rate and rarity are not the same axis. A card being common and cheap does not stop it from being run in a huge number of decks, and a mythic further down the list is not necessarily rarer in practice, just less frequently slotted in.

Every price below is a snapshot only. Prices in this article are in Australian dollars and they move over time with supply, demand and the broader market, so treat the figures as a point-in-time reading rather than a permanent fact. Singles can be picked up through the card pages on our own site, or sourced through eBay Australia if you are shopping around.
