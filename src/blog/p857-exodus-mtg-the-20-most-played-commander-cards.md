Exodus arrived on the 15th of June 1998, the third and final set in the Ice Age block, and it remains one of the more heavily mined sets for Commander staples despite its age. This list ranks the twenty most played Exodus cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play across decks. It is a play-frequency ranking, not a price ranking, so the order will not always match the value of the cards involved.

Every card here has a single printing within Exodus, so there was no need to pick between multiple versions or note a shared price between them. In sets where a card does carry more than one printing, our method takes the best EDHREC rank of the card overall and the highest price among its Exodus printings, flagging any case where two printings land on the same price rather than quietly picking one. Here, each entry is clean: one printing, one price, one rank.

The cards range from commons and uncommons that see wide but low value play, up to rares that carry prices well into the hundreds of dollars. Soul Warden sits at the top of the list, at EDHREC rank 506, priced at AU$4.65. Prices below are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, and figures quoted here will drift as the market does, whether readers are buying through our own card pages or through eBay Australia.
