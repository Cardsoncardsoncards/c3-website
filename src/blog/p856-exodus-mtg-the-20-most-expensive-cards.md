Exodus is one of the older Magic: The Gathering sets, released back on 15 June 1998 as the third and final expansion in the Weatherlight block. It's a small set by modern standards, but it has produced a handful of cards that have held real value in the decades since, largely thanks to their history in Vintage and Legacy play and their scarcity as older rares. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from Exodus by their current Australian dollar price.

Every card here appears only once on the list, even though some Magic cards see multiple printings within a single set (different foil treatments, borders or promotional versions, for example). Where a card has more than one priced printing in Exodus, it's ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and any other priced printings are noted alongside it. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

As it happens, every card in this particular list has just one printing carrying a price in Exodus, so the methodology note about multiple printings and ties is more a statement of how we approach the full data set than something you'll see play out below. City of Traitors sits alone at the top, and the list runs down from there to Penance at number twenty.

Keep in mind that these are snapshot prices. Magic singles move around constantly based on supply, demand and the broader market, so the figures below reflect a point in time rather than a fixed value. For current pricing and to buy singles, readers can check the card pages here on Cards on Cards on Cards, or search listings on eBay Australia.
