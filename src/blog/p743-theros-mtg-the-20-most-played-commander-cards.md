Theros arrived on the 27th of September 2013, and a decade on it still supplies a surprising number of cards that turn up in Commander decks every day. This list ranks the twenty most played Theros cards using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card is seen more often across the decks EDHREC tracks. It is a measure of play frequency, not a measure of price, and the two things do not always move together.

Every card in this set has just a single printing carrying a price, so there are no ties to note this time around and no gap between a cheap version and a pricier alternate frame. Where a set does throw up multiple printings, our methodology takes the lowest EDHREC rank as the card's position and the highest priced printing as its headline figure, flagging any exact tie rather than quietly picking a winner. That is not needed here, but it explains why the number above each entry is described the way it is.

What follows is ordered from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20. Swan Song sits at the top by a wide margin in rank terms, while the bottom of the list is far more tightly packed. Keep in mind that all prices are a snapshot in Australian dollars and will shift over time, so treat the figures as a point of reference rather than a fixed number.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Theros release?**
Theros released on the 27th of September 2013.

**How is this list ranked?**
Cards are ordered by EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card appears in more Commander decks tracked by EDHREC. This is a play frequency measure, not a price measure.

**How are multiple printings and ties handled?**
A card that has several printings within the set is ranked using its lowest (best) EDHREC rank and priced using its highest priced printing. If two printings happen to share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently, though every card on this particular list has only one priced printing.

**Which card is the most played Theros card in Commander?**
Swan Song is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 70 and priced at AU$20.12.

**Where can I buy these singles in Australia?**

**Do these prices change over time?**
Yes. All prices quoted here are a snapshot in Australian dollars and will move as the market changes, so treat them as a point in time rather than a fixed figure.

**Why are Commander staples like the scry lands ranked so closely together?**
The Theros scry lands (the various Temple cards) fill similar roles across many decks, so EDHREC tracks them at broadly similar play rates, which is why several of them cluster tightly in the middle of this list.
