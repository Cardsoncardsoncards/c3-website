Urza's Destiny closed out the original Urza block when it released on 1999-06-07, following Urza's Saga and Urza's Legacy onto the tournament scene. It is a small set by modern standards, but it carries a handful of cards that have held their value for a long time, and this list ranks the twenty most expensive of them by current Australian dollar price.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card had more than one printing within Urza's Destiny. Where a card does carry multiple priced printings, the ranking uses the highest priced version, and any exact tie between printings is noted plainly rather than resolved by an arbitrary tiebreak. As it happens, every card below has just one priced printing in this set, so there are no such ties to report here.

Replenish sits at the top of the list, some way clear of everything else. From there the list runs down through rares and one uncommon, ending with cards priced in single digits. Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only, as singles markets move over time, so treat every figure here as accurate as of when it was recorded rather than fixed forever.
