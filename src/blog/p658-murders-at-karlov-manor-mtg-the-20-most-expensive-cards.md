Murders at Karlov Manor landed on 9 February 2024, taking the Ravnica murder-mystery premise and running it through ten guilds' worth of detectives, culprits and clue tokens. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price, drawing on our own site listings and the wider secondary market.

Because so many Murders at Karlov Manor cards were printed more than once, most obviously the surveil lands that appear in both regular and showcase versions, each card here counts once. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked by whichever printing costs the most, and the other printing prices are noted alongside it rather than folded into an average. Any printings that share an identical price are flagged as a tie instead of being silently separated.

The gap between a card's most and least expensive printing varies a lot across this list. Some cards, like the surveil dual lands near the top, show a fairly modest spread between versions, while others further down have one printing worth many times the other. Read those printing counts and prices as they are given above each entry, since that's where the exact figures live.

All prices are a snapshot and will shift as the market moves, sometimes within weeks of a set's release and sometimes years later. Use this list as a guide to relative standing within Murders at Karlov Manor rather than a fixed reference.
