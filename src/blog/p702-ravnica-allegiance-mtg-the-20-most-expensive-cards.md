Ravnica Allegiance landed on the 25th of January 2019, the second half of the Guilds of Ravnica story and a set that leaned hard into its ten guild identities. It gave Magic players a run of shockland reprints, a fresh crop of guild leaders, and a handful of cards that have held value well beyond the standard rotation that once defined them. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from Smothering Tithe at the top down to Biogenic Ooze at the foot of the table.

Every card here is counted once. Where a card has more than one printing within Ravnica Allegiance, it is ranked by whichever of those printings carries the higher price, and any case where two printings land on the same figure is called out rather than quietly folded together. As it happens, every card in this particular set of 20 has only a single priced printing, so there is no internal competition to resolve this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly with supply, demand and reprint news, so treat every figure below as accurate for the moment it was recorded rather than a fixed number. Readers can check current listings and buy singles through our own card pages or through eBay Australia.
