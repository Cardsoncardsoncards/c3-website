Urza's Legacy is a small, dense set from February 1999, sitting inside the Urza block that gave Commander players a surprising number of long-serving staples for a set of its size. This list runs through the twenty most played cards from that set in Commander today, drawing on EDHREC rank as the measure of play, with rank 1 being the most played card and the numbers climbing from there.

Each card on this list appears once, even where Urza's Legacy printed it more than once. Where a card has several printings in the set, it is ranked using its single best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of its printings carries the highest price. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly smoothed over.

As it happens, every card here has just one priced printing in this set, so the methodology note above matters more for how the list was built than for anything you will see repeated in the entries. What you will see is the spread between a common like Cloud of Faeries at the cheap end and a rare like Grim Monolith sitting well above everything else on the list.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Card markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as accurate to when it was recorded rather than a permanent number.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Urza's Legacy release?**
Urza's Legacy released on 15 February 1999, as part of the Urza block.

**What does this list measure?**
It ranks the twenty most played Urza's Legacy cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower rank number means the card sees more play.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears once on the list. If a card has several printings in Urza's Legacy, it is ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and priced using its highest priced printing, and any exact price tie between printings is noted rather than resolved silently.

**Which card is the most played card from the set in Commander?**
Frantic Search is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 102, well ahead of the rest of the list.

**Are these prices fixed?**
No. Every price here is a snapshot taken at one point in time, and card prices move afterwards, sometimes substantially.

**Does a higher price mean a card is more played?**
Not necessarily. Grim Monolith is priced far above every other card on this list but is not the most played, and cheaper cards like Frantic Search and Cloud of Faeries outrank several more expensive rares.
