Avatar: The Last Airbender is Wizards of the Coast's Magic: The Gathering crossover set, released on 21 November 2025 and built around the beloved animated series. This post ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in the Commander format, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower rank number means a card sees more play at the kitchen table.

Some cards in this set were printed more than once. Where that happens, the card is ranked by whichever printing has the better (lower) EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever printing carries the higher price tag. The other printing's price is listed alongside it for comparison, and where two printings happen to share an identical price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over.

Every price below is quoted in Australian dollars and reflects a snapshot at time of writing. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so treat these figures as a point of reference rather than a permanent fixture. Singles can be sourced through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and it's worth checking both before buying.

The list runs from number 1, the most played card in the set according to EDHREC, down to number 20. Read on for how each card stacks up against its neighbours, and what the spread between printings might tell you.
