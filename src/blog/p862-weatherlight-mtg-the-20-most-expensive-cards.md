Weatherlight is the Magic: The Gathering set released on the 9th of June 1997, the fourth instalment in the original Tempest block story and a set fondly remembered for its flavourful legendary creatures and the ship itself. This post ranks the twenty most expensive Weatherlight cards by current Australian dollar price, from Null Rod at the top down to Well of Knowledge at the foot of the list.

Every card here appears exactly once. Where a card has more than one printing within Weatherlight, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings happen to land on the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, none of the twenty cards below have more than a single priced printing in this set, so the list is a clean run of headline figures with no ties to note.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and they are a snapshot, not a fixed value. Singles markets shift with supply, so a figure recorded today may look different in a few months. Readers can find these cards through the card pages on our own site, or by checking listings on eBay Australia.
