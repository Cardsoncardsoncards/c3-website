Magic 2012 was the third of Wizards of the Coast's core sets built to welcome new players while still giving established tables something to build around, and it landed back on 2011-07-15. Years on, its dual lands, mana creatures and efficient utility spells still turn up in Commander decks across the country, and this list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play. It is a play frequency list, not a value list, so a cheap common can easily sit above an expensive rare if more decks are running it.

Every card in this set happens to have a single printing with a listed price, so there are no ties to note and no secondary printings to weigh up here, though our methodology always ranks by best EDHREC rank first and prices by the highest priced printing when a card does have more than one. Where two printings do share a price exactly, we say so rather than quietly picking a winner. That approach keeps the ranking consistent even when a future set post has to deal with a card printed three or four times.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Card markets move week to week, sometimes day to day, so treat every figure here as accurate at the time of writing rather than a permanent number. For current pricing and to buy singles, our own card pages are the direct route, and eBay Australia is worth checking as a secondary market for the same cards.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Magic 2012 release?**
Magic 2012 released on 2011-07-15, and it has been available for singles purchase ever since.

**Which card is the most played from Magic 2012 in Commander?**
Swiftfoot Boots is the most played card from the set, sitting at EDHREC rank 12, the lowest rank number and therefore the highest play frequency on this list.

**How is this list ordered?**
The list runs from number 1, the most played card, down to number 20, using EDHREC rank where a lower number means more decks run that card.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
A card with several printings in the set is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. Where two printings share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently, though no card on this particular list needed that treatment.

**Are the prices in this post fixed?**

**Does a card's rarity determine its price on this list?**
Not consistently. Several rares and even the one mythic, Sun Titan, are priced lower than some commons and uncommons on this list, so rarity alone does not predict price here.
