Foundations released on 15 November 2024 and quickly established itself as a set with staying power in the singles market, blending new mythics with reprints of long-running staples. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, drawing on data captured across every printing available within Foundations itself.

Where a card has more than one printing in the set, only the highest priced version determines its rank, though the prices of its other printings are listed alongside it for comparison. On the rare occasion two printings share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than resolved arbitrarily. Not every printing carries a tracked price at the time of writing, which is why the printing counts and the priced counts don't always match.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly, and a card's position on this list can shift within weeks as supply and listings change. Readers looking to buy or compare current prices can do so through the card pages on our own site or via eBay Australia.
