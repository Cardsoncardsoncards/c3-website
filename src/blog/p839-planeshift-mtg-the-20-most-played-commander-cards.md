Planeshift is the small third set of the Invasion block, released on 2001-02-05, and it remains a curious pocket of Magic history: a short print run, a wedge of gold cards tied to the block's multicolour theme, and a handful of cards that have quietly found homes in Commander decks two decades on. This list ranks the twenty most played Planeshift cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks. Number one on this list is the most played card from the set full stop, and the numbers climb from there down to twentieth place.

Each card here appears exactly once, even where Planeshift printed it more than once. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, the rank shown is its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and the price shown is its highest priced printing. Any printing that shares a price exactly with another is noted as a tie rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just one priced printing in Planeshift, so that methodology note is mostly there for completeness.

Prices are given in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Magic singles move, sometimes quickly, and a price recorded today will drift with supply, demand and the broader market. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and should expect the figures below to be a reference point rather than a fixed number.
