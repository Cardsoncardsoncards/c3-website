Fate Reforged released on 2015-01-23, the smaller companion set to Khans of Tarkur, and it has spent the years since quietly earning a spot in Commander decks through its dual lands, dragons and a handful of enduring staples. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play across the format's decks. It is a play frequency list, not a price list, though we show pricing alongside it because the two rarely move together.

Where a card has more than one printing within Fate Reforged, our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank for its position on the list, and prices it at whichever printing carries the higher tag. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. Only Crux of Fate on this list has a second printing worth noting, and even then only one of its printings carries a listed price.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value, particularly for anything nearer the top or bottom of what any given seller has in stock.
