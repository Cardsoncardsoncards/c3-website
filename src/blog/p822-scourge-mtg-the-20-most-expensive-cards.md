Scourge is the third and final set in the Onslaught block, released on 26 May 2003. It's a small set by modern standards, but it left behind a handful of cards that still carry weight on the singles market two decades on. This list ranks the twenty most expensive Scourge cards by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to the twentieth spot.

Each card on this list appears once, ranked by its highest priced printing. Where a card has more than one printing in the set, that spread is noted above the blurb rather than folded quietly into a single number. As it happens, every card here has just one priced printing in Scourge, so there's no gap to measure between versions this time around, though ties in price are called out where they occur elsewhere in this kind of list.

Sliver Overlord sits at the top at AU$60.63, the single most expensive card to come out of this set. From there the list runs down through a mix of rares and uncommons, with the gap between neighbouring cards narrowing considerably by the time you reach the bottom half of the twenty.

Keep in mind that these are snapshot prices. Magic singles move with supply, reprints, and format relevance, so the figures here reflect one point in time, not a fixed value. Readers looking to buy or sell can check listings on our own card pages or browse eBay Australia for comparison.
