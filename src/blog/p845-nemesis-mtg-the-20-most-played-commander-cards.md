Nemesis arrived on 2000-02-14 as the second set in the Masques block, a compact release that has nonetheless left a durable mark on Commander tables two decades on. This list ranks the twenty most played Nemesis cards in the format according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decklists. Every card here has just a single printing within the set, so there is no best-of-several-printings juggling to explain, though the methodology still applies: where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked by its best EDHREC placement and priced by its highest priced printing, with shared prices between printings noted rather than quietly rounded away.

The order runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20. Skyshroud Claim sits at the top, and from there the list moves through a mix of rares, uncommons and commons whose Commander presence has little to do with their rarity on the printed sheet. Prices shown are a snapshot pulled at time of writing and quoted in Australian dollars; singles markets shift constantly, so treat every figure as a point in time rather than a fixed value.

Readers looking to pick any of these up can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, where Nemesis stock varies in condition and availability. What follows is a short note on each card, focused on where it sits relative to its neighbours on the list rather than on what it does at the table.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Nemesis release?**
Nemesis released on 2000-02-14 as part of the Masques block.

**Which card is the most played Nemesis card in Commander?**
Skyshroud Claim is the most played, holding an EDHREC rank of 293, the lowest (and therefore best) rank of any card in the set.

**How does the methodology handle cards with multiple printings in the set?**
A card with several printings in Nemesis is ranked by its best EDHREC rank and priced by its highest priced printing. Where two printings share exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently.

**Do any cards on this list have multiple printings within Nemesis?**
No, each of the twenty cards here has a single priced printing in the set, so no tie-breaking or multi-printing pricing came into play for this particular list.

**Are the prices listed fixed?**

**Does a lower EDHREC rank number mean a card is played more or less?**
A lower EDHREC rank means a card is played more often. Skyshroud Claim's rank of 293 is the lowest, and therefore the most played, on this list.
