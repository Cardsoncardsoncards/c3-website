New Phyrexia arrived on 13 May 2011, closing out the Scars of Mirrodin block with a mechanical, mono coloured hybrid mana theme and a full run of Praetors leading the mythic slot. It is a small set by modern standards, but it carries a heavy load of cards that have stayed relevant to cube, Commander and constructed decks for well over a decade, which is reflected in the spread of prices below.

This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards in New Phyrexia by their price in Australian dollars, from number 1 down to number 20. Every card here happens to have a single priced printing in the set, so there are no split rankings to untangle and no ties to note this time around, though the methodology would treat a shared price as a tie rather than force one card above the other.

Prices are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly, and a figure quoted today can shift within weeks, so treat every number below as a point in time rather than a fixed value.
