Shadowmoor arrived on 2 May 2008, a set built around hybrid mana and a shift into a darker, corrupted version of the Lorwyn plane it followed. Long after its limited-edition life ended, it kept a foothold in Commander through a run of mana-fixing lands and a handful of build-around enchantments, and that is what this list tracks. The ranking below orders the twenty most played Shadowmoor cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decks.

Some cards in Shadowmoor were printed more than once within the set. Where that happens, the card is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and priced using whichever printing carries the highest price. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. None of the twenty cards below happened to need that tie-breaker, but the rule stood ready regardless.

Every price quoted here is a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at one point in time. Card prices move, sometimes within weeks, so treat the figures as a reference point rather than a fixed number. Singles can be sourced through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and checking current listings before buying is always the safer approach.

The list runs from number 1, Reflecting Pool, the single most played Shadowmoor card in Commander at EDHREC rank 173, down to number 20. Rarity ranges across rare, uncommon and common, and the EDHREC ranks stretch from 173 at the top to 3118 at the foot of the list, giving a sense of how steeply play frequency falls away even within one set's most-used cards.
