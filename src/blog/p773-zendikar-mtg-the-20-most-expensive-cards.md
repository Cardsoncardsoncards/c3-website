Zendikar arrived on 2 October 2009, bringing with it a wave of fetch lands and plane-themed rares that have gone on to become fixtures in Constructed decks for well over a decade. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current price in Australian dollars, from the top spot down to number 20. It is a single snapshot of the singles market, not a permanent ledger.

Each card on this list appears only once. Where a card has more than one printing within Zendikar, the entry uses the highest priced version to determine its rank, and if two printings of the same card are priced identically, that tie is noted plainly rather than resolved by an arbitrary tiebreaker. Every figure quoted here is exact, to the cent, because rounded numbers would misrepresent the data.

Prices for older sets like Zendikar move constantly with supply, reprints, and general market drift, so what you see here reflects one point in time. The list draws entirely on the price and printing data supplied for each card, nothing about mechanics, deck relevance, or player demand has been factored in.
