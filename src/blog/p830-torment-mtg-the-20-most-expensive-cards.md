Torment is the second set in the original Odyssey block, released on the 4th of February 2002. It's remembered as one of the most black-heavy sets Magic has ever printed, and that flavour shows up throughout this list, though a handful of green and red cards still muscle their way into the top twenty. This post ranks the twenty priciest cards from the set, by current Australian dollar price on our tracked listings.

Every card here is counted once. Where a card has more than one printing within Torment, we take its highest priced printing as the ranking figure, and if two printings land on the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, none of the cards in this particular list have more than a single priced printing in the set, so the numbers you see are each card's only recorded price here.

Prices are snapshots taken at time of writing and they move, sometimes by a fair margin, as stock and demand shift over time. Treat the ordering below as a picture of where things sit right now rather than a permanent ranking.
