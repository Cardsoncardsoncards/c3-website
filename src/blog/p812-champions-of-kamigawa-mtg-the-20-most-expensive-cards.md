Champions of Kamigawa was the first set of Magic: The Gathering's Kamigawa block, released on 1 October 2004. Built around a legend-and-spirit-heavy vision of Japanese myth, it introduced mechanics like Bushido and Ninjutsu and gave the game a run of legendary creatures and utility lands that have stayed on collectors' shelves ever since. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price.

The methodology is simple. Every card is counted once, and where a card has more than one printing in this set, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price. If two printings happened to share the exact same price, that tie would be noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other, though as it turns out every card here has only a single priced printing in the set.

Minamo, School at Water's Edge sits at the top of the list at AU$87.30, with the field spreading out below it down to Gifts Ungiven at number 20. Keep in mind that singles prices are not fixed. They move with the market, so treat the figures below as a snapshot rather than a permanent record, and check current listings before buying.
