Lorwyn Eclipsed released on 23 January 2026, and Commander players wasted no time putting its cards into their decks. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set according to EDHREC rank, counting down from number 1, the most played card, to number 20. Lower EDHREC rank means a card sees more play, so the card at the top of this list is the one showing up in the most decks.

Several cards in Lorwyn Eclipsed have more than one printing within the set itself. Where that happens, the card is ranked by whichever printing has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever printing carries the highest price. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or another.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars. Singles markets move day to day, and a figure that holds true this week may look different in a month, so treat these numbers as a point in time rather than a permanent fixture.
