Unlimited Edition is the second printing of Magic: The Gathering, released on 1 December 1993, and it remains one of the earliest points of entry for players and collectors chasing the game's original card pool. Unlike the print-damaged first edition, Unlimited cleaned up the black border issues of Alpha and Beta while keeping the same card list, which is part of why it still commands serious attention on the secondary market three decades later. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by Australian dollar price, from Timetwister at the top down to Shivan Dragon at the foot of the table.

Every card here has a single tracked printing within Unlimited Edition, so the ranking is a plain sort by headline price rather than a comparison across variants. Where the data notes more than one printing for a card, the methodology takes the highest priced version as the headline figure, and any exact price ties between printings are called out rather than resolved arbitrarily. No such ties or multiple printings appear in this particular twenty, so each entry below stands on one number alone.

Prices are shown in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets for cards this old move constantly, and figures quoted here will drift with time, so treat the list as a picture of relative standing rather than a fixed reference. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which carry current listings for Unlimited Edition singles.
