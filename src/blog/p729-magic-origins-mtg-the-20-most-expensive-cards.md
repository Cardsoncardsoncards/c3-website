Magic Origins closed out the "core set" era of Magic: The Gathering when it released on 2015-07-17, and it remains one of the more fondly discussed core sets thanks to its run of five planeswalker origin stories. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, running from the single priciest card at number 1 down to number 20. It is a snapshot of the market for singles, not a verdict on any card's playability or long term value.

Where a card has more than one printing within Magic Origins, the methodology takes its highest priced printing as the figure used for ranking. If two different printings of the same card land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out explicitly rather than quietly resolved one way or the other, so nothing is hidden by the ranking process. As it happens, every card on this particular list has only a single priced printing within the set, so there are no ties or multi-printing comparisons to untangle here, each entry stands on one number alone.

Prices in this list are all in Australian dollars and are pulled from current listings. Card markets move, sometimes by a little, sometimes by a lot, and a figure that is accurate today can look quite different in a few months depending on supply, reprints elsewhere, and general shifts in demand across the wider card market. Treat every number below as a point in time rather than a fixed fact.

Readers in Australia can shop for these singles through the card pages on our own site, and through eBay Australia, where a good spread of Magic Origins listings tends to be available. Both venues are worth checking against each other since listed prices for the same card can vary between sellers and platforms.
