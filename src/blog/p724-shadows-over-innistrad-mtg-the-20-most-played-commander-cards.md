Shadows over Innistrad returned to the plane's gothic horror roots when it released on 8 April 2016, bringing double-faced cards, a strong graveyard theme and a run of cycles (the "Painland" duals among them) that have since found homes in Commander decks well beyond their original Standard environment. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. It is a measure of play frequency, not of price or power level in isolation.

Every card here appears once, even where a card had more than one printing within the set. Where several printings existed, the card is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. Any case where two printings share the exact same price is called out explicitly rather than quietly folded into one number. In this particular set, none of the twenty cards below had more than one priced printing to compare, so the pricing is simple: one card, one figure.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week and sometimes day to day, so treat every figure here as accurate at the time of writing rather than a permanent value. Readers can check current listings and buy through the card pages on our own site, or compare against listings on eBay Australia.
