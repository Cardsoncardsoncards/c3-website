Planeshift is the third and final set in the Invasion block, released on 5 February 2001. It is a smaller expansion than the sets that came before it in the block, but it has produced a handful of cards that have held collector attention for well over two decades, driven by their roles in constructed and casual formats across that time. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from Planeshift by current Australian dollar price, counting down from the priciest at number one to the cheapest at number twenty.

Where a card has more than one printing within Planeshift itself, we take the highest priced of those printings to decide its rank on this list, and we say plainly in the entry how many printings exist and how many of those actually carry a tracked price. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, we note that tie rather than quietly picking a winner. In this particular list several cards do have a second printing in the set, but only one printing per card carries a price we can point to, so the headline figure and the card's rank are one and the same in every case here.

Every price below is a snapshot taken at the time of writing. Magic singles move constantly with supply, demand and reprints elsewhere, so a figure that is accurate today may look quite different in a few months, in either direction. Treat this as a point in time rather than a permanent ranking.

Readers looking to buy any of these singles in Australia can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, where Planeshift stock continues to circulate among sellers in varying condition and quantity.
