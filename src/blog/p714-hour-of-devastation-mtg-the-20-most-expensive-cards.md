Hour of Devastation closed out the Amonkhet block when it released on 14 July 2017, bringing the story of Amonkhet's fall to a close with a set full of gods, sphinxes and desert horrors. It is a set remembered for its mythic rare cycle of Egyptian-flavoured gods and for planeswalkers that still see play in Commander decks today, which is part of why some of its cards still command a noticeable price years after release. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from Hour of Devastation by their current Australian dollar price, from the priciest card down to the twentieth.

The methodology is simple but worth stating plainly. Every card appears once on this list, even if it has more than one printing in the set. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved in one direction. As it happens, every card in this particular top twenty has just a single priced printing in the set, so there are no internal printing comparisons to make this time around, only the ranking itself.

One thing to keep in mind throughout is that these figures are a snapshot. Card prices in Australian dollars move with the market, with card condition, with supply from reprints elsewhere, and with ordinary demand fluctuations, so a price recorded today will not be identical to one recorded in a few months. Treat the numbers below as a point in time rather than a fixed fact.
