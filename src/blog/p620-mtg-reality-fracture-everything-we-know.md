Magic: The Gathering | Reality Fracture releases worldwide on 2 October 2026, closing out the three year Metronome storyline that has run from Wilds of Eldraine through Tarkir: Dragonstorm. Prerelease events run 25 September to 1 October, with the set landing on MTG Arena on 29 September.

## The Echoverse, explained

Reality Fracture sends players into the Echoverse, an alternate reality created by planeswalker Jace Beleren as his answer to years of Omenpath chaos across the Multiverse. Rather than an invading army, the threat this time is Jace himself, now recast as The Theorist on his own villain card, a mythic planeswalker whose in-story title has replaced his name on the frame. The set leans on mirrored pairs throughout, showing familiar legends alongside twisted Echoverse versions of themselves, including the original five Lorwyn planeswalkers shown both as their true selves and as warped reflections.

## The headliner: Bloodline Recollector

The confirmed centrepiece is Bloodline Recollector, a serialized double rainbow foil card in a throwback full art frame, illustrated by Power Nine artist Mark Poole, with only 500 numbered copies available exclusively in Collector Boosters. It's a warped mirror of Secrets of Strixhaven's own headliner, fitting the set's whole mirrored pair concept. Expect this to be the single card collectors are chasing hardest once packs open.

## Hexhaven and the new mechanics

The set introduces Hexhaven, a dark inversion of Strixhaven built around five allied color colleges: Fatehold (white blue), Theorix (blue black), Stingerquill (black red), Konstrari (red green) and Vigorbloom (green white). Wizards has confirmed every booster contains an echoed pair, a two sided character mechanic baked directly into how packs are collated, alongside the return of Exhaust, first seen in an earlier set.

## Six Commander decks, no repeats

Reality Fracture ships with six confirmed Commander products. Multiverse Reforged is a four color (white blue black red) 100 card deck led by a new version of Jace Beleren with 16 new cards. Alongside it sit five mono color Foundations Commander decks: Calling All Angels (white), Keen Engineering (blue), Wretched Ranks (black, name to be confirmed as spoilers continue), plus red and green counterparts still being previewed. That's a genuinely large Commander product spread for a single release.

## Legality and product structure

The main Reality Fracture set (FRA) is legal in every format, including Standard. Reality Fracture Commander (FRC) and the Special Guests (SPG) cards are legal in Commander, Legacy and Vintage. Play Boosters draw from both FRA and SPG, and those cards are fully playable in Reality Fracture Draft and Sealed.
