Oath of the Gatewatch is the small set that followed Battle for Zendikar, released on 22 January 2016 and built around the same Eldrazi and Zendikar themes. It's a compact set by design, which means the spread between its most and least expensive chase cards is narrower than in a full-size expansion, but the ranking below still shows plenty of separation once every card is lined up by price.

This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20. Where a card has more than one printing in the set, it's ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and any other printings are listed alongside it. If two printings happen to land on the exact same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or another.

Most cards here have a single printing carrying a price, so the ranking is a simple ladder from Kozilek, the Great Distortion at the top down to Ayli, Eternal Pilgrim at the foot. The one exception is Wastes, which has four priced printings in the set and is ranked here by the highest of the four.

Keep in mind that all prices below are a snapshot. Magic singles prices move constantly with supply, demand and time since release, so the figures quoted here reflect a moment rather than a fixed value.
