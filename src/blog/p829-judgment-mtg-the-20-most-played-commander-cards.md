Judgment is a small set from the Odyssey block, released on the 27th of May, 2002. It never carried a large card pool, but a surprising number of its uncommons and rares still see play in Commander two decades on, which makes it a decent case study in how age and format relevance interact with price. This list ranks the 20 most played cards from the set, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks.

Every card here has a single printing in Judgment, so there are no tie breaks to note this time round. Where a set does have several printings of the same card, our methodology takes the best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings and prices the card at whichever printing is highest, flagging any exact price ties rather than resolving them silently. That rule sits ready in the background here even though this particular list never had to call on it.

The order below runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20. Anger sits at the top with an EDHREC rank of 351, well clear of everything else on the list, and the ranks spread out from there down past the 7,000 mark by the twentieth entry. Keep in mind that all prices are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number.

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Judgment released?**
Judgment released on the 27th of May, 2002, as part of the Odyssey block.

**Which card is the most played from Judgment in Commander?**
Anger is the most played card from the set, sitting at EDHREC rank 351, well ahead of every other card on this list.

**How does the ranking handle cards with multiple printings in the set?**
When a card has several printings within Judgment, its rank is taken from the best (lowest) EDHREC rank among those printings, and its price from whichever printing is highest. Every card on this particular list happens to have only one printing, so no tie breaking was needed here.

**What happens when two printings share the exact same price?**
The methodology notes that tie rather than resolving it silently, though this list has no such case since none of these twenty cards carry more than one priced printing in the set.

**What does EDHREC rank actually measure here?**
It reflects how often a card appears in Commander decks as tracked by EDHREC, with a lower number meaning the card is used in more decks.
