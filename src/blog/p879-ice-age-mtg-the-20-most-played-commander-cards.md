Ice Age arrived on the 3rd of June 1995, and thirty years on it still supplies a surprising number of cards that see regular play in Commander. This list ranks the twenty most played Ice Age cards using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decks. The order runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20.

Ice Age is a set full of single printings within its own checklist, so the methodology here is simple in practice even though it is built to handle more complicated cases. Where a card had multiple printings within the set, we would rank it by its best EDHREC rank and price it by its highest priced printing, noting explicitly if two printings landed on the same price rather than quietly picking one. For this particular twenty, every card has just one priced printing in Ice Age, so no tie notes are needed, but the rule stands for future lists built the same way.

Every price below is a snapshot, not a permanent figure. Singles markets move week to week, and a card sitting at AU$0.83 today can drift either direction without any change to how often it is played.
