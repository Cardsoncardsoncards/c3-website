Dragons of Tarkir landed on 27 March 2015, rounding out the Khans of Tarkir block with a full set of dragon legends and the mechanics that came with them. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest single card down to the twentieth.

Every card here has just one printing in the set, so there is no averaging or "highest of several" to worry about, and no ties turned up in this particular twenty. Where a future update to this page does find two printings sharing a price exactly, that tie will be noted in the line rather than quietly broken one way or the other.

Sarkhan Unbroken sits at the top at AU$30.33, and the field spreads out from there down to Dragonlord Ojutai at AU$1.19. Keep in mind that these are snapshot figures. Singles prices move with the market, so the gaps between cards on this list will shift over time even if the order holds roughly steady.
