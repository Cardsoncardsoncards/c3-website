Morningtide rounded out the Lorwyn block when it released on the 1st of February, 2008, bringing a smaller, more focused card pool than its predecessor. It is a set built around tribal synergies and a tighter design, and that focus has left a lasting mark on which cards from it still carry meaningful value today. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price, ordered from number 1, the priciest, down to number 20.

Every card in this list is counted once. Where a card has more than one printing within Morningtide itself, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out explicitly rather than quietly resolved in either direction. As it turns out, every card here has just a single priced printing in the set, so the methodology is simple to apply, but the rule still stands for any set where that is not the case.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a fixed record. Singles markets shift with supply, reprints elsewhere, and general demand across the wider card economy, so a figure quoted here today may look different in a few months. Readers can chase these singles down through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which reflect live listings rather than a static catalogue.

With that groundwork laid, here is how Morningtide's most valuable 20 cards stack up against each other, from the rare that tops the set at AU$78.68 down to the rare that closes out the list at AU$5.81.
