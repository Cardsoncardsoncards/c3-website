Urza's Legacy is the second set in the Urza block, released back on 1999-02-15 as part of the run that closed out the classic Urza storyline. It's a small set by modern standards, but it packed in a number of cards that have held collector interest for over two decades, from artifact tools to efficient creatures that saw play well beyond their era. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to twentieth place.

Each card here appears only once on the list. Where a card has multiple printings within Urza's Legacy, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and any printings sharing an identical price are noted as a tie rather than arbitrarily separated. In practice, every card on this particular list has just a single priced printing in the set, so the numbers you see are the whole picture for each entry.

Grim Monolith sits alone at the top by a wide margin, and the drop from first to second place is the steepest on the list. From there the field settles into a more gradual decline, with rarity mixed throughout, rares dominate but a few uncommons and even a common make an appearance near the bottom.

Keep in mind that card prices are not fixed. What follows is a snapshot, and figures for singles will shift over time depending on the market. For buying and selling in Australia, our own site carries card pages for singles, and eBay Australia is another common avenue.
