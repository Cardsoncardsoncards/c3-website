Darksteel is the second set in the Mirrodin block, released on 6 February 2004, and it remains one of the most quietly influential sets in Commander history thanks to its artifact-heavy design. This list ranks the twenty most played Darksteel cards in the format using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks. It is a play-frequency list, not a price list, so a cheap common can easily outrank an expensive rare if more decks are running it.

Every card here appears once. Where a card had multiple printings within Darksteel itself, it was ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and priced using its highest priced printing, with any exact price ties between printings noted rather than silently resolved. As it happens, none of the twenty cards below have more than one priced printing within this set, so no ties needed calling.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value.
