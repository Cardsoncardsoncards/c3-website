Apocalypse closed out the Invasion block when it released on the 4th of June, 2001, rounding out a story arc that had introduced multicolour strategy as a defining theme of the era. It is a compact set by modern standards, but it still produced a run of cards that have held collector interest across two decades of reprints, format shifts and shifting card pools. This list takes the 20 most expensive cards from that set, measured in Australian dollars, and ranks them from the priciest down to the twentieth.

The methodology is simple to state even if the underlying market is not. Every card on this list appears once, and where a card had more than one printing within Apocalypse itself, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the higher price. Had two printings landed on exactly the same figure, that tie would be called out explicitly rather than quietly resolved one way or another, though as it happens every card here has a single priced printing in the set, so no such tie arises this time.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot rather than a fixed record. Singles markets move with supply, with format legality, and with the general churn of the secondary card market, so a figure recorded today will drift over weeks and months. Treat the ordering and the numbers as accurate to the moment they were pulled rather than as something fixed in stone.

What follows is the list itself, from Shivan Reef at the top down to Mystic Snake at the bottom, with brief notes on how each card sits relative to the rest of the field.
