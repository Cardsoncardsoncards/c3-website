Khans of Tarkir landed on the 26th of September 2014 and reshaped Commander mana bases almost overnight, thanks to a run of fetch lands and tri-lands that are still doing work in decks today. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card sees more play across the format. Number 1 is the most played, number 20 the least played of this group, and the order runs straight down from there.

Where a card has more than one printing within Khans of Tarkir, our methodology takes its best (lowest) EDHREC rank as the entry's rank, and its highest priced printing as the headline figure. Any other printings are listed with their own prices alongside, and if two printings happen to land on the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card on this particular list has a single priced printing in the set, so there is no splitting to speak of here, but the rule stands for future lists.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, and a price quoted here today will not be the price you see tomorrow, so treat every figure as a point in time rather than a promise.
