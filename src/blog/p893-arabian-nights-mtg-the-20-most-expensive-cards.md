Arabian Nights holds the distinction of being the first expansion set ever released for Magic: The Gathering, arriving in December of 1993 and drawing its flavour from the tales of the Arabian Nights folklore collection. It introduced a wave of desert imagery, djinn, efreets and other mechanical firsts to the game, and because it is so early in Magic's printing history, original copies are scarce relative to almost anything printed since. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current price in Australian dollars, from the single most expensive card down to the twentieth.

Where a card has more than one printing within Arabian Nights, it is ranked according to its highest priced printing, with any other printings and their prices noted directly beneath the card's rank. If two printings happen to share an identical price, that tie is called out explicitly rather than being broken by some arbitrary rule. As it turns out, every card on this particular list has just the one priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or secondary prices to report this time around, but the methodology stands for future updates.

It is worth remembering that these figures are a snapshot taken at one point in time. Card prices, particularly for a set as old and limited as Arabian Nights, can shift noticeably over weeks and months as supply and buyer interest change. Treat the numbers below as accurate for now rather than as a permanent ranking.
