Tenth Edition is one of the core sets that reprinted a broad slate of Magic: The Gathering staples and fan favourites, released on 13 July 2007. It draws its card pool from across the game's history rather than introducing new mechanics, which is part of why its price list is such a mix of format-defining rares and quieter uncommons. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20.

Where a card has more than one printing within Tenth Edition, it is ranked by whichever of those printings carries the higher price, and only the printings that actually carry a listed price are counted. If two printings land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. Several cards below have two printings in the set, but the data shows a price for only one of them in each case.

Every figure here is a snapshot. Singles prices for Magic cards move with supply and time, so the order and the gaps between entries can shift after this list is published. Treat the ranking as a picture of the market at the time of writing, not a fixed record.
