Aether Revolt arrived on 2017-01-20 as the second set in the Kaladesh block, and it left Commander tables with a deep bench of efficient artifacts, energy support and colourless utility that still shows up in decks years later. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card sees play in more decks. It is not a price list first and a play list second, it is the reverse: play data sets the order, and price is reported alongside it.

Every card here has one printing in Aether Revolt with a tracked price, so the methodology note about multiple printings and shared prices is mostly a formality for this particular set. Where a card did carry more than one priced printing, the rule is that the ranking uses the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, while the headline price shown is the highest of them. Any exact tie between two printings would be called out rather than quietly resolved, though no such tie appears in this particular twenty.

Prices are listed in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, sometimes because of reprints, sometimes because of shifts in Commander popularity, so treat every figure below as accurate at the time this was written rather than as a permanent number. Readers can check current listings and buy through the card pages on our own site, or compare against listings on eBay Australia.

The cards below run from the most played, at rank 31, down to a group sitting well past rank 2000. Rarity is mixed throughout, with mythics, rares, uncommons and even a couple of commons all finding a place, which says something about how broadly this set's value was spread across the rarity slots.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Aether Revolt release?**
Aether Revolt released on 2017-01-20, as the second set in the Kaladesh block.

**Which card is the most played from Aether Revolt in Commander?**

**How was this list ordered?**
Cards are ordered by EDHREC rank from lowest to highest, since a lower rank means a card appears in more Commander decks.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
A card with several printings in the set is ranked using its best, that is lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and priced using its highest priced printing. If two printings share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently, though none of the twenty cards here had that situation.
