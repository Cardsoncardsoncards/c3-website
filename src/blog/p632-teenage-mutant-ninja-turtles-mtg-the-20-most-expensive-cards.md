Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles is Magic: The Gathering's crossover set built around the four brothers, their allies and their rogues gallery, released on 6 March 2026. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set, measured in Australian dollars at the time of writing. Prices for trading card products move constantly, so treat this as a snapshot rather than a fixed record.

Because many cards in this set were printed more than once, each card appears exactly once on this list, ranked by whichever of its printings carries the highest price. Where a card has several priced printings, the other printing prices are listed alongside the headline figure directly above each entry, and where two printings happen to land on the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved. The gap between a card's cheapest and most expensive printing varies a lot across this list, and that gap is often more revealing than the headline number on its own.

Sitting at the top of the list is Super Shredder, priced above every other card in the set. From there the list runs down through a mix of mythic and rare cards, with prices falling from over forty dollars at the top to a little over three dollars by the twentieth spot. Singles for any of these cards can be bought through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia.
