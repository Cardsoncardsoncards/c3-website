Return to Ravnica landed on 5 October 2012, bringing the ten guilds of the plane back into focus and reviving the shockland cycle for a new generation of players. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price, with Cyclonic Rift sitting alone at the top of the pile at AU$44.32.

Every card in the set is priced individually, and where a card has more than one printing within Return to Ravnica, its highest priced printing is what determines its rank. If two printings shared the exact same price, that tie would be noted rather than quietly resolved one way or another. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just one printing carrying a price, so there are no ties to untangle and no secondary figures to weigh against the headline number.

Prices are not fixed. What follows is a snapshot of the Australian singles market at the time of writing, and the figures attached to each card will shift as supply, printings and interest change over time. Treat the ranking as a picture of one moment rather than a permanent scale.
