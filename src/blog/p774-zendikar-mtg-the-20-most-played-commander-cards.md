Zendikar landed on the 2nd of October 2009, and it remains one of the most influential Magic sets ever printed, largely thanks to a cycle of fetch lands that still shapes how manabases are built in Commander today. This list ranks the twenty Zendikar cards most played in the format, using EDHREC rank as the measure, with a lower number meaning a card shows up in more decks. Misty Rainforest sits at the top, and the list runs down to Valakut, the Molten Pinnacle at twenty.

Some cards in Zendikar have more than one printing within the set itself. Where that happens, the card is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of its printings carries the highest price. If two printings happened to share the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than glossed over. In this particular list every card has just a single priced printing in the set, so the arithmetic stays simple throughout.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars and will drift with the market over time, both up and down, so treat the figures as a point of reference rather than a guarantee. Singles can be sourced through the card pages here on Cards on Cards on Cards, or through eBay Australia, for readers who prefer to compare listings before buying.

Zendikar released on the 2nd of October 2009, and it has been available for well over a decade.

It ranks the twenty Zendikar cards most played in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card appears in more decks.

A card with several printings in Zendikar is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. If two printings share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently.
