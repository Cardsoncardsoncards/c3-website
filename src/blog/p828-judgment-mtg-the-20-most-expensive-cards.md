Judgment is the third and final set in the Odyssey block, released on the 27th of May 2002. It's a set built around graveyard interaction and the Wish cycle, and nearly two decades on it still carries a spread of prices worth tracking for anyone building a collection from that era. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards in the set by current price in Australian dollars.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card had multiple printings within Judgment. Where a card has more than one printing, we rank it by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out plainly rather than being broken by some arbitrary rule. As it happens, every card here has a single priced printing in the set, so there's no internal ranking to untangle this time round, though the note on methodology stands for future sets where that won't be the case.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a fixed value. Singles markets move with supply, demand and time, so the figures printed above each entry reflect a point in time rather than a permanent number. Treat the ordering and the gaps between entries as the useful part of this list, the raw prices themselves will drift.
