Amonkhet arrived on the 28th of April 2017, bringing Egyptian-inspired plane hopping, cycling, and a full cycle of cycling dual lands to Standard and, eventually, Commander tables everywhere. This list ranks the twenty most played Amonkhet cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. It is a snapshot of play patterns, not a value guide, though we have paired each entry with pricing to give some shape to the numbers.

Our methodology is simple but worth spelling out. Each card appears once on this list, even if it saw multiple printings within the set. Where a card has more than one printing here, it is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. If two printings happen to land on the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. In this particular set, every card on the list has just a single priced printing, so the methodology's tie handling and multi printing logic sit in the background rather than the foreground.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a point in time. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a snapshot rather than a permanent fact. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which will reflect more current pricing than a written article ever can.

What follows is the countdown from twenty up to the most played Amonkhet card in Commander, Evolving Wilds, sitting at EDHREC rank 19.
