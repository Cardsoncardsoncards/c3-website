The Hobbit lands in Australia on 14 August 2026. Preorder and spoiler season listings are already active, which means the singles market is already moving before a single pack has been opened at scale.

The prices below are preorder prices, not settled post release trading. Treat this as a read on what collectors expect right now, not a final verdict. We'll publish a follow up once the set has been open on tables for a few weeks, comparing this list against what actually happens once real sales data comes in.

Prerelease events run 7 to 13 August, a full week before the global release, and early sealed pulls from that window tend to create short lived price spikes that don't always hold once broader Play Booster supply lands on 14 August. Collector Boosters are historically the product most likely to concentrate premium treatments of a set's headline cards, worth knowing if you're chasing the exact printing pictured below rather than just a playable copy.

Every card on this list appears once, ranked by its highest priced printing. Several cards in The Hobbit have more than one version, a standard print and a premium alternate. Where that's the case, we've listed the second price underneath so you can see the gap between them. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, we break the tie alphabetically by card name.
