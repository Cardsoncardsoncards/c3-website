Shards of Alara landed on the 3rd of October 2008, a set built around wedge colour combinations and a memorable run of tri-land cycles. It gave Commander players a swathe of enduring staples, from mana fixing to artifact synergy pieces, many of which still see play well over a decade later. This list ranks the twenty most played Shards of Alara cards in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decklists.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card had several printings within the set. When that happens, the card is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved, so the numbers you see reflect exactly what the data shows.

All prices are listed in Australian dollars and are a snapshot at the time of writing. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Singles can be sourced through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking both before buying.
