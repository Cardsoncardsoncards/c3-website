Riftbound: Vendetta releases worldwide on 31 July 2026, and for the first time in the game's history, there is no staggered regional rollout. English and Simplified Chinese versions launch on the same day. If you have been waiting to jump into Riftbound, or you already have a binder full of Origins, Spiritforged and Unleashed, this is the set to pay attention to.

## What is actually in Vendetta

Vendetta is Riftbound's fourth main set, and it is built entirely around rivalry. Four pairs anchor the whole release: Zed against Shen, Nasus against Renekton, Vi against Jinx, and Jayce against Viktor. Every printing decision in the set was filtered through the question of who each card's rival actually is, and it shows in the card pool.

The set trims down from the 12 Legends seen in Origins and Spiritforged to 9, the first time a Riftbound set has done this. Total card count is 166, smaller than earlier sets, but that has not stopped Vendetta from including more than 50 Showcase cards. The headline addition is the Overnumbered treatment, a Showcase subtype that pairs two rival champions on facing artwork, designed so the two halves line up visually when placed side by side.

## Showdown Decks: a new way in

Vendetta also introduces Showdown Decks, Riftbound's first two-player preconstructed product. The confirmed pairing is Shen against Zed, two 56-card decks plus two booster packs in one box, built to be opened and played head to head straight away. It is the same idea that has worked well for Magic's Duel Decks and Pokemon's Battle Decks over the years, and it gives newer players a genuine on-ramp into the rivalry storyline without needing to build a deck from scratch.

Getting hold of one is not as simple as walking into a shop, though. Riot is running Shen vs Zed through a lottery-style entry window. Players register during a 48-hour window, and selections are made entirely at random.

Anyone drawn gets an email with a 24-hour purchase link before the slot passes to someone else. If you want a shot at one, keep an eye on your inbox around launch and make sure Riot's emails are not sitting in spam. If you are locked out of your Riot account or miss the window, Riot's recovery platform and player support ticketing are the official channels to sort it out rather than chasing a resale copy at a markup.

## What to watch on the secondary market

With 9 Legends instead of 12 and a smaller overall card count, chase rarity is likely to concentrate harder into fewer slots than previous sets. The Overnumbered pairs are the obvious early target, since they are a genuinely new subtype rather than another alternate art on an existing treatment, and early demand tends to be highest on mechanics nobody has seen before.

For Australian collectors, the simultaneous global launch is worth noting on its own. Every previous Riftbound set, Origins, Spiritforged and Unleashed, released in China first, with English and other regions following weeks or months later. That staggered gap used to create its own price movement, as import copies traded ahead of local stock while everyone else waited.

Vendetta removes that gap entirely. Expect the market to settle into its real pricing faster than it has for any set so far, since there is no early-adopter import premium to correct for once English stock catches up. That also means the first few days after launch are likely to be a more honest read on genuine demand than they were for Origins, Spiritforged or Unleashed.

## What preview season already told us

Official previews ran from 6 to 17 July, with 222 cards revealed before release day, including the full rundown of the four rivalry pairs and a first look at the Wild Rift collaboration alt art. That means Vendetta is not a total blind buy the way earlier Riftbound sets were on release day. Players who followed the preview season already have a reasonable read on which archetypes the rivalry structure supports, which is worth factoring in if you are deciding between a sealed pool at a launch event or picking up singles directly once the set is live.

Vendetta releases worldwide on 31 July 2026, with no staggered regional rollout. Local game store stock and opening hours will determine exact same-day availability.

**How many Legends are in Vendetta?**
Nine, down from the 12 seen in Origins and Spiritforged. This is the smallest Legend count of any Riftbound set so far.

**What are Overnumbered cards?**
A new Showcase subtype introduced in Vendetta, pairing two rival champions on facing alternate artwork, designed to be displayed side by side.

Through a lottery-style entry window run by Riot. Players register during a 48-hour window, and successful entrants receive a 24-hour purchase link by email.

Simultaneous worldwide release removes the import-gap pricing seen in earlier sets, so Australian prices should track global demand more closely than previous Riftbound releases.
