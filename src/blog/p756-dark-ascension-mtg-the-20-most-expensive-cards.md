Dark Ascension is the second set in the Innistrad block, released on the 3rd of February 2012. It continues the horror themes of its predecessor, leaning further into zombies, werewolves and the machinations of a returning vampire lord. This post looks at the 20 most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, drawing on the singles market as it stands today.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card was printed more than once within Dark Ascension. In those cases the ranking uses the highest priced printing available, and if two printings land on the same price, that tie is noted plainly rather than glossed over. As it happens, every card in this particular list carries a single priced printing within the set, so there are no internal splits to untangle here, though ties do appear between separate cards further down the order.

Mikaeus, the Unhallowed sits alone at the top of the list, priced well clear of everything beneath it. From there the list settles into a steadier decline, with several cards separated by only a handful of cents. Prices are not fixed points, they shift with the market, so treat the figures here as a snapshot rather than a permanent record.

Singles matching these figures can be found through the card pages on our own site, and through eBay Australia, both of which are worth checking against each other before buying.
