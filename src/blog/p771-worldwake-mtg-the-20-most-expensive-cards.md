Worldwake landed on the 5th of February 2010 as the second set in the Zendikar block, and it is remembered as one of the more heavily played small sets of its era. This list ranks the 20 most expensive Worldwake cards by current Australian dollar price, from Eye of Ugin at the top down to Admonition Angel at number 20.

Each card on this list appears once. Where a card has more than one printing within Worldwake, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and any printings that happen to share an identical price are noted as a tie rather than quietly separated. As it happens, every card in this particular top 20 has just a single priced printing in the set, so there is no juggling of variants required here, though the methodology still applies as a rule for the list as a whole.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at time of writing. Singles markets shift, so the figures below should be read as a point-in-time reference rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy can check our own card pages or search listings on eBay Australia for current availability.
