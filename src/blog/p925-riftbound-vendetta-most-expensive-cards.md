Riftbound Vendetta has been out for roughly four weeks, which is long enough for its singles market to have found a level rather than a preorder guess. The shape of that market is unusual even by trading card standards: the most expensive card in the set costs AU$4,320.46, and the identical card in its ordinary printing costs 22 cents.

**Data checked: 26 August 2026.** Vendetta released 31 July 2026. Australian dollar figures are live C3 Card Vault prices, which convert market prices at the daily rate and track the broader market rather than Australian shopfronts. We are ranking by highest priced printing per card, and we say plainly below where a price rests on very few listings.

## Quick answer

The Akali, Rogue Assassin Signature printing leads the set at AU$4,320.46, followed by Zed at AU$1,901.73 and Jayce at AU$1,751.95. Every card in the top ten is either a Signature or an Overnumbered showcase treatment. The base printings of those same legends cost between 19 cents and one dollar. Vendetta is not an expensive set to play. It is an expensive set to collect, and the entire premium sits in two treatment tiers.

## Methodology

Worth stating explicitly, because "most expensive" lists are easy to write misleadingly.

- **Basis:** highest priced printing per card on C3 data at 26 August 2026.
- **Printings are not averaged.** A card's Signature, Overnumbered, Alternate Art and base printings are separate entries with separate prices, and we have not blended them into one figure.
- **Sealed products are listed separately** from single cards rather than mixed into the ranking.
- **These are market prices, not completed sales we have individually verified.** On a set four weeks old, top-end prices frequently rest on a handful of listings.
- **"Most wanted" is used narrowly.** We only apply it where there is a demand signal beyond price, such as competitive play. Price alone on a thin listing pool is not evidence of demand, and we have not treated it as such.

Kennen's Signature printing costs roughly 8,600 times its base printing. They play identically.

This is the single most important thing to understand before buying anything from this set. **Vendetta's competitive cards are almost free.** The [complete package of format-shaping Vendetta commons and uncommons costs about one dollar](/blog/riftbound-vendetta-most-played-cards/). Everything above is a collector market that runs in parallel and has essentially no connection to how the game is played.

## Where competitive demand actually is

Applying the "most wanted" label properly means looking for demand evidence beyond price.

**Kennen has it.** It is the metagame-leading legend at roughly 9 per cent metashare across 16 tournaments with 64 or more players since release. That is a genuine demand signal, and it is the best justification in the set for a premium printing carrying a premium. If you are buying a Kennen premium printing, this is the one card in the set where competitive evidence supports the price, and [current Kennen Vendetta listings on eBay Australia](https://www.ebay.com.au/sch/i.html?_nkw=riftbound+vendetta+kennen&campid=5339146789&customid=vendetta-kennen-chase&mkevt=1&mkcid=1&mkrid=705-53470-19255-0&toolid=10001) are the comparison against the AU$400.91 and AU$1,641.02 tracked figures.

**Nasus has it.** The second Vendetta legend to establish itself competitively.

**Akali does not, in the competitive sense.** It leads the set at AU$4,320.46 without appearing among the four Vendetta legends recording Top 8 finishes. Akali's price is character popularity and treatment scarcity, which are perfectly real drivers, but they are collector drivers rather than competitive ones. That is a legitimate thing to buy provided you know which one you are buying, and [current Akali Vendetta listings on eBay Australia](https://www.ebay.com.au/sch/i.html?_nkw=riftbound+vendetta+akali&campid=5339146789&customid=vendetta-akali-chase&mkevt=1&mkcid=1&mkrid=705-53470-19255-0&toolid=10001) will tell you whether the tracked figure reflects a real market or a handful of listings.

**Ambessa is the clearest caution.** Its Signature printing is AU$708.63 and its Overnumbered is AU$181.34, yet on current evidence it has not established itself as a serious competitive option. That is a premium priced purely on the character and the treatment.

## Understanding the treatment ladder

Riftbound's premium structure trips people up because several tiers look similar in a listing but behave very differently in price. Here is the ladder as the data shows it, cheapest to dearest.

**Base printing.** The ordinary card. Rare or Epic rarity, priced between roughly 13 cents and a few dollars. This is what you play with.

**Alternate Art.** A different illustration on an otherwise ordinary card. Typically a few dollars: [Kennen, Keeper of Balance alternate art](/cards/riftbound/vendetta-kennen-keeper-of-balance-alternate-art-135a-166) at AU$4.65 against AU$0.36 for the base, or [Akali, Silent alternate art](/cards/riftbound/vendetta-akali-silent-alternate-art-038a-166) at AU$24.29. This is the accessible premium tier.

**Showcase promotional.** The SP-numbered cards, sitting between roughly AU$20 and AU$130. [Kai'Sa, Survivor](/cards/riftbound/vendetta-kaisa-survivor-sp1-006) at AU$129.48 tops this band.

**Overnumbered.** Cards numbered above the set's stated size, running from roughly AU$180 to AU$720 in Vendetta.

**Signature.** The top tier, from roughly AU$700 to AU$4,300.

The jump that matters is from Alternate Art to Overnumbered. Everything below that line is affordable, and everything above it is a serious purchase. If you are collecting rather than investing, the Alternate Art tier gives you the visual upgrade at roughly one per cent of the Signature price, and it is where we would point most people. [Current Vendetta alternate art listings on eBay Australia](https://www.ebay.com.au/sch/i.html?_nkw=riftbound+vendetta+alternate+art&campid=5339146789&customid=vendetta-altart-tier&mkevt=1&mkcid=1&mkrid=705-53470-19255-0&toolid=10001) are the practical entry point to that tier.

## The promotional cards that outrank the whole set

One thing worth knowing before you conclude the Akali Signature is the ceiling: it is not, once you look beyond Vendetta itself.

The Riftbound Organized Play promotional cards carry metal printings that outprice everything in the set. [Irelia, Blade Dancer in metal from the Prize Wall](/cards/riftbound/riftbound-organized-play-promotional-cards-irelia-blade-dancer-metal-prize-wall-195-221) is AU$5,075.00, the [Master Yi, Wuju Bladesman metal Prize Wall card](/cards/riftbound/riftbound-organized-play-promotional-cards-master-yi-wuju-bladesman-metal-prize-wall-019-024) is AU$3,223.22, [Diana, Scorn of the Moon in metal](/cards/riftbound/riftbound-organized-play-promotional-cards-diana-scorn-of-the-moon-metal-prize-wall-197-219) is AU$2,754.99, and the [Master Yi metal Best Of](/cards/riftbound/riftbound-organized-play-promotional-cards-master-yi-wuju-bladesman-metal-best-of-019-024) is AU$2,293.29.

Note which legends those are. Irelia, Master Yi and Diana are exactly the established competitive names from the [most played rankings](/blog/riftbound-vendetta-most-played-cards/), and their metal promos sit above the entire Vendetta Signature tier.

That is a genuinely useful signal about where durable value sits in this game. The most expensive cards in Riftbound are not the newest premium treatments from the current set. They are organised-play promos of legends that have held competitive relevance across multiple sets, and their scarcity comes from an event distribution that cannot be reprinted into.

The practical implication for anyone treating Vendetta Signatures as a store of value: the top of this game's market rewards proven competitive staples with event-limited distribution, not new-set treatments priced on release attention.

## Cards to avoid chasing at peak hype

- **Anything Signature, if you intend to play with it.** The base printing is the same card for a fraction of one per cent of the price.
- **Ambessa premium printings.** Highest premium-to-performance mismatch in the set.
- **Top-end prices generally, right now.** Four weeks post-release with thin listing pools at the top end is a noisy market. A very small number of transactions can move a headline figure sharply.
- **Anything bought expecting Radiance not to matter.** Radiance releases 23 October 2026 and will reset attention across the game.

## What is likely to move

**Upward pressure:** Kennen premium printings, if it holds its metashare lead through [RQ Singapore](/blog/riftbound-rq-singapore-australia-guide/) on 4 to 6 September. Competitive validation is the most durable support a collector price can have.

**Downward pressure:** Ambessa premiums, if it continues to underperform. Mel premiums, while Ezreal and Diana remain preferred.

**Wildcard:** Radiance on 23 October. A new set moves attention and money, and Vendetta's premium tier is priced on attention.

Track live movement at the [C3 Riftbound Card Vault](/cards/riftbound), compare across sets on [C3 Market Insights](/market), or [check current Vendetta listings on eBay Australia](https://www.ebay.com.au/sch/i.html?_nkw=riftbound+vendetta+signature&campid=5339146789&customid=vendetta-most-expensive&mkevt=1&mkcid=1&mkrid=705-53470-19255-0&toolid=10001).

## The C3 take

Buy the base printings if you want to play, and there has rarely been a cheaper set to get competitive with. Buy the Runes if you want an accessible, bounded collecting target. Approach the Signature tier as a collectible rather than an investment, on a four week old market where the top prices rest on very few listings.
