Core Set 2019 arrived on 2018-07-13, and nearly seven years on it remains one of the more heavily played core sets at the Commander table. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from that set according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card sees more play across the format. This is not a price list dressed up as a popularity list, it is the reverse, popularity first, with pricing shown for context.

Every card here has a single printing in Core Set 2019 that carries a price, so there is no averaging or best-of-several-printings math to explain this time around. Where a set does throw up multiple printings, our method takes the lowest (best) EDHREC rank as the card's position and its highest priced printing as the headline figure, noting explicitly any case where two printings land on the same price rather than quietly picking one. That housekeeping doesn't come into play for this particular twenty, but it is worth stating plainly since it governs how the ranking is built.

Reliquary Tower sits at the top, the single most played card from the set at EDHREC rank 10, priced at AU$5.50. From there the list runs down through commons, uncommons, rares and mythics alike, a reminder that play frequency in Commander has little to do with rarity or price tag. Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only, they move with the market, so treat every figure here as accurate at time of writing rather than fixed.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Core Set 2019 release?**
Core Set 2019 released on 2018-07-13, and this list draws on that full set.

**Which card is the most played from Core Set 2019 in Commander?**
Reliquary Tower is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 10, priced at AU$5.50.

**How was this list built?**
Every card in the set was checked against its EDHREC rank, and the twenty lowest (most played) ranks were kept, ordered from number 1 down to number 20.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
A card with several printings in Core Set 2019 is ranked by its lowest (best) EDHREC rank and priced by its highest priced printing, and if two printings share an identical price that tie is noted rather than resolved silently. None of the twenty cards on this particular list had more than one priced printing, so this rule did not need to be applied here.

**Are the prices on this list fixed?**

**Does a card's price reflect how often it is played in Commander?**
Not necessarily. Several rares and mythics on this list are priced lower than commons or uncommons ranked near them, showing that play frequency and price do not always move together.
