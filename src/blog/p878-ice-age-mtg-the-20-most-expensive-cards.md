Ice Age arrived back in June 1995, and it remains one of the most recognisable early expansions to the Magic: The Gathering card pool. It introduced the cycle of pain lands that still turn up in this list decades later, along with a scattering of rares and uncommons that have held collector interest for different reasons. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current price in Australian dollars.

Each card on this list appears once. Where a card has more than one printing within Ice Age, it is ranked by whichever of those printings carries the highest price, and if two printings happened to share a price exactly, that tie would be noted rather than quietly resolved one way or another. In this particular run of twenty, every card here has a single tracked printing in the set, so the headline figure above each entry is simply that card's price.

Prices are shown to the cent and were accurate at the time of writing, but singles pricing shifts over time with supply and demand, so treat the figures as a snapshot rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy can check card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia.
