Duskmourn: House of Horror is Magic: The Gathering's horror-themed set, released on 27 September 2024. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from that set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decks. It is a play-frequency list, not a price list, though prices are shown alongside for reference.

Several of these cards have more than one printing within Duskmourn: House of Horror, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other variants. Where a card has multiple printings, it appears once on this list, ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across all versions, and priced by whichever printing carries the highest price. When two printings happen to share an identical price, that tie is called out directly rather than glossed over.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are current as of when this data was pulled. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a snapshot rather than a fixed number. For current pricing, check the individual card pages on our site or search listings on eBay Australia.

The list runs from the single most played card in the set down to the twentieth. Terramorphic Expanse tops it at EDHREC rank 27, a considerable distance ahead of everything else here.
