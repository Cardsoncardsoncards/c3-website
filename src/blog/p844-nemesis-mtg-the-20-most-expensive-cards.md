Nemesis is the second set in the Masques block, released on 2000-02-14, and it remains a compact but keenly tracked entry in Magic's early history. This list gathers the twenty most expensive cards from that set, ranked from number 1 down to number 20 by their current Australian dollar price. Every card here draws from real listings, and the ordering reflects exactly where each one sits today rather than any judgement about design or playability.

The methodology is simple but worth stating plainly. Each card appears once on the list, even if it saw more than one printing within Nemesis. Where a card has multiple printings, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card in this particular twenty has just one printing carrying a price in our data, so the comparisons here are clean, one number against another, with no averaging or hidden weighting.

Nemesis sits at an interesting point in Magic's printing history, old enough that quantities are limited but not so old that it carries the scarcity of the earliest sets. That combination shows up in the spread of prices below, from a top figure a little over fifty dollars down to entries sitting just above three dollars. The rarity breakdown is mixed too, with rares dominating the upper and middle portions of the list but a handful of uncommons and commons finding their way in as well.

Keep in mind that every price quoted here is a snapshot. Card markets move, sometimes gradually and sometimes quickly, and a figure that is accurate today may look quite different in a few months. Readers can buy singles either through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking current listings before assuming any number below still holds.
