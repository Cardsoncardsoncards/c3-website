The Lost Caverns of Ixalan released on 17 November 2023, returning Magic to the plane of Ixalan with a full complement of rares and mythics that have since settled into their own distinct price bands on the secondary market. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by Australian dollar price, drawing on the full range of printings each card received within Lost Caverns of Ixalan itself, whether that means a single showcase treatment or several stylistic variants sold alongside the standard version.

Where a card has more than one printing in the set, it is ranked by whichever of those printings currently carries the highest price, and the other printing or printings are noted alongside it rather than folded away. When two printings land on exactly the same figure, that tie is stated plainly instead of being broken by some arbitrary tiebreaker. This keeps the methodology honest: a card with a AU$70 alternate art and a AU$20 borderless version is ranked on the AU$70 figure, but the AU$20 figure is not hidden from the reader.

Every price below is a snapshot taken at one point in time. Magic singles move constantly, sometimes by cents and sometimes by dollars, as supply, print runs and format relevance shift after release. Treat the figures here as a reading for the day this list was compiled rather than a permanent ranking, and expect some movement if you check back later.

Readers looking to buy any of these singles in Australia can do so through the card pages on our own site, or through eBay Australia, where both individual sellers and larger stores list Lost Caverns of Ixalan singles across a range of conditions and printings.
