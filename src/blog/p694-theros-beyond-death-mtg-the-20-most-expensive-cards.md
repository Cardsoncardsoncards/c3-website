Theros Beyond Death is the return trip to the plane of Theros, released on the 24th of January 2020, and it remains a set with a distinctive spread of gods, devotion cards and constructed staples that have kept it relevant well after its release date. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards in the set by their current Australian dollar price, drawing on the same singles data that our card pages and eBay Australia listings reflect.

Where a card has more than one printing in Theros Beyond Death, such as a showcase or borderless treatment alongside the regular version, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the higher price. That other printing's price is still shown, so readers can see the gap between versions at a glance. Where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Shadowspear sits at the top of the list, the only card in the set to clear the sixty dollar mark, with Nyxbloom Ancient and Thassa's Oracle rounding out the podium behind it. From there the list tapers fairly steadily down to Arasta of the Endless Web at number twenty, though the drop-off between some neighbours is sharper than others.

Every price here is a snapshot. Singles prices move with supply, demand and time, so the figures attached to each card reflect a moment rather than a fixed value. Anyone buying or selling should check the current listing on our site or on eBay Australia rather than relying on numbers from a ranking article.
