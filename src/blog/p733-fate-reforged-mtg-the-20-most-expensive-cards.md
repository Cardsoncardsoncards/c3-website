Fate Reforged arrived on 23 January 2015 as the second set in the Khans of Tarkir block, jumping the story forward to explore an alternate timeline. It is a smaller set than its predecessor, built around the five wedge clans as they might have looked under different circumstances, and it produced a handful of cards that have held collector interest well beyond release.

This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards in the set by current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20 as stated. Where a card has more than one printing within Fate Reforged, only the highest priced printing is used to set its rank, and any printing that shares an identical price with another is noted as a tie rather than silently broken by some other factor.

Prices here are a snapshot, not a fixed record. Singles markets move constantly with supply, so what you see today on a card page or on eBay Australia may differ from the figures printed alongside this list even a short time later.
