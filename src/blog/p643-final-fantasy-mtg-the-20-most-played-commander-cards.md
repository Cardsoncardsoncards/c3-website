Final Fantasy is Magic: The Gathering's crossover set with Square Enix's long running franchise, released on 13 June 2025. It touched down with the sort of card day frenzy usually reserved for Secret Lair drops, and months on it has settled into a distinct pocket of the Commander metagame. This list ranks the 20 most played cards from the set in Commander decks, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decklists.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Final Fantasy itself, whether as a showcase treatment, a borderless version, or some other alternate frame. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, it appears once on this list, ranked by whichever printing has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. When two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

The prices quoted throughout this piece are a snapshot only. Card prices move constantly, sometimes within days, so treat every figure here as accurate at time of writing rather than a permanent fact about the card. For current pricing, our own card pages track singles alongside listings on eBay Australia, both of which are common ways local players source these cards.

What follows is a rundown from the most played card in the set down to the twentieth, with commentary on how each card's placement and pricing sit against the rest of the list rather than any restatement of the numbers already shown above each entry.
