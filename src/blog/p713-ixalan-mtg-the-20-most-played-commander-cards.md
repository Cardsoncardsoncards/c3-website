Ixalan arrived on the 29th of September 2017, bringing dinosaurs, pirates and a return to Standard-era tribal themes, but its legacy in Commander runs through a quieter set of cards, mostly the dual lands that quietly slot into nearly every deck that can run them. This list ranks the twenty most played Ixalan cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks. The order runs from number 1, the single most played card, down to number 20.

Where a card has more than one printing within Ixalan itself, our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and prices by whichever printing carries the highest price tag. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. As it happens, every card on this particular list has only a single priced printing within the set, so the maths stays simple here.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a few cents in a week, sometimes more, so treat every figure here as accurate at the time of writing rather than a permanent fixture. Readers can chase these cards down through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking both before settling on a purchase.

The result is a spread that runs from cheap, common utility pieces through to cards sitting well into double digits, with the ranking driven entirely by how often each card turns up in decklists rather than by its price.
