Limited Edition Beta is one of the earliest Magic: The Gathering sets ever printed, released on 1993-10-04 as the second print run of the game's original card pool. Nearly every card in it has gone on to a long life in Commander decks, which makes it a useful lens for looking at how age, rarity and playability intersect in the modern singles market. This list ranks the twenty most played Beta cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play across the format.

Where a card has more than one printing within Beta itself, our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank for its position on the list, and prices it by whichever of those printings carries the higher price tag. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than resolved arbitrarily. In this particular set every card on the list has a single priced printing, so no such ties or gaps appear here, but the rule still governs how the list was built.

Every price below is a snapshot, quoted in Australian dollars, and the singles market moves constantly, so treat these figures as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy can find these cards through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which carry current listings that will drift from what's printed here over time.
