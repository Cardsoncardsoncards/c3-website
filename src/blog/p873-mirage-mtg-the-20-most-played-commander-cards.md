Mirage arrived on 8 October 1996, one of the more overlooked expansions of Magic's early years, and yet a surprising number of its cards still see play in Commander today. This list ranks the twenty most played Mirage cards according to EDHREC rank, a measure of how often a card turns up in published Commander decklists across the format. A lower rank means the card shows up more often, and the list runs in that order, from number one down to number twenty.

Every card in this set appears on the list exactly once, even where it has more than one printing within Mirage itself. Where a card does have multiple printings, it is ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and priced by its highest priced printing, and any exact price ties between printings are called out rather than quietly smoothed over. As it happens, every card here has just one priced printing in this set, so there is no tie to report and no gap between printings to explain.

Prices are given in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, sometimes sharply, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy can check our own card pages or search eBay Australia, where Mirage stock turns up with some regularity given the set's age.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Mirage release?**
Mirage released on 8 October 1996, making it one of the older sets to still show up regularly in Commander decklists.

**Which Mirage card is the most played in Commander?**
Rampant Growth holds that position, sitting at EDHREC rank 26, comfortably ahead of every other card in this list.

**How does the ranking handle cards with more than one printing in the set?**
A card with multiple printings is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. Where two printings share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently, though none of the cards in this particular list had more than one priced printing.

**Where can I buy these Mirage singles in Australia?**
Our own card pages carry singles for these cards, and eBay Australia is another option worth checking given how long this set has been in circulation.

**Are the prices in this list fixed?**
No, every price here is a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at one point in time. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so figures should be treated as a reference point rather than a guarantee.

**Why does the most expensive card on the list not rank first in play?**
Price and play rate measure different things. Lion's Eye Diamond, for example, is priced far above every other card here but ranks well behind cards like Rampant Growth and Dark Ritual on EDHREC.

**What does EDHREC rank actually measure?**
It reflects how often a card appears across published Commander decklists tracked by EDHREC, with a lower number meaning the card appears more often. It says nothing about price on its own.
