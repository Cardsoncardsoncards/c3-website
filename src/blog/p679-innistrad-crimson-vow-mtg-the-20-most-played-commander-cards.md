Innistrad: Crimson Vow arrived on 2021-11-19 as the second half of Innistrad's return, following on from Innistrad: Midnight Hunt and leaning further into the set's vampire and gothic horror themes. It has since settled into Commander's card pool as a source of lands, value creatures and a handful of build-around mythics, and this list ranks its twenty most played cards in the format according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card sees more play.

Several cards in Crimson Vow were printed more than once within the set, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other variants. Where that happens, the card is ranked once using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing, with the other printing or printings listed alongside for comparison. When two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved by an arbitrary tiebreaker.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes a lot, so treat every figure as a point in time rather than a permanent value. Readers looking to buy can check card pages on our own site or browse listings on eBay Australia.

The most played card from the set by a wide margin is Evolving Wilds, sitting at EDHREC rank 19, well ahead of everything else on this list. From there the list moves through the set's dual lands, its vampire payoffs and a scattering of commons and mythics that found homes in decks across the format.
