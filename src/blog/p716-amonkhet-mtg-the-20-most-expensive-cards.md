Amonkhet arrived on the 28th of April 2017, bringing Magic's players to the desert plane of Naktamun for a set built around exert, eternalise, and a memorable cycle of God cards. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, drawing on our own listings and the wider Australian market. It's a snapshot of a set that's had years to settle into the secondary market, and the results show a fairly steep drop-off from the top.

Every card on this list is counted once. Where a card has more than one printing within Amonkhet, we rank it by whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings happen to land on the exact same figure, that tie is called out rather than glossed over. As it turns out, none of the twenty cards below have a second priced printing within the set itself, so each entry here is a single line rather than a comparison between versions.

Prices are not fixed. They move with supply, demand and time, and the figures quoted below are current at time of writing rather than permanent. Anyone using this list as a shopping guide should check the live price on the card page before assuming the number still holds.

Australian buyers can pick up singles through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and prices between those two markets can and do differ.
