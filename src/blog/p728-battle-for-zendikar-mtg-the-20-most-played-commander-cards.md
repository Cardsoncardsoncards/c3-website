Battle for Zendikar arrived on 2 October 2015, bringing back the plane's hallmark lands and a wave of Eldrazi-themed cards that have since found homes in Commander decks of all stripes. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decks. It runs from number one, the card seen in the most decklists, down to number twenty.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card had more than one printing within the set. Where that happens, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the lowest (best) EDHREC rank, and the price shown is for whichever printing is highest priced. Any two printings that land on exactly the same price are noted as a tie rather than having one arbitrarily placed above the other.

For every card in this set, there was only a single priced printing to work with, so the methodology's tie-breaking and multi-printing rules mostly sit in reserve here rather than doing visible work. Still, the numbers are worth reading carefully, because the gap between the top of this list and the bottom is wide, both in play rate and in price.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly, so treat every figure here as accurate at time of writing rather than a permanent value.
