Saviors of Kamigawa closed out the original Kamigawa block when it released on 2005-06-03, and it remains a small set that still turns up in a surprising number of Commander decks two decades later. This list ranks the twenty Saviors of Kamigawa cards most played in Commander today, ordered using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks. Every card here is a single printing within this set, so there is no tie-breaking or multi-printing arithmetic to walk through this time around, each entry simply carries its own EDHREC rank and its own price.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at time of writing. Card prices move, sometimes by a lot, so treat the figures printed above each blurb as a point in time rather than a promise. Where a card in this set has more than one printing with a price attached, our methodology takes the highest priced printing as the headline figure and notes any exact ties rather than quietly picking one, though for this particular twenty that situation doesn't arise.

What follows is a straight countdown from the most played card in the set down to the twentieth, with a short note on each entry about where it sits relative to its neighbours. Mikokoro, Center of the Sea leads the pack by a wide EDHREC margin, and from there the list settles into a mix of rares, uncommons and commons whose prices don't track their play rank in any tidy way.
