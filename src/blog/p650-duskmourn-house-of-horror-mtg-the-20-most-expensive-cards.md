Duskmourn: House of Horror is the horror-flavoured Magic: The Gathering set that released on 27 September 2024, and it has settled in as a set with a distinct mix of mythic showpieces and workhorse rares that keep showing up in cash prices. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by Australian dollar price, counting down from number 1 at the top to number 20 at the bottom. Every dollar figure quoted here is in Australian dollars and reflects a snapshot in time, not a fixed value, so treat the ordering as a picture of the market on the day these figures were pulled rather than a permanent ranking.

Several cards in Duskmourn were printed more than once inside the set itself, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other variant slots, and those variant printings do not always carry a tracked price. Where a card has multiple priced printings, this list uses the single highest priced version to decide its rank, and the other printings are listed alongside it for comparison. On the rare occasion that two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than being quietly broken one way or the other.

Rarity varies across this list too. Mythic rares dominate the top end, but several regular rares, including the three-card Verge cycle members that appear more than once here, sit comfortably inside the top twenty on the strength of their headline printing alone. Multiple printings per card is normal for this set, not an anomaly, so a low printing count next to a card like Untimely Malfunction is just as informative as a wide gap between two versions of a mythic like Overlord of the Balemurk.

As always with singles pricing, these figures move. Card prices respond to shifts in supply, print runs and general market drift over time, so what follows is a snapshot rather than a guarantee. Readers looking to buy or sell singles in Australia can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which reflect more current pricing than any fixed list can.
