Dominaria United returned to the plane of Dominaria in September 2022, mixing new legendary characters with a strong vein of cycle lands and efficient spells that Commander players folded into their decks almost immediately. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card shows up in more decklists. It is a played list, not a price list, so a card's spot here reflects how often it gets slotted into a deck rather than what it costs to buy.

Several cards in Dominaria United have more than one printing within the set itself, whether through borderless treatments, promo versions or other variants. Where that happens, this list uses the single lowest EDHREC rank across those printings to decide placement, and prices the card at whichever printing carries the highest price. If two printings happen to land on the same price, that tie is noted plainly rather than smoothed over. The result is a list where the ranking tracks play, while the pricing detail underneath tracks the spread across every version printed in this set.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets shift week to week, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. The commentary below each entry looks at how printings compare to each other and how each card sits against its neighbours on the list, without repeating the numbers already shown above it.
