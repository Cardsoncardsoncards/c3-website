Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles is Magic: The Gathering's crossover set, released on 2026-03-06, bringing Splinter, the four brothers and a cast of mutants into card form. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decks. It is not a price list first and a play list second, it is the other way around, with pricing shown for context rather than as the sorting key.

Several cards in this set have multiple printings, and the methodology treats each card as a single entry regardless of how many versions exist. Where a card has more than one printing, it is ranked using its best (lowest) EDHREC number and priced using its highest priced printing, with the other printings' prices listed alongside for comparison. On the rare occasion two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly broken.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles can be bought through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and figures for both move over time as supply and demand shift, so treat every dollar figure here as a moment in time rather than a fixed number.
