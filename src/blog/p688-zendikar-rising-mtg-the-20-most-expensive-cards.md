Zendikar Rising returned to the plane of Zendikar with a set built around the modal double faced cards that had first appeared in Innistrad, and it landed on 25 September 2020 with full parties, party mechanics and a heavy dose of land based value. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by their current price in Australian dollars, running from the priciest at number one down to number twenty. It is a price list, not a guide to what any card does, so nothing here touches rules text or in game function.

Some cards in Zendikar Rising were printed more than once within the set itself, most obviously through showcase or borderless treatments sitting alongside the ordinary version. Where a card has multiple printings, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price, and the other printing's price is noted separately rather than folded into an average. If two printings ever land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out plainly rather than quietly broken by some arbitrary tiebreaker.

Every figure quoted here, in the ranking itself and in the FAQ below, is written exactly as pulled from the pricing data, cents included. Card prices are not fixed things. They shift with supply, with reprints elsewhere, and with the ordinary churn of a secondary market, so treat this list as a snapshot taken at one point in time rather than a permanent scoreboard. For Australian buyers looking to act on any of this, singles are available through the card pages on our own site as well as through eBay Australia.
