Planar Chaos landed on the 2nd of February 2007, a small set built around the idea of colours doing jobs they normally don't. Fifteen years on, it still turns up in Commander decks with surprising regularity, and this list ranks its twenty most played cards by EDHREC rank, from Urborg, Tomb of Yawgmoth at the top down to Braids, Conjurer Adept at twenty. Lower EDHREC rank means the card shows up in more decks, so this is a play-frequency list rather than a price list.

Where a card in this set has more than one printing, our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and prices it by whichever printing carries the highest price. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card in this particular twenty has a single priced printing in Planar Chaos, so the maths stays simple here even though the rule exists for sets where it doesn't.

Every headline price is a snapshot taken at the time of writing. Card prices in Australian dollars move, sometimes quickly, and a figure quoted today may look different in a month. Singles can be sourced through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and it's worth checking both before buying.

**How is this list ordered?**
Cards are ranked from most played to least played using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card appears in more decks.

**How are multiple printings within the set handled?**
When a card has several printings in Planar Chaos, its play rank is taken from the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank among those printings, and its price is taken from whichever printing is priced highest.
