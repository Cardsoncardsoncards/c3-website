Magic 2014 was the last of Wizards of the Coast's annual "core set" releases before the format was retired, landing on the 19th of July 2013 with a mix of returning staples, sliver tribal support, and a handful of planeswalkers. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, drawing on the market as it stands rather than on nostalgia or nameplate recognition.

Each card on this list appears once. Where a card has more than one printing within Magic 2014, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out directly rather than quietly resolved. In practice, every card here has just a single priced printing in the set, so the ranking is a plain sort from highest to lowest.

Prices are not fixed. What follows is a snapshot of the secondary market at the time of writing, and figures for singles will drift as supply and demand shift over time. Readers looking to buy or sell can check current listings through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, both of which will reflect more up to date numbers than any list can capture permanently.
