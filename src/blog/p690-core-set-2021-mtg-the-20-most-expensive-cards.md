Core Set 2021 arrived on 2020-07-03 as one of the last of the annual "core set" releases Wizards of the Coast produced before shifting its release calendar around, and it has stuck in collectors' minds thanks to a run of mythic bombs that still show up in Commander decks and cube drafts today. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by Australian dollar price, drawing on our own catalogue data. It is a snapshot of value, not a guide to what is good on the battlefield.

The methodology is simple to state but worth spelling out. Where a card has more than one printing within Core Set 2021, whether that is a showcase treatment, a borderless variant, or some other alternate frame, we take the highest priced version as the card's ranking price and list the other printings underneath it for comparison. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. Cards with a single printing in the set are ranked on that printing alone.

A few cards on this list have a striking number of printings. Teferi, Master of Time in particular carries nine separate priced versions in the set, which is unusual and worth noting before you get to its entry. Most other cards here have just one or two printings, so the spread between the headline price and the next cheapest version tends to be smaller and more direct to read.

Every price quoted here, including the AU$74.57 that puts Terror of the Peaks at the top of the list, is a snapshot taken at time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes by a little and sometimes by a lot, so treat these figures as a point of reference rather than a fixed number. Readers looking to buy or sell can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, where listings for Core Set 2021 singles are generally easy to find.
