Magic Origins launched on 2015-07-17 as the Core Set that closed out the pre-planeswalker origin story arc, and it has quietly stocked Commander decks ever since with fixing lands, a few efficient rares and a couple of mythics that have aged into build-arounds. This list ranks the 20 most played Magic Origins cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means more decks include the card. Rogue's Passage sits at the top of the list, and Herald of the Pantheon closes it out at number 20.

Every card here appears exactly once on the list, even where a card has more than one printing within Magic Origins. Our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings to set the position, and the highest priced printing to set the headline figure shown above each entry. Where two printings land on the same price, that is called out directly rather than glossed over. As it happens, every card in this particular top 20 has just a single priced printing in the set, so there is no tie or spread to untangle this time.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes within days, so treat the figures here as a point in time rather than a guarantee of what you will pay when you go looking. Readers can source these cards through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking both before buying.

The spread across this list is wide, from a headline price of AU$0.20 up to AU$14.44, and the EDHREC ranks run from 18 all the way out past 1800. That range says something about how differently these cards get used, from cheap fixing lands that slot into almost any deck to pricier rares and mythics that ask for a more specific build.
