Kaladesh arrived on the 30th of September 2016, bringing an artifact-heavy, energy-counter world to Standard and, in the years since, to a long list of Commander decks. This post ranks the twenty most played Kaladesh cards in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure of play, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decklists. It is a played list, not a value list, so cards that are cheap to acquire can still sit near the top if a lot of decks want them.

Every card here appears exactly once, even where Kaladesh printed it more than once. When a card has multiple printings in the set, we take its best (lowest) EDHREC rank to place it on the list, and we price it at whatever its highest priced printing currently sells for. If two printings land on exactly the same price, we say so rather than quietly picking one over the other.

All twenty cards below happen to have just a single priced printing in this set, so there is no splitting of prices to untangle this time round. The prices themselves are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, so treat the figures as a point in time rather than a fixed number.

The order below runs from number 1, the most played Kaladesh card in Commander, down to number 20. Read on for how each card compares to its neighbours on the list.
