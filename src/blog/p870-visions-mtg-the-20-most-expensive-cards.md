Visions arrived on the 3rd of February 1997 as a small expansion set to Magic: The Gathering, and it has left behind a surprisingly dense list of pricey singles for a set of its size. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards in Visions by Australian dollar price, from the top single down to number 20, using current figures rather than any adjusted or historical value.

The methodology is simple. Every card appears once on this list. Where a card has more than one printing within Visions, it is ranked by whichever of those printings carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, none of the 20 cards below have a second priced printing in this set, so each entry stands on a single figure.

Every price here is a snapshot. Magic singles move in value constantly, sometimes by a little and sometimes sharply, so the numbers attached to each card reflect the moment this list was compiled rather than a fixed or guaranteed price. Readers in Australia can check current listings and buy singles through the card pages on our own site, or through eBay Australia, where prices will naturally vary from what is shown here.

What follows is the countdown itself, each card given its rank, price, printing count and rarity directly above a short note on where it sits relative to the rest of the field.
