Antiquities was released on 1994-03-04, and remains one of the most distinctive early expansions in Magic's history thanks to its heavy focus on artifacts and its small, tightly themed card pool. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by their Australian dollar price today, running from the single priciest card down to the twentieth spot. Because the set is small and its print runs are decades old, prices for individual cards can vary a great deal, and that variation is exactly what this list sets out to capture.

Some cards in Antiquities were printed more than once within the set itself, and where that happens, this list uses the highest priced printing of each card to determine its rank. Other printings of that same card are listed alongside it rather than ignored, so readers can see the full spread rather than just the top figure. On the rare occasion that two printings share the same price to the cent, that tie is noted plainly rather than resolved by an arbitrary tiebreaker.

Every price here is a snapshot taken at a point in time. Magic singles move in price constantly, influenced by supply, print history and collector interest across markets, and a figure quoted today may look quite different in a few months. Readers in Australia can source these singles through the card pages on our own site, and through eBay Australia, both of which reflect live market movement rather than the fixed figures in this article.

The list below covers a wide spread of rarities and price points, from four figure rares at the very top to commons and uncommons rounding out the bottom of the twenty. Read on for the full ranking, with brief notes on each card's place in the order.
