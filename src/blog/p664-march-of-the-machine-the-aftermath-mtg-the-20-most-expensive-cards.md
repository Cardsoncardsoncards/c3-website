March of the Machine: The Aftermath is the small epilogue set that followed on from March of the Machine, released on 12 May 2023. It is a compact set, but it still carries a spread of planeswalkers, mythic legends and rares that have settled at very different points on the price ladder. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to number 20.

Where a card has several printings within the set, it is ranked by whichever of those printings is priced highest, and the other printings are listed alongside it rather than folded away. Where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. Most cards in this set carry five printings, though only three of them tend to carry a tracked price at any given time.

Every figure here is a snapshot. Singles prices on cards this old move around with supply and demand, so treat the numbers as a point-in-time reading rather than a fixed fact. Readers looking to buy or sell any of these cards can do so through the card pages on our own site or via eBay Australia.
