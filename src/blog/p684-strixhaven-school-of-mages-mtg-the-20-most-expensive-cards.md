Strixhaven: School of Mages arrived on the 23rd of April 2021, bringing Magic's take on a magical university, complete with five rival colleges and a run of lesson-themed uncommons. It is a set built around learning and lore, but this list has nothing to do with flavour text. It is a plain ranking of the 20 most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20.

Where a card has more than one printing within Strixhaven, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price. Any other printings and their prices are noted alongside the headline figure, and where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. A few cards in the set carry three printings, though not all of those printings carry a tracked price.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a fixed value. Singles markets move with supply, so the gaps between a headline price and its other printings will shift over time even if the relative order of the list holds steady for a while. Treat every figure here as accurate at the time it was recorded, not as a permanent number.
