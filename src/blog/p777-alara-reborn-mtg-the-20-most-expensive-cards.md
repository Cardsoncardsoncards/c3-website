Alara Reborn is the third and final set in the original Alara block, released on 30 April 2009. It's a set built almost entirely around multicolour cards, and it's remembered for a run of high-impact rares and mythics that have held collector interest well over a decade later. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards in the set by current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20.

Where a card has more than one printing within Alara Reborn, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price, and any printings that share an identical price are noted as a tie rather than artificially separated. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just a single priced printing in the set, so there's no splitting of printings to untangle here, though a couple of positions near the bottom of the list do sit close enough together to be worth a second look.

All figures below are in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at time of writing. Card prices are not fixed and they move with the market, so treat this as a point-in-time reading rather than a permanent order. Singles can be sourced through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and prices seen there may differ slightly from what's listed here.
