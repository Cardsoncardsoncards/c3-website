Aetherdrift is the Magic: The Gathering set released on 14 February 2025, and this list looks at the 20 most expensive cards from it in Australian dollars. The set brought a run of high velocity mechanics and a full cycle of "Verge" lands, several of which appear well up this list. What follows is a ranking, not a rules guide, built purely from price data as it stands at the time of writing.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card was printed more than once within Aetherdrift. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and the other printings for that card are noted alongside it. Where two printings of the same card land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

The list runs from number 1, the single most expensive card in the set, down to number 20. Riverpyre Verge sits at the top. Prices for Magic singles move constantly with supply, demand and reprint news, so treat every figure here as a snapshot rather than a fixed number, and check current listings before buying.
