Conflux is the third and final small set in the Alara block, released on 6 February 2009. It sits between Shards of Alara and Alara Reborn and rounds out the block's five-colour theme with a run of gold cards, mythic bombs and the odd overlooked common. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set in Australian dollars, from the priciest single card down to number twenty.

Every card in this set carries a single printing with a listed price, so there are no internal ties to note and no split between a "headline" figure and a cheaper alternate version. Where a future update introduces a second printing for any of these cards, this list's methodology would rank by whichever printing costs more, and flag any exact price matches rather than quietly picking a winner. For now, each entry below reflects one card, one printing, one price.

Prices are pulled at a point in time and will drift as the market does. Treat the figures here as a snapshot rather than a permanent ranking, and check the live price on the card page before buying.
