Onslaught arrived on 2002-10-07 as one of the pivotal tribal sets of Magic's early history, and more than two decades later it still turns up constantly in Commander decks thanks to a run of fetch lands and value pieces that never really went out of style. This list ranks the twenty most played Onslaught cards in the format according to EDHREC, where a lower rank number means a card sees more play across submitted decklists. Number one is the most played card in the set, and the list counts down from there to number twenty.

Every card here appears only once, even where Onslaught printed it more than once. When a card has multiple printings in the set, its position on this list uses the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank among those printings, and its listed price is the highest price of the group. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. As it happens, every card in this particular top twenty has only a single priced printing in Onslaught, so the methodology note matters more for how the list was built than for anything a reader will see reflected in the entries below.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes by a little and sometimes by a lot, and a fetch land or a niche uncommon can shift in price over weeks or months without anything about the card's Commander popularity changing at all. Treat every dollar figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent fact about the card.

The shape of this list is a familiar one for anyone who has looked at Onslaught's Commander footprint before: a cluster of expensive fetch lands at the very top, then a long tail of cheaper rares, uncommons and commons that see play for narrower reasons. That split between the five headline lands and everything beneath them is really the story of the whole set as far as Commander is concerned.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Onslaught release?**
Onslaught released on 2002-10-07, and this list looks at how its cards are used in Commander decks well over twenty years later.

**What does this list actually measure?**
It ranks the twenty most played Onslaught cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card appears in a higher proportion of decks. It is a measure of play frequency, not a measure of price or quality.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears once on the list regardless of how many times Onslaught printed it. Its rank position uses the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank of any of its printings, and its listed price is the highest price among those printings, with any exact price ties between printings noted rather than resolved silently.

**Which card is the most played?**
Polluted Delta is the most played Onslaught card in Commander on this list, sitting at EDHREC rank 36 and priced at AU$179.50.
