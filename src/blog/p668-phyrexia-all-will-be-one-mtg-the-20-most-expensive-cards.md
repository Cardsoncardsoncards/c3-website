Phyrexia: All Will Be One closed out the Phyrexian invasion storyline that ran through Dominaria United and The Brothers' War, and it landed with a compact, mythic-heavy checklist that has kept a handful of cards well ahead of the rest of the set on the secondary market. This list gathers the twenty most expensive cards from the set, priced in Australian dollars, and ranks them from the single highest priced card down to number twenty.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within the set itself, whether as showcase treatments, borderless versions or other variants. Where a card has multiple printings, this list uses only its highest priced version to determine rank, and the other printings are noted separately rather than folded into the ranking. Where two printings of the same card land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than resolved arbitrarily.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at one point in time. Singles markets move constantly with supply, so treat every figure here as a point of reference rather than a fixed value. The card pages on our own site and listings on eBay Australia are both reasonable places to compare what a given printing is actually asking right now.
