Fourth Edition landed on the 1st of April 1995, one of the old core sets that reprinted a broad swathe of early Magic staples for a new wave of players. It has no set symbol on many of its cards, which makes it an odd duck to collect, but it still carries a handful of cards that command real prices decades on. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards in the set by their Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to number twenty.

Every card here has just one printing within Fourth Edition itself, so there is no need to sort out which version of a card counts. Where the methodology does matter is in how the set handles cards with several printings elsewhere: they are ranked by their highest priced printing here, and any exact tie between two printings would be noted rather than quietly resolved. None of the twenty below actually needs that treatment, since each has a single priced printing in this set, but it explains why the ordering works the way it does.

Prices throughout are given in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a fair margin, so treat every figure as current at time of writing rather than fixed. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and the same movement applies wherever you shop.

Cards are ranked by the highest priced printing they have within the set, and if two printings shared an identical price, that tie would be noted rather than resolved arbitrarily. None of the twenty cards here required that treatment, as each has only one priced printing in Fourth Edition.
