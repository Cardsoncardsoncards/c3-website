The Lost Caverns of Ixalan returned Magic players to the jungles and sunken ruins of Ixalan, released on 17 November 2023, and it left behind a Commander card pool that has settled into some clear patterns of play. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander decks, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists relative to the rest of the pool.

Each card on this list appears once, even when the set gave it several printings. Where a card has more than one printing here, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever printing costs the most. When two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over or broken by some invented rule.

Cavern of Souls sits at the top of the list, at EDHREC rank 114, and it's the only card here that comfortably clears rank 200. From there the list stretches down through rares, uncommons, mythics and one common, ending at rank 1620 for Trumpeting Carnosaur. The gap between rank 1 and rank 20 is wide, and it says more about how concentrated Commander play is on a small handful of cards than it does about the total size of the set.

Every price quoted below is a snapshot, not a fixed value. Singles prices move constantly with supply, demand and time since release, so the figures attached to each entry reflect one moment and will drift. Readers in Australia can check current listings through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia.

It ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower rank number means a higher rate of play across tracked decks.

Each card appears once on the list. It is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across its printings, and priced using whichever printing in the set costs the most.

That tie is stated plainly rather than being broken by any arbitrary rule, so readers see the shared figure as it stands.
