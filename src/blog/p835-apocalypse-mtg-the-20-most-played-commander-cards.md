Apocalypse rounded off the Invasion block back on 2001-06-04, closing out one of the more richly multicoloured eras in Magic's history. It is a small set, and it packed in an outsized share of cards that still see Commander tables today, from the cycle of painland duals to a handful of spells that have anchored decks for two decades. This list ranks the twenty most played Apocalypse cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists.

Every card on this list appears once, even if it happened to receive more than one printing within Apocalypse itself. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, we take the best (lowest) EDHREC rank to place it, and price it by whichever of those printings carries the highest price. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card here has a single priced printing in this set, so the maths stays simple this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot in time. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat the figures here as a point of reference rather than a permanent fact. You can shop for these cards through the card pages on our own site or via eBay Australia, and it's worth checking both before settling on a copy.

The order below runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20.
