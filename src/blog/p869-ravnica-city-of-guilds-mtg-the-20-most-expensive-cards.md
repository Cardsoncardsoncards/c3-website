Ravnica: City of Guilds landed on 7 October 2005 as the set that introduced the ten-guild structure to Magic: The Gathering, and it remains one of the more collected blocks from that era. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20, using single copies rather than sealed product.

Every card in this set that carries a price only has one priced printing, so there is no case here of choosing between multiple versions of the same card. Where prices happen to land close together between neighbouring entries, that closeness is simply how the numbers fall, not a tie that needed resolving.

Prices throughout are shown exactly as recorded at time of writing, down to the cent. Singles markets shift with supply and demand, so treat this as a snapshot rather than a fixed reference, and expect the figures to move over the coming weeks and months.
