Conflux released on 6 February 2009 as the third and final set in the Alara block, a small release that punched well above its weight in terms of Commander playability. It gave the format a handful of enduring staples, several of the block's signature five-colour and multicolour tools, and a good number of role-players that still see play in decks built around the wedges and shards the block introduced.

This list orders the twenty most played Conflux cards in Commander by EDHREC rank, from number 1 down to number 20, with a lower rank meaning the card shows up in more decks. Every card here has a single printing within the set, so there are no ties to note and no secondary printings to weigh against the headline price, the price you see above each entry is simply that card's own single listed price.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets shift with supply, demand and reprints elsewhere, so the figures attached to each card reflect a point in time rather than a fixed value. Where a comparison is made between cards on this list, it is drawn only from the figures given, nothing here is an estimate or a rounding.

Readers looking to buy any of these singles can do so through the card pages on our own site, or through eBay Australia, both of which carry current listings for Conflux cards at a range of conditions and price points.
