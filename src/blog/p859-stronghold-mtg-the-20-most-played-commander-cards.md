Stronghold is one of the older Magic: The Gathering sets on record, first released back on 1998-03-02, but its cards still show up in Commander decks today. This list takes the twenty Stronghold cards most played in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card appears in more decks. It is not a list of the most expensive cards from the set, though price and play rate do sometimes line up.

Each card on the list appears once, even if it happens to have more than one printing within Stronghold. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and priced using whichever printing carries the highest price. Any case where two printings land on exactly the same price is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card here has a single printing carrying a price in our data, so this mostly matters as a rule for how the list was built rather than something visible in the numbers below.

Prices are listed in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as accurate at the time it was pulled rather than a permanent number. Australian buyers can find these cards through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking both before buying.

What follows is the ranking from number 1, the most played Stronghold card in Commander, down to number 20.

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Stronghold released?**
Stronghold released on 1998-03-02, making it one of the older sets covered on the site.

**What does this list measure?**
It ranks the twenty Stronghold cards most played in Commander decks according to EDHREC rank, where a lower rank number means a card is played more often.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears only once on the list. If it has several printings within Stronghold, its rank uses the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank among those printings, and its price uses the highest priced printing, with any exact price ties noted rather than resolved silently.

**Which card is the most played Stronghold card in Commander?**
Mox Diamond is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 246 with a price of AU$1629.89.

**Are the prices on this list fixed?**

**Does a higher price always mean a card is more played?**
No. Volrath's Stronghold, for example, carries a high price but ranks well down the EDHREC order compared to cheaper cards like Grave Pact or Burgeoning.
