Dark Ascension arrived on 3 February 2012 as the second set in the Innistrad block, a small but dense release that leaned hard into the block's gothic horror themes of zombies, vampires and grim tribal payoffs. It never had the raw card count of a modern set, but its Commander footprint has proven surprisingly durable, with several cards from it still showing up in decks built more than a decade later. This list ranks the 20 most played Dark Ascension cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play across the format.

Every card here appears only once on the list, even where Dark Ascension gave it more than one printing. When a card has multiple printings within the set, we use its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank to place it, and we price it using whichever of its printings carries the highest price. Where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it turns out, every card on this particular list has just one priced printing in the set, so the methodology is simple in practice even if the rule itself has to account for messier cases.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so the figures attached to each entry reflect a point in time rather than a fixed value. Buy through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and expect the numbers to drift as demand and supply shift after this post goes up.
