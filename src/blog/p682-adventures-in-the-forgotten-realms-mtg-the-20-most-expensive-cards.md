Adventures in the Forgotten Realms brought Dungeons & Dragons into Magic: The Gathering when it landed on the 23rd of July, 2021, mixing familiar creature types with a set of characters and locations pulled straight from the tabletop game. It was the first full crossover set of its kind for Magic, and its cards have carried varying degrees of collector interest in the years since release. This list takes the 20 most expensive cards in the set, by Australian dollar price, and ranks them from number 1 down to number 20.

Where a card has more than one printing within Adventures in the Forgotten Realms, the ranking uses the highest priced version to set its position on the list, and the other printing's price is noted alongside it. If two printings of the same card happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than resolved by some arbitrary tiebreaker. A handful of cards on this list carry only a single priced printing, and that is noted too.

Every price below is a snapshot. Magic singles move in both directions over time, based on print runs, supply reaching secondary markets and plenty of other factors, so the figures here reflect a moment rather than a fixed value.
