Innistrad: Midnight Hunt landed on the 24th of September, 2021, and gave Commander players a gothic horror toolbox full of double-faced cards, graveyard shenanigans and a full cycle of slow lands. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander decks, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decklists. It is a snapshot of play patterns, not a value guide, though we have paired each card with its current pricing for context.

Several cards in this set were printed more than once within Midnight Hunt itself, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other variants. Where that happens, the card is listed once, ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. Where two printings happen to share the same price exactly, that tie is noted rather than quietly smoothed over.

Prices are given in Australian dollars and reflect the market at the time of writing. Singles markets move constantly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number, and check current listings on our own card pages or on eBay Australia before buying.
