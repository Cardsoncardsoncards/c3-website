Avatar: The Last Airbender is Magic: The Gathering's set built around the beloved animated series, released on 21 November 2025. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set in Australian dollars, ordered from number 1, the priciest card, down to number 20.

Where a card has more than one printing in the set, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, with the other printings listed alongside it for comparison. Where two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved. Some cards have printings without a listed price at all, which is called out where it applies.

Prices in this list are a snapshot and will move as the market for the set settles in, so treat the figures as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy or sell singles in Australia can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia.
