Outlaws of Thunder Junction landed on 19 April 2024, bringing a heist-flavoured Western setting to Magic: The Gathering along with a fresh batch of cards that Commander players have since folded into their decks in large numbers. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. It is not a value list or a power ranking, it is a play-frequency snapshot drawn straight from EDHREC's data.

Some cards in this set were printed more than once, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other variants. Where that happened, we ranked the card by its single best (lowest) EDHREC rank across all its printings, and priced it by whichever printing carries the highest price. Any other printings are noted separately, and where two printings happen to share an identical price, that coincidence is called out rather than smoothed over.

Every price here is in Australian dollars and reflects a point in time. Singles markets move constantly, so treat the figures as a snapshot rather than a permanent record. You can check current listings and buy singles through our own card pages or through eBay Australia if you want to see how things have shifted since this was written.
