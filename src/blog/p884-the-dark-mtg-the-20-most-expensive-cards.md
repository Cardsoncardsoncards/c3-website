The Dark is one of Magic: The Gathering's earliest expansions, released on 1994-08-01, and it remains one of the more thinly printed sets from the game's first years. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current price in Australian dollars, running from number 1 down to number 20 as stated once here and not repeated through the rest of the piece.

Every card on this list has a single priced printing in The Dark, so no headline figure here is the result of choosing between multiple versions. Where a set does carry more than one printing of a card, our methodology takes the highest priced version as the headline figure, and any shared price between two printings is noted as a tie rather than silently resolved. That situation does not arise anywhere in this particular twenty.

Goblin Wizard sits at the top of the list at AU$138.66, the single most expensive card from The Dark by this measure. The rest of the field descends from there, with the gap between neighbouring cards narrowing considerably by the time the list reaches its lower half.

Prices printed here are a snapshot and will move over time as market conditions change. Readers looking to buy or sell singles from The Dark can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia.
