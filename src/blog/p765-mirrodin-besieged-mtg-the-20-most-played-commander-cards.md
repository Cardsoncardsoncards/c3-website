Mirrodin Besieged landed on the 4th of February 2011, the middle chapter of the Scars of Mirrodin block, and it remains a set that Commander players still dip into for a specific run of efficient, mechanically dense cards. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card shows up in more decks. It is not a value list and it is not a power ranking, it is simply a record of what gets played.

Every card in this set happens to have a single printing, so the methodology note about multiple printings and tied prices is mostly academic here. Where our data notes state that ties would be flagged rather than silently broken, that rule simply never gets triggered across these twenty entries, since each card carries one price from one printing.

Prices below are quoted exactly as recorded at the time of writing, in Australian dollars, right down to the cent. Card prices move constantly with supply, demand and the broader market, so treat every figure here as a snapshot rather than a permanent number. For current pricing, always check the live listing on the card page.

The order below runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20. Singles for anything on this list can be sourced through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, depending on what condition and printing a buyer is after.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Mirrodin Besieged release?**
Mirrodin Besieged released on the 4th of February 2011, as the second set in the Scars of Mirrodin block.

**Which card is the most played from this set in Commander?**
Go for the Throat is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 370 and priced at AU$0.82.

**How does this list handle cards with multiple printings in the set?**
Each card is ranked once by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across its printings, and priced by whichever printing carries the highest price. Where two printings share an exact price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently, though every card on this particular list has only one printing so the case never arises here.

**Where can Australian buyers purchase these singles?**

**Are the prices in this list fixed?**
No, every price quoted is a snapshot taken at time of writing. Card prices move over time with market conditions, so figures should be checked against the live card page before buying.

**What does EDHREC rank actually measure?**
It reflects how often a card appears in Commander decklists tracked by EDHREC, with a lower rank number meaning the card is used more widely.

**Is this list a measure of card value or power level?**
No, it measures play frequency in Commander decks only. Price and rarity are reported alongside it, but the ranking itself is based purely on how often each card is played.
