Odyssey landed back on 1 October 2001, and more than two decades on it still supplies a handful of cards that show up regularly in Commander decks today. This list ranks the twenty most played Odyssey cards in the format using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card appears in more decks. It runs from the single most played card at spot one down to twentieth place.

Each card on this list appears only once, even if Odyssey printed it more than once. Where a card has several printings in the set, we ranked it using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced it using its highest priced printing. If two printings happened to share an identical price, that tie is called out explicitly rather than quietly picking one over the other. As it happens, every card here has a single priced printing, so there is no ambiguity to resolve this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, sometimes sharply, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value. For current pricing and to buy, our own card pages and eBay Australia are the two places most Australian collectors will end up looking.
