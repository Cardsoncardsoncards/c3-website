Marvel Super Heroes brought a fresh wave of Magic: The Gathering cards built around Marvel's roster into the hands of Australian collectors, and this list rounds up the twenty priciest singles from the set at the time of writing. It released on the 26th of June, 2026, and has already settled into a clear price hierarchy topped by a single mythic that sits well ahead of the rest of the field.

The ranking here works on a simple rule: each card counts once, no matter how many times it was printed within Marvel Super Heroes. When a card has multiple printings, only its highest priced version determines its spot on the list, and the other printings are noted alongside it rather than folded into the ranking. Where two printings of the same card land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a fixed record. Singles markets move week to week, and a card sitting at position twelve today could easily shift by the time you read this. Readers chasing any of these singles can look through our own card pages or check listings on eBay Australia, where both prices and availability will vary from what is shown here.
