Lorwyn arrived on 12 October 2007, introducing tribal themes and a memorable cast of kithkin, elves, merfolk and treefolk that still echo through Commander decks today. This list ranks the twenty most played Lorwyn cards in that format, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decks. It is a play-frequency list, not a price list, though we print the current price alongside each entry for context.

Every card in this set happens to have a single tracked printing, so there is no best-of-several-prints sorting to explain and no tie to note this time around. Where the methodology matters more is in how it would handle cards with multiple printings, ranking by the lowest EDHREC rank among them and pricing by the highest, with any exact price match between two printings flagged rather than quietly collapsed. That rule simply had nothing to bite on here.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, sometimes sharply, so treat every figure here as accurate for the moment it was recorded rather than a permanent number. Readers in Australia can pick up copies through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, comparing listings before buying.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Lorwyn release?**
Lorwyn released on 12 October 2007, and it has been available for collectors and players ever since.

**What does this list measure?**
It ranks the twenty most played Lorwyn cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower rank number means the card appears in more decks.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
A card with several printings is ranked by whichever printing has the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest-priced printing. If two printings happen to share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently, though none of the cards on this particular list needed that treatment.

**Which card is the most played Lorwyn card in Commander?**
Ponder is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 143, well ahead of every other card on this list.

**Where can Australian buyers pick up these singles?**
Readers can buy through the card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia.

**Are the prices in this post fixed?**
No. Every price quoted is a snapshot in Australian dollars and singles prices move over time, so figures should be treated as current only at the time they were recorded.

**Does a higher price mean a card is played more?**
Not necessarily. Several cards further down this list carry higher prices than cards ranked above them, showing that price and play frequency do not always track each other.
