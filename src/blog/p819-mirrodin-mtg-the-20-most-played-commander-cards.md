Mirrodin arrived on 2003-10-02 and left one of the deepest marks on Commander of any early-2000s set, thanks to its artifact-heavy design and a run of cheap, efficient permanents that slot into almost any deck regardless of colour. This post ranks the 20 most played cards from the set in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists relative to the rest of the card pool. The list runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20.

Every card here appears only once in the countdown even if it was printed more than once within Mirrodin. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, its position is determined by the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and the price shown is the highest of the available printing prices. If two printings happened to share an identical price, that would be flagged as a tie rather than quietly resolved one way or the other, though as it happens every card on this particular list has only a single priced printing in Mirrodin.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it's worth checking current listings before assuming any number below still holds.

The cards that follow range from bulk-common artifact lands through to one of the set's few genuinely expensive chase pieces, and the spread between the cheapest and priciest entries says a fair bit about how differently the market has treated cards that see roughly comparable play.
