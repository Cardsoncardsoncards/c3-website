Future Sight closed out the Time Spiral block when it released on 4 May 2007, and it remains one of the more curious sets in Magic's history thanks to its future-shifted card frames and its clutch of cards that hinted at mechanics still years away from proper release. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by current price in Australian dollars, from the most costly at the top down to the twentieth entry at the bottom of the list.

Every card in this ranking appears only once, even where a card had more than one printing within the set. When that happens, the card is placed according to whichever of its printings carries the highest price, and the other printing's figure is shown alongside it rather than folded away. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out plainly instead of being broken by some invisible rule. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just a single priced printing in Future Sight, so the methodology's tie-handling and multi-printing rules exist mainly for completeness this time round.

Prices shown here are a snapshot only. Magic singles move constantly with supply, demand and the wider market for older cards, so the figures attached to each entry reflect one point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy any of these cards can do so through the card pages on our own site or via eBay Australia, both of which will reflect more current pricing than a static list ever can.

The spread across these twenty cards is wide. Sliver Legion sits well clear of the field at the top, while the bottom half of the list clusters tightly together within a couple of dollars of each other, which says something about how concentrated collector interest in this set becomes once you get past its headline names.
