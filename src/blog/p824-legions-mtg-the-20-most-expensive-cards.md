Legions arrived on the third of February, 2003, as a small-set follow up that leaned hard into tribal themes, with sliver creatures and single colour cards making up the bulk of the checklist. It is a compact set, without a booster draft's usual spread of splashy mythics, so the price list at the top is dominated by a handful of rares that have held collector interest over two decades. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards in Legions by Australian dollar price, from the priciest single card down to the twentieth spot on the list.

Every card here appears once in the ranking, even in the rare case where a card had more than one printing in the set. Where a card carries multiple printings, the ranking uses the highest priced version to place it on the list, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out in the text above the blurb rather than being broken arbitrarily. In this particular set, as it happens, every one of the twenty cards below has a single priced printing, so there is no tie-breaking or printing comparison to walk through card by card.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week and sometimes day to day, so treat every figure here as accurate at time of writing rather than a fixed number. The card pages on our own site and listings on eBay Australia are both reasonable places to check a current asking price against what is printed here.

The list below runs from Seedborn Muse at number one, the most expensive card in the set, down through a run of sliver creatures, muse cycle cards and a few standalone rares, finishing at Drinker of Sorrow in twentieth place.
