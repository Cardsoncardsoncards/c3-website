Mirage arrived on 8 October 1996, the first set in a block that pushed Magic's storyline into new territory with the Order of the Ebon Hand, Jamuraa, and the return of Feroz's Ban. It has since become one of the more curious corners of the game's back catalogue, sitting between the Alliances era and the Visions expansion that followed it. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, from the priciest at number one down to number twenty.

Each card in this ranking appears only once, even if it saw more than one printing within Mirage. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked according to whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings happened to share an identical price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or another. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just one priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or secondary printings to weigh up here.

Prices throughout are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at one point in time. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so the figures attached to each entry below should be read as a record of where things stood rather than a permanent fact about the card.
