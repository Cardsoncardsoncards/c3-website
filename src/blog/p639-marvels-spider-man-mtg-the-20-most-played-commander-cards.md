Marvel's Spider-Man landed on the 26th of September 2025, bringing Peter Parker, his allies and his rogues gallery into Magic's card pool. This list looks at the 20 cards from that set with the most play in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure. A lower rank number means a card is showing up in more decklists, so the card at rank 1 on this list is the one Commander players are actually putting to work most often.

Some cards in this set were printed more than once, whether as showcase treatments, borderless versions or other variants. Where that happens, we rank the card by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across all its printings, and price it using whichever printing carries the highest price tag. When two printings land on exactly the same price, we say so rather than quietly picking one, since collapsing that into a single number would hide information a buyer might want.

Prices in this list are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move day to day, so treat every figure here as accurate at time of writing rather than a permanent number. If you are shopping locally, our own card pages and eBay Australia are both worth checking against whatever figure is quoted below.

What follows is the countdown from 20 up to the single most played card in the set, with a short note on each entry covering how it sits against its neighbours on the list rather than repeating the numbers already printed above it.

**How is this list ranked?**
Cards are ordered by EDHREC rank, from lowest to highest, with a lower number meaning more play in Commander decks.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears only once on the list. It is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across all printings, and priced using its highest priced printing. Where two printings share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently.

No, rarity is noted for each card but has no bearing on its position, which is determined purely by EDHREC rank.
