Magic 2015 was the last of Wizards of the Coast's annual Core Set releases, landing on 18 July 2014 with a mix of returning planeswalkers, sliver tribal support and reprint staples. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest single card down to the twentieth.

Every card in the set is eligible only once. Where a card had more than one printing within Magic 2015, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings happened to land on exactly the same figure that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card on this particular list has only a single priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or secondary printings to weigh against the headline figure here.

Prices are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week and sometimes day to day, so the figures quoted reflect the data used to build this list and not a permanent value. Readers can check current listings for any of these cards through the card pages on our own site or on eBay Australia.
