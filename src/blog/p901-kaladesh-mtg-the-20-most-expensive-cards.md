Kaladesh arrived on 30 September 2016, bringing Kaladesh block's inventors, artificers and aether-powered machinery to Standard tables and cube lists alike. It remains one of the more fondly remembered sets from that era, home to a run of planeswalkers and artifact support cards that have kept a foothold in Commander decks long after rotation. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to twentieth place.

Every card here is counted once. Where a card has more than one printing within Kaladesh itself, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is noted rather than resolved by some arbitrary tiebreaker. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just a single priced printing in the set, so there is no splitting of figures required this time round, though the methodology stays the same for any future set where that changes.

Prices below are a snapshot only. Singles values shift with supply, with reprints in later products, and with how a card is being used in whatever formats currently value it, so the figures quoted here will not stay fixed. Readers can check current pricing and buy singles through the card pages on our own site, or shop around on eBay Australia.
