Classic Sixth Edition landed in April 1999 as the first core set to reflect the rules overhaul that came with the game's sixth edition update, and it remains a fixture for collectors piecing together late-nineties Magic. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, from Vampiric Tutor at the top down to Goblin King at the foot of the table.

Every card here has a single tracked printing within Classic Sixth Edition, so there is no need to weigh up alternate versions or resolve ties between printings on this occasion. Where a card does carry more than one priced printing in the set, our methodology takes the highest priced version to determine its rank, and any exact tie between two printings is noted rather than quietly folded into one number. That approach keeps the ordering honest even when the underlying pricing data is messy.

Prices below are a snapshot taken at time of writing and expressed in Australian dollars. Singles markets move, sometimes by a little and sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers can chase these cards down through the card pages on our own site or via eBay Australia, both of which will reflect more current pricing than a static list ever can.

Classic Sixth Edition released on 21 April 1999, making it one of the earlier core sets in Magic's history.

**What is the most expensive card in Classic Sixth Edition?**

**How does this list handle cards with multiple printings in the set?**
Cards are ranked by their highest priced printing in Classic Sixth Edition, and if two printings share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than resolved arbitrarily. None of the twenty cards on this list had more than one priced printing, so no ties needed resolving here.
