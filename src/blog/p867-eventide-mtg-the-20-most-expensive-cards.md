Eventide arrived on 25 July 2008 as the second half of the Shadowmoor block, a set built around hybrid mana and multicoloured strategies that pushed two-colour design into unusual corners. It's a small, dense set, and its price list today is a useful snapshot of which cards from that era have held collector interest and which have settled into bulk territory. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards in Eventide by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest single card down to the twentieth.

Every card here appears once on the list even if it has more than one printing within the set. Where a card has multiple printings, we rank it by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings land on exactly the same figure, we say so rather than pretending one edges out the other. Most of the cards below only ever had one printing in Eventide, so the headline price is simply the price of that single version.

Prices throughout are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week and sometimes day to day, so treat every figure here as accurate at time of writing rather than a fixed number. As always, the amounts printed directly above each entry are the ones to trust; anything in the prose is comparison rather than a fresh quote.
