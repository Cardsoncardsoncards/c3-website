Scourge is the third and final set in the Onslaught block, released on 2003-05-26. It is a small set by modern standards, but it left a surprising mark on Commander, contributing tribal payoffs, ritual effects and a handful of cards that have become staples in specific archetypes. This list ranks the 20 most played Scourge cards in Commander decks today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower rank means the card shows up in more decklists.

Every card on this list appears exactly once, even where a card had more than one printing within Scourge. In those cases the ranking uses the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across its printings, and the price shown is the highest priced printing of the card. Where two printings land on the same price exactly, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. As it happens, none of the 20 cards below have more than one priced printing in this set, so every entry is a single line, single price affair.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value. Readers looking to buy can check card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia for comparison.

**How is this list ranked?**
Cards are ordered by EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card appears in more Commander decks, from the most played at number 1 down to number 20.

Each card appears only once on the list. It is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across its Scourge printings, and priced by whichever of those printings is highest, with exact price ties between printings noted rather than resolved silently.
