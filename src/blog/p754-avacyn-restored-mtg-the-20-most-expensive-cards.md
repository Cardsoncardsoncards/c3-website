Avacyn Restored closed out the Innistrad block with a return of the Angel herself, and it landed on shelves on the 4th of May, 2012. It's a set built around big flashy mythics and angel tribal payoffs, and that shows up clearly in the price list below, where mythics occupy a good chunk of the top half. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by their Australian dollar price today.

Every card here is listed once. Where a card has more than one printing within Avacyn Restored, we rank it by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie gets called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just a single priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or secondary printings to weigh up this time round.

Prices below are snapshots taken in Australian dollars and they move, sometimes by a fair bit, as supply and interest shift over time. Treat the figures as a point-in-time read rather than something fixed. For buying and selling singles, our own card pages and eBay Australia are the two places most readers use to check current listings.
