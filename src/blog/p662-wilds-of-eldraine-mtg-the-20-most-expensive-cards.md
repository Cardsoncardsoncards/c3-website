Wilds of Eldraine landed on 8 September 2023, bringing a cottagecore fairytale spin to Magic: The Gathering with a set full of adventurous villagers, fae bargains and mythic-heavy chase slots. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by Australian dollar price, drawing on both the mainline and the Eldraine bonus material where relevant.

For cards with more than one printing in the set, whether that's a showcase frame, a borderless treatment or another variant, the card is ranked by whichever of its printings carries the highest price. The other printing's price is noted alongside it rather than dropped, and where two printings land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out plainly instead of being broken by some arbitrary rule.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, sometimes card to card, so treat every figure here as accurate at time of writing rather than fixed. Readers in Australia can check current listings on our own card pages or on eBay Australia if they want to compare against what's shown below.
