Aetherdrift launched on 14 February 2025, and its Commander footprint has settled into a shape that will feel familiar to anyone who has watched a set's dust settle on EDHREC before. This list pulls the 20 most played cards from the set, ordered by EDHREC rank from number 1 down to number 20, where a lower rank means a card turns up in more decklists.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Aetherdrift itself, most often as a common land alongside a rarer variant. Where that happens, the card is ranked once using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of its printings costs the most. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly smoothed over.

The result is a spread that runs from cards worth well under a dollar to rares sitting well into double figures, with played frequency and price pulling in almost opposite directions for most of the list. Prices are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only; they move constantly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number.
