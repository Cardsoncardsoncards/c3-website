Avacyn Restored landed on the 4th of May, 2012, closing out the Innistrad block with a set built around flip cards, angels and the reborn Avacyn herself. It is a set that punched well above its weight in Commander circles, and this list ranks the twenty Avacyn Restored cards most played in the format today, using EDHREC rank as the measure. A lower EDHREC rank means a card turns up in more decks, so the card at number one on this list is the one Commander players reach for most often.

Every card here appears once, even where the set gave it more than one printing. Where a card had multiple printings within Avacyn Restored, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the best EDHREC number, and priced using its highest priced printing. Had any two printings landed on exactly the same price, that tie would be flagged rather than glossed over, though as it happens every card on this list has a single priced printing.

Cavern of Souls sits at the top, ranked 114 on EDHREC and priced at AU$75.59, well clear of everything below it. From there the list runs down through a mix of rares, mythics, uncommons and commons, a reminder that played rate in Commander and rarity on the card frame do not always move together.

Prices below are a snapshot taken at time of writing. Singles markets shift constantly, so treat every figure as a point in time rather than a fixed number, and check current listings before buying.
