Innistrad: Crimson Vow closed out 2021's double dose of gothic horror, following straight on from Innistrad: Midnight Hunt and leaning further into vampires, coffins and Gothic wedding drama. It released on 19 November 2021 and has had a few years now to settle into the secondary market, which is what this list is drawn from. What follows is a ranking of the 20 most expensive cards in the set by current Australian dollar price.

Where a card has multiple printings within Crimson Vow itself, such as a borderless or showcase treatment alongside the regular version, we rank it by whichever printing is priced highest and note the other printings underneath. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. This means a card's position on the list reflects its best-selling version, not an average.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat the figures here as a point in time rather than a fixed reference. The card data and the numbers quoted are accurate to the cent at the time this was written.

The list runs from number 1, the single most expensive card in the set, down to number 20. Toxrill, the Corrosive sits at the top.
