Time Spiral is the set that Wizards of the Coast built as a love letter to Magic's own history, first released on the 6th of October, 2006. It leaned hard into nostalgia, drawing mechanics and flavour from across the game's past, and that reputation has kept a steady collector interest in its cards ever since. This list runs through the 20 most expensive cards from the set, priced in Australian dollars.

Every card here is ranked once, using its highest priced printing where a card appears more than once in the set. In cases where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than smoothed over or broken by some arbitrary tiebreaker. As it happens, every card on this particular list has only a single priced printing in Time Spiral, so the ranking is a straight read of one number per card.

Gemstone Caverns sits at the top of the list at AU$72.43, clear of everything below it. From there the list runs down through a mix of rares, an uncommon or two, and a single common, ending at Paradox Haze at AU$5.57. Worth remembering throughout is that these are snapshot prices. Singles markets move, and the figures here reflect where things stood at time of writing, not a fixed value.

Whether you're filling a gap in a Time Spiral collection or just curious how the set's value is distributed, the list below gives a card by card look at where the money sits, and how far the gaps stretch between neighbours on the ladder.
