Phyrexia: All Will Be One landed on 10 February 2023 as the second half of the Phyrexian saga that began with Kamigawa: Neon Dynasty and closed out the March of the Machine storyline. It is a compact set, but a heavy hitter in Commander circles, giving the format a stack of compleated legends, new utility lands and a handful of cheap, efficient support cards that found their way into decks well beyond the mythic mirrors.

This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. Terramorphic Expanse takes the top spot by a wide margin over the rest of the field, a reminder that unglamorous fixing often outpaces flashier mythics in raw play count.

Several cards in this set have multiple printings, and the methodology treats each card as a single entry regardless of how many versions exist. Where a card has more than one printing, it is ranked by its lowest EDHREC rank and priced by whichever printing carries the highest price tag, with any other printings listed alongside it. When two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved arbitrarily.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot in time. Singles markets move constantly, so treat every figure here as a point of reference rather than a fixed value.

The tie is stated explicitly rather than broken by any arbitrary rule, as with Thrummingbird, where two printings share the same price.
