Guilds of Ravnica returned Magic to its guild-driven city plane in 2018, leaning into ten factions across two sets, and the singles market has settled into a familiar shape years on from release. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from the priciest at number one down to the twentieth entry, giving readers a snapshot of where collector value sits within this particular print run.

Every card in this list happens to carry a single priced printing in Guilds of Ravnica, so the methodology is simple in practice even though the rule behind it is not: where a card has more than one printing, the highest priced version determines its rank, and any exact tie between printings is noted rather than quietly resolved. That rule matters more for sets with multiple treatments of the same card, but it is stated here so the ranking logic is transparent regardless of what the individual entries turned out to need.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets shift with supply, reprints elsewhere, and general demand for the plane's guild mechanics, so a figure quoted today will not be exact in six months. Readers looking to buy or sell can check card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia, both of which will reflect more current pricing than a fixed list ever can.

The spread across these twenty cards runs from Steam Vents at the top down to March of the Multitudes at the foot of the list, covering shockland reprints, mythic planeswalkers, and a handful of build-around rares that have held modest but persistent value. What follows is a rundown of each entry in order, with notes on how it sits relative to its neighbours.
