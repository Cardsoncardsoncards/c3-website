Magic 2012 was the core set released on 15 July 2011, one of the last of Wizards of the Coast's annual "M-series" core sets before the format shifted again in later years. It brought back a run of iconic reprints alongside new mythic designs, and more than a decade on it still turns up regularly in Australian singles listings. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20.

Every card here is counted once. Where a card has more than one printing within Magic 2012, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price, and any exact tie between two printings is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card on this particular list has a single priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or secondary prices to weigh up this time round.

Prices are given in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week and sometimes day to day, so treat every figure here as current at time of writing rather than fixed. ## Frequently asked questions

**When was Magic 2012 released?**
Magic 2012 released on 15 July 2011 as a core set, and it has been fully released for some time, so every card on this list is a legacy printing rather than a current product.

**How does this list handle cards with multiple printings in the set?**
Each card is counted once and ranked by its highest priced printing within Magic 2012. Where two printings share an identical price, that tie is noted explicitly rather than being broken arbitrarily, though on this particular list every card had just one priced printing.

**Which card is the most expensive in Magic 2012 by this measure?**
Grand Abolisher is the most expensive card on the list, priced at AU$28.76, ahead of Sundial of the Infinite at AU$20.11.
