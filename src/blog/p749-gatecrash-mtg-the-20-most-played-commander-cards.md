Gatecrash, released back on 1 February 2013, was the second set in Ravnica's second guild block, and it left Commander tables with a small pile of enduring shock lands, a couple of guild-defining legends, and a stack of role-players that have quietly stuck around ever since. This list ranks the 20 most played cards from the set in Commander, ordered by EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. Watery Grave sits at the top of that list, and from there the rankings spread out across dual lands, guild mechanics payoffs and a few bulk-bin commons that still see play because they do a job cheaply.

Each card on this list appears once, even if Gatecrash printed it more than once. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, it is ranked by whichever printing has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or another. As it happens, every card below has just a single priced printing in this set, so there is no tie to report and no gap between printings to discuss.

Prices are listed in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a fixed value. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as accurate at the time it was recorded rather than a permanent number. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on this site or through eBay Australia, both of which carry current listings for Gatecrash singles across the rarity spread seen here.
