Urza's Saga is the 1998 expansion that opened the Urza block, a set now remembered as much for its power level as for the artifact-and-land shenanigans it introduced. It has gone on to become one of the most heavily mined sets in Magic's history for cards that still see play in Vintage, Legacy and Commander, which is reflected in the spread of prices below. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their price in Australian dollars, running from number 1 down to number 20.

Where a card has more than one printing within Urza's Saga, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price, and any printings that share an identical price are noted as a tie rather than arbitrarily separated. In this particular set, every card on the list happens to have a single priced printing, so there are no ties or secondary prices to weigh up. That keeps the comparisons simple, but it is worth remembering for other sets where multiple printings are common.

Every figure here is a snapshot. Magic singles prices move constantly with supply, demand and the broader trading card market, so the exact numbers quoted will drift over time even if the relative order holds for a while. Treat this as a picture of where things stand now rather than a fixed reference.

Readers in Australia can find these cards through the card pages on our own site, and through eBay Australia, both of which are worth checking against each other before buying.
