Khans of Tarkir landed on 26 September 2014, and it remains one of the more fondly remembered Limited environments Wizards has produced, thanks largely to its three-colour wedge identity and a fetchland cycle that never quite goes away in price conversations. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20.

Each card appears once on this list. Where a card has several printings within Khans of Tarkir itself, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and any printings that share an identical price are noted as a tie rather than arbitrarily separated. As it happens, every card below has only a single priced printing in this set, so there is no splitting of variants to worry about here, just one number per card.

Prices are shown in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, and a figure recorded today will drift as supply and demand shift over the coming months. Treat the ordering and the numbers as a picture of the market at the time this was written, not a permanent ranking.
