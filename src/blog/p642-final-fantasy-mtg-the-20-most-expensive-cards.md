Final Fantasy is Magic: The Gathering's crossover set with Square Enix's long running RPG series, released on 13 June 2025. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set in Australian dollars, drawing on singles pricing across every printing available. Where a card appears in the set more than once, only its highest priced printing decides its place on the list, with the prices of its other printings noted alongside for comparison.

Several cards on this list share printing counts or sit close together in price, and where two printings of the same card land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or another. The gap between a card's most and least expensive printing varies a great deal across this list, and that gap is often the more interesting number than the headline price itself.

Prices throughout are snapshots. Singles markets move, and a figure recorded today can shift within weeks as supply and interest in the set settles. Treat the numbers here as a point in time rather than a fixed reading.
