Magic 2010 was released on 17 July 2009, one of the core sets that helped define the "M10" era of streamlined templating and singleton mythic rares. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, running from number 1 down to number 20 as stated once here and not repeated through the list.

Each card appears only once on this list. Where a card has more than one printing within Magic 2010, it is ranked according to whichever of those printings carries the higher price, and any case where two printings land on the exact same figure is called out as a tie rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. As it happens, every card in this particular twenty has a single priced printing in the set, so the headline figure above each entry is the only number in play for that card.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, so the figures here reflect a point in time rather than a fixed value, and readers should expect some drift if they check back later.
