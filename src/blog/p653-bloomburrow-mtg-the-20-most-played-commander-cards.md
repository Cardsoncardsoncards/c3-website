Bloomburrow arrived on 2 August 2024 as one of the more distinctive Magic: The Gathering sets in recent memory, built around a cast of animal folk and a strong emphasis on tribal synergies. That flavour translated quickly into Commander decks, and this list uses EDHREC rank to measure exactly that: how often each card turns up in published Commander deck lists, with a lower rank meaning more frequent play.

The twenty cards below are ordered from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20. Where a card has several printings within Bloomburrow, we rank it by its single best (lowest) EDHREC rank and price it by whichever of those printings is currently most expensive. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved.

Every price you see is a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at time of writing. Card markets move, sometimes within days, so treat these figures as a point of reference rather than a guarantee of what you will see when you go looking for a copy.

Cards are ranked from number 1 down to number 20 by EDHREC rank, with a lower rank meaning more played.
