Dragons of Tarkir landed on 27 March 2015, closing out the Khans of Tarkir block with a full tilt into dragon tribal and multicolour value. A decade on, it remains a set that Commander players return to, less for splashy mythics and more for the unglamorous roleplayers that keep decks running. This list ranks the 20 most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play across decks.

Every card in this set happens to have a single tracked printing, so the methodology mentioned here mostly runs on rails: rank comes from EDHREC's own figures, and price is taken straight from that one printing. Where our wider methodology allows for multiple printings of a card, we would price by the highest of them and note any exact ties rather than quietly picking a winner. For this particular set, that scenario simply didn't come up.

Prices are listed in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at time of writing. Singles markets shift constantly with supply, demand and reprints elsewhere, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value. The order below runs from the single most played card in the set down to the twentieth, purely on played frequency rather than price.
