Revised Edition landed on 11 April 1994 as the third core set for Magic: The Gathering, and it remains one of the most traded printings from the game's earliest years. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20, using the highest priced printing available for each card. Where a card has more than one printing in the set, only its top figure decides its rank, and any exact price ties between printings are called out rather than quietly smoothed over.

The list is dominated early by the original dual lands, a reflection of how few printings this era of Magic ever received rather than anything about their current legality or popularity. Further down, the list turns to a mix of well known rares and a couple of uncommons that have held value out of step with their printed rarity. Every card here is drawn from a single Revised Edition printing with one recorded price, so the comparisons below are between different cards on the list rather than between reprints of the same card.

Prices in this piece are a snapshot only. Singles pricing for a card this old moves with condition, supply and the broader market for older Magic cards, so the figures quoted here will drift over time even if the relative order of the list holds steady for a while.

The methodology notes that tie rather than resolving it silently, though none of the twenty cards here had more than one priced printing to compare.
