Tenth Edition landed back on 2007-07-13 as one of Magic's core sets, a straight reprint collection with no new cards but plenty of staples that went on to have long lives in Commander. This post ranks the 20 cards from that set most played in the format today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card sees more use across decks. The list runs from number 1, the most played, down to number 20.

Because several of these cards were printed more than once within Tenth Edition, the methodology takes each card once only. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, it is ranked by whichever printing has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever printing carries the highest price. If two printings happen to land on the exact same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars, and singles markets move day to day, so treat these figures as a point in time rather than a fixed number. Readers can chase down copies through the card pages on our own site or by browsing eBay Australia, where Tenth Edition stock tends to circulate in both bulk and graded lots.

Tenth Edition released on 2007-07-13.

**Which card from Tenth Edition is the most played in Commander according to this list?**

**How does the ranking handle cards with more than one printing in the set?**
Each card appears only once, ranked using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across its printings and priced using whichever of its printings is highest priced. If two printings share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently.

**Are these prices fixed?**
No, every price listed is a snapshot at a point in time. Singles markets move, so the same card can cost more or less by the time it's checked again.

**Why do some cards with a worse EDHREC rank cost more than cards with a better rank?**

**Does this list include every card printed in Tenth Edition?**
No, it covers only the twenty cards from the set with the strongest (lowest) EDHREC ranks, meaning the twenty most played in Commander.
