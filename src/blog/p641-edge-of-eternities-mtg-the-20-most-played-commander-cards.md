Edge of Eternities released on the 1st of August 2025, and it has already settled into the Commander landscape with a familiar shape: a handful of staple lands and mana fixers sitting well ahead of the flashier mythics further down the pack. This list ranks the 20 most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means more decks are running the card. It is a snapshot of play patterns, not a value guide, and it reads top to bottom from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20.

Several cards in Edge of Eternities have more than one printing, so a simple rule was applied throughout. Where a card appears more than once in the set, it is ranked by whichever printing has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. When two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved. Not every printing carries a listed price, and that is reflected in the printing counts given above each entry.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are quoted exactly as recorded at the time of writing, down to the cent. Singles markets move constantly, and a card sitting at a particular price this week can shift by the time you read this, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. For buying, singles for these cards are available through the card pages on our own site, and through eBay Australia.

What follows is the full list of 20, with the rank, headline price, printing count and other printing prices stated plainly above each entry, and a short note underneath adding context that those figures alone do not cover.
