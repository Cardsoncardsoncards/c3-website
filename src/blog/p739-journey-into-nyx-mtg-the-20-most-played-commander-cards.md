Journey into Nyx closed out the Theros block back on 2014-05-02, wrapping up a story built around gods, devotion and the underworld with a smaller, more focused set than the two that came before it. It is a set that punches well above its size in Commander, thanks to a run of mono-coloured gods, useful enchantment removal and a land that has become a staple across almost every colour combination. This list ranks the twenty most played Journey into Nyx cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks.

Every card here appears once, even though some Journey into Nyx cards have seen multiple printings since 2014. Where a card has more than one printing in this set, it is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using the highest priced of those printings. If two printings happen to share exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot in time. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as accurate for the moment it was recorded rather than a permanent number.

The card immediately above each blurb carries its rank, its price and its printing count, so the write-up below it deals with what those figures imply rather than repeating them.
