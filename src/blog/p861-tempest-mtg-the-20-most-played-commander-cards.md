Tempest first hit shelves back on 14 October 1997, and more than a quarter of a century later it remains one of the more heavily represented sets in Commander decks. This list ranks the 20 Tempest cards most played in the format, ordered from number 1 down to number 20, using EDHREC rank as the measure of play. A lower EDHREC rank means a card shows up in more decks, so Counterspell at rank 16 sits well ahead of Scroll Rack down at rank 1366.

Every card here has just one Tempest printing carrying a price, so there are no ties to resolve and no secondary printings to weigh up this time round. Where a set does throw up multiple printings of the same card, our method takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank to set position, and the highest priced printing for the headline figure, noting explicitly any case where two printings land on the exact same price rather than quietly picking one.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a single snapshot in time. Singles markets move, sometimes by a fair margin, so treat every figure here as a point of reference rather than a fixed value. Readers can source these cards through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking current listings before assuming a price from this list still holds.

Rarity spans common through to rare across this list, and there is no strong pattern tying rarity to play rate here, common staples like Disenchant sit near the bottom while rare mana rocks and utility lands populate the middle and top. What the list does show clearly is a wide spread between the most played card and the twentieth, with a gap of well over a thousand rank positions from top to bottom.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Tempest release?**
Tempest released on 14 October 1997, and the set has been available for the secondary market ever since.

**What does this list measure?**
It ranks the 20 Tempest cards most played in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower rank means the card appears in more decks.

**How are multiple printings and ties handled?**
Each card appears once on the list even if Tempest included more than one printing of it. The card's position uses its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, its headline price is the highest priced printing, and if two printings share a price exactly that tie is noted rather than resolved silently. In this particular list every card has only one priced printing, so no ties or secondary prices appear.

**Which card is the most played?**
Counterspell is the most played Tempest card in Commander, sitting at EDHREC rank 16 and priced at AU$3.33.
