Kaldheim landed on 5 February 2021, bringing a snow-drenched, saga-inspired take on Magic's Norse mythology to tables everywhere. It introduced the Pathway land cycle, foretell as a mechanic, and a run of gods and giants that have since found homes in a wide range of Commander decks. This list looks specifically at how those cards have performed in that format, using EDHREC rank as the measuring stick, where a lower number means a card shows up in more decklists relative to the rest of the card pool.

The methodology behind this ranking is simple to state but worth spelling out. Each card appears exactly once on the list, even if Kaldheim printed it more than once. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, its position is set by the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank of any version, and the price shown as its headline figure is the highest price among those printings. When two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other, so readers can see the flat line for what it is rather than assuming a typo.

Sitting at the top by a wide margin in play terms is Village Rites, the only card here in genuinely disposable common territory. From there, the list runs through a mix of rares, mythics and uncommons, including the four-part cycle of Pathway lands, a couple of gods, and one of the set's headline dragons. Rarity and price do not track play rate in lockstep here, and that gap is part of what makes a played-based ranking different from a straight price list.

Prices throughout are quoted exactly as recorded at the time of writing, in Australian dollars, and they are not static. Singles markets shift with supply, reprints and format trends, so treat every figure below as a snapshot rather than a permanent fact. Readers can shop for these cards through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and comparing both is usually worthwhile before buying.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Kaldheim release?**
Kaldheim released on 5 February 2021, and it has been available in the Commander singles market ever since.

**Which card from Kaldheim is the most played in Commander?**
Village Rites holds that position, with an EDHREC rank of 197, the lowest, and therefore most-played, rank of any card in this list.

**How does this list handle cards with multiple printings?**
Each card appears once, using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across all its Kaldheim printings, and its headline price is the highest price recorded among those printings.

**What happens when two printings of a card share the same price?**
The tie is noted rather than resolved silently. Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider is the one example on this list where two printings share an identical price.
