Prophecy is one of the smaller sets from Magic's early history, released on 5 June 2000 as the final act of the Masques block. It is not a set most players think of as a Commander staple, yet a handful of its cards have found lasting homes in the format, and EDHREC's play-rate data lets us rank exactly which ones. This list runs from the most played card in Commander down to the twentieth, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card sees more play across decks tracked by the site.

Every card here appears once, even if Prophecy printed it more than once. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, we ranked it by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and priced it using its highest priced printing. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, we note the tie rather than quietly picking one over the other. In this particular set, every card on the list happens to have a single priced printing, so the headline figure above each entry is the only price recorded for that card in Prophecy.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Card markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value. Readers in Australia can check current pricing and buy singles through our own card pages or through eBay Australia.

The list below starts with Rhystic Study at rank 44, the only card from Prophecy with meaningful presence near the top of Commander's play patterns, and works down through cards with progressively higher (less played) EDHREC ranks to Keldon Firebombers at number twenty.
