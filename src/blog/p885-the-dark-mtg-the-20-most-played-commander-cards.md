The Dark is one of the oldest sets in Magic's history, released back on 1994-08-01, and it remains a fixture for players and collectors chasing early, atmospheric printings of classic effects. This post ranks the 20 most played cards from the set in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card sees more play across decks on that site. The order runs from number 1 down to number 20, most played to least, and covers a real spread from staples that show up everywhere down to more niche inclusions.

Several cards in The Dark received more than one printing within the set itself. Where that happens, the card is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. If two printings of a card land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted plainly rather than resolved by picking one arbitrarily. This keeps the list honest about what the underlying data actually shows.

Every price quoted here is a snapshot in Australian dollars. Card prices move over time with supply, demand and the broader market, so the figures attached to each entry reflect a moment rather than a fixed value. Readers can pick up singles either through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and both markets will show some drift from what's printed here as time passes.

The Dark released on 1994-08-01, making it one of the earliest Magic: The Gathering expansions.

A card with several printings in The Dark is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing. If two printings share exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than broken arbitrarily.
