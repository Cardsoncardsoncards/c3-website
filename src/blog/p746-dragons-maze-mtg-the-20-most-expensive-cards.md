Dragon's Maze rounded out the Return to Ravnica block when it released on the 3rd of May, 2013, and it remains the smallest set of that trio. It brought each of the ten guild pairings back into the spotlight one more time, with a run of guild leader creatures and a spread of multicoloured spells that has kept the set relevant to Commander tables long after its Standard days ended.

This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from Dragon's Maze by current Australian dollar price, counting down from number one. Every card in the set is counted once. Where a card has more than one printing within the set, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price, and any case where two printings land on exactly the same figure is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just a single priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or multi-printing gaps to work through here.

Prices are a snapshot taken at one point in time and will shift as supply and interest change. Treat every figure here as a reading for the day it was pulled, not a fixed value. If you are buying, check the current listing before you commit.
