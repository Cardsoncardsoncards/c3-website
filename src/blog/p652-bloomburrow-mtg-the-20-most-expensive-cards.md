Bloomburrow is the Magic: The Gathering set that released on the 2nd of August 2024, bringing a cast of anthropomorphic animal creatures across the ten colour pairs. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their Australian dollar price, drawing on our own card pages and eBay Australia listings for reference points.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card has several printings within Bloomburrow itself. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked according to whichever printing carries the highest price, and the other printings for that card are listed alongside it. Where two printings happen to share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

The order below runs from number 1, the single most expensive card in the set, down to number 20. Mythic rarity dominates the upper half of the list, though rares do appear throughout, including a handful of cards with only one printing in the set at all.

Prices are a snapshot and will move over time as supply and secondary market conditions shift. Treat the figures here as a point of reference rather than a fixed value.
