Time Spiral Timeshifted is the companion "timeshifted" set released alongside Time Spiral on 2006-10-06, a purebred nostalgia project built entirely from cards that had never before appeared with the modern card frame. Every card in the set carries the special rarity symbol, a quirk of how Timeshifted was constructed rather than a reflection of individual scarcity, so the usual common-to-mythic ladder does not apply here. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price.

The methodology is simple but worth stating plainly. Each card appears once on this list, even where a card has more than one printing within the set. Where a card does carry multiple printings, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings land on the exact same figure, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card in this particular top twenty has only a single priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or secondary prices to report this time round.

Prices below are a snapshot only. Magic singles move constantly with supply, demand and the broader market, so the figures quoted here reflect the moment this list was compiled and will drift over time. For current pricing and to buy, readers can check the card pages here on Cards on Cards on Cards or search listings on eBay Australia.
