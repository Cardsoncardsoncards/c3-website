Dominaria United landed on 3 February 2023, returning to the plane of Dominaria with a set that mixed new legends with plenty of nostalgia for long-time players. It is the kind of set that gives a price list some texture: a headline mythic that towers over everything else, a handful of lands and enchantments that hold modest but consistent value, and a long tail of cards sitting within a few dollars of each other. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, from the top down to twentieth place.

Where a card has more than one printing within Dominaria United, such as a showcase frame or a promotional version, we take its highest priced printing as the number used for ranking. The other printings are still listed alongside it so you can see how far apart they sit. On the rare occasion that two printings land on the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

A word on the numbers themselves: these are snapshot prices, gathered at a point in time, and the secondary market for Magic singles shifts constantly with supply and demand. What you see below is accurate as of when it was written, not a permanent fact about the card. If you are shopping for any of these singles, treat the figures as a guide to relative standing on the list rather than a fixed price tag.

The gap between rank one and the rest of this list is worth flagging up front. Sheoldred, the Apocalypse sits well clear of everything below it, while the majority of the remaining nineteen cards are separated from their neighbours by cents rather than dollars. That compression lower down the list makes small movements in price capable of reshuffling several positions at once.
