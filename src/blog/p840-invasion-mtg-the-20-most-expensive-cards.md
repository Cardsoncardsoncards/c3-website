Invasion arrived on 2 October 2000 as the set that formally kicked off Magic's multicolour era, pushing gold cards and allied colour pairs into the spotlight in a way earlier blocks had only gestured towards. It remains a set collectors return to for its rares and uncommons built around colour pairing, and this list looks at the twenty cards from the set currently carrying the highest price tags in Australian dollars.

The ranking here works on a simple principle: every card appears once, and if a card has more than one printing within Invasion itself, it is ranked by whichever of those printings currently costs the most. Where two printings of the same card land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than glossed over. In this particular set, as it happens, every card on the list has just a single tracked printing, so there is no internal competition between print runs to resolve, only the ranking of one card against another.

Sitting at the top is Phyrexian Altar, priced well clear of anything else in the set, with Aura Shards and Tsabo's Web following at a considerable distance behind it. From there the list settles into a more gradual slope down through rares like Elvish Champion, Spirit of Resistance and Captain Sisay, before easing further towards the uncommons and rares that close out the twenty, such as Reya Dawnbringer and Coalition Victory.

Every figure quoted below is a snapshot taken at one point in time. Magic singles prices shift with supply, demand and the broader market, so a card's position today is not a guarantee of where it will sit next month or next year.
