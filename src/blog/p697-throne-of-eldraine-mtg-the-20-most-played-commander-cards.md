Throne of Eldraine landed on 4 October 2019, dropping fairytale-flavoured mechanics and a swathe of staples into the Commander format that have stuck around ever since. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decklists. It is a play frequency ranking first and foremost, not a price ranking, so a common land can easily sit above a mythic that costs a hundred times more.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Throne of Eldraine itself, whether through borderless treatments, extended art, or other variants. Where that happens, the card is ranked once, using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing, with the other printing's price listed alongside it. On the couple of occasions where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars and will drift as the market moves, both up and down, in the weeks and months after this was written. Readers in Australia can chase these singles down through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, where listings for both original and variant printings tend to run alongside one another.
