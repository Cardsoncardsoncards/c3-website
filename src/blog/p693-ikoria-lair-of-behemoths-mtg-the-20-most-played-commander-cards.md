Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths landed on 24 April 2020, bringing giant creatures, companions and a stack of lands that would go on to see heavy Commander play. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from that set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decklists. It is not a value list and it is not a list of the most expensive cards in the set, though a few of those show up here anyway.

Several of these cards have more than one printing within Ikoria itself, most obviously the five Triomes and a handful of rares that got a second treatment. Where that happens, the card is ranked once using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, and priced using whichever printing carries the higher price. Where two printings happen to land on the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. Evolving Wilds sits at the top of the list, and everything below it is ordered strictly by EDHREC rank, not by price.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. Where you buy is up to you, our own card pages carry these singles, and eBay Australia is another common place to check current listings.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths release?**
The set released on 24 April 2020.

**Which card from the set is the most played in Commander?**
Evolving Wilds is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 19, well ahead of every other card on this list.

**How does this list handle cards with more than one printing in the set?**
Each card appears once. If it has multiple printings within Ikoria, it is ranked using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and priced using its highest priced printing, with any exact price ties between printings called out rather than resolved silently.

**Is this list ranking cards by price or by how often they are played?**
By how often they are played, using EDHREC rank. Price is shown alongside each entry but does not decide the order.
