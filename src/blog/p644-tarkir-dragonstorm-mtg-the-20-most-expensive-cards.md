Tarkir: Dragonstorm, released 11 April 2025, sent Magic's players back to the plane of Tarkir with a new roster of dragons, clan legends and supporting cast. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price, from the single priciest card down to number twenty.

Where a card has multiple printings within the set, such as a borderless or showcase treatment alongside its regular version, the card is ranked once by whichever printing carries the highest price. Any other priced printings of that same card are listed alongside it, and where two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Prices throughout are shown in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at time of writing. Singles markets move constantly with supply, so a figure quoted here today may drift by the time a reader goes looking for the card themselves.

Readers on the hunt for any of the twenty cards below can check listings through the card pages on Cards on Cards on Cards, or compare against eBay Australia for additional supply.
