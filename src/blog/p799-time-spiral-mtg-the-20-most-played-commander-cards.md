Time Spiral first hit shelves on 2006-10-06, and it remains one of the most fondly remembered "callback" sets Wizards of the Coast has ever produced, packed with reprints and riffs on mechanics from across Magic's history. This post looks at the 20 cards from the set that see the most play in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decklists.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card had several printings within Time Spiral itself. When a card does carry more than one printing in the set, the rank shown is its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, and the headline price is drawn from whichever printing costs the most. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Every price below is a snapshot. Magic singles move constantly with supply, demand and reprint news, so treat these figures as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers in Australia can track down these cards through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and it's worth checking both before buying.

The list runs from number 1, the most played card in the set, down to number 20. Terramorphic Expanse sits at the top.
