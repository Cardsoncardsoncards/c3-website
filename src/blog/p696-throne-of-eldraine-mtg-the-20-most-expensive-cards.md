Throne of Eldraine landed on 4 October 2019, bringing a fairytale-flavoured set to Magic: The Gathering with adventure spells, throne cards and a small but memorable pile of high-value staples. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, drawing on data across every printing found within Throne of Eldraine itself.

Where a card has more than one printing in the set, it is ranked by whichever printing is priced highest, with the other printing's price noted alongside it. If two printings happen to land on the exact same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. Only Chulane, Teller of Tales appears with a single priced printing in this set; every other card on the list carries two.

Prices shown here are a snapshot and will shift over time as supply, demand and the broader singles market move around. Readers in Australia can track down copies of any of these cards through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and it is worth checking current listings before assuming a figure from this list still holds.
