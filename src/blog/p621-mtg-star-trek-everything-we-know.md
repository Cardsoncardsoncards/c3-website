Magic: The Gathering | Star Trek releases worldwide on 13 November 2026, timed to Star Trek's 60th anniversary. Prerelease events run 6 to 12 November, with the set landing on MTG Arena on 10 November. It's the final Magic set of 2026, closing out one of the busiest release calendars in the game's history.

## A crossover 60 years in the making

Built in collaboration with CBS, Star Trek pulls crews, adversaries and starships from across the full 60 year franchise, from The Original Series through Lower Decks. Wizards first announced the set at MagicCon Atlanta in 2025, and it was previewed at the STLV: Trek to Vegas convention running 5 to 9 August 2026. Early spoilers already confirm mythic treatments of Captain Kirk and Captain Janeway, alongside the U.S.S. Enterprise-D.

## The signed headliners

The standout collector feature is a run of seven signed headliner cards, each depicting an iconic Star Trek captain and individually signed by the actor who originally portrayed that character. Wizards has confirmed roughly 250 signed copies of each, found exclusively in Collector Boosters. That's a genuinely different kind of chase card to anything Magic has shipped before, closer to a signed memorabilia insert than a standard premium treatment.

## New mechanics: Assimilate, Face a Dilemma and Federation

Star Trek introduces three new mechanics. Assimilate lets you take control of a creature or put a creature card onto the battlefield under your control, a direct nod to the Borg. Face a Dilemma triggers when players choose between modes on a card. Federation scales with the number of creature types among your non Borg creatures, rewarding a spread of different Starfleet species and roles in a single deck. Spacecraft and the station keyword both return from Edge of Eternities alongside these three new additions.

## Four Commander decks, four factions

Star Trek ships with four confirmed Commander precons, each built around a different in-universe faction. Federation Fleet leans Jeskai (white blue red) around starships and card draw. Landing Party is Bant (green white blue) built on landfall. Klingon Fury is Mardu (red white black) playing an aggressive Warriors plan. We Are the Borg is Esper (white blue black) themed around Assimilate and artifact creatures. Full decklists haven't been published yet, but the colours and themes are confirmed.

## Legality and product structure

The main Star Trek set (TRK) is Standard legal, alongside every other constructed format, a genuinely full premier level Universes Beyond release rather than a supplemental product. The Commander deck cards (TRC) and Stardates cards (SDS) are legal in Commander, Legacy and Vintage. Beyond boosters and the four Commander decks, the product line includes a Beam Me Up Bundle with exclusive Spacecraft foil promos, Scene Boxes, a Beginner Box and Draft Night support.
