War of the Spark closed out the Ravnica Allegiance era with a planeswalker-heavy set built around the invasion of Ravnica by Nicol Bolas, and it released on 3 May 2019. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by Australian dollar price, running from number 1, the priciest card in the set, down to number 20.

Every card here is a single entry, even if it happens to have more than one printing within War of the Spark. Where a card has multiple printings, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card in this particular list has just one priced printing in the set, so there is no internal jostling to sort out this time around.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a few cents and sometimes by a good deal more, so treat these figures as a point-in-time read rather than something fixed. Readers can track down copies through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia.
