Theros arrived on the 27th of September 2013, bringing Magic's card frame face to face with a full Greek mythology theme, gods included. It gave the game a cycle of indestructible-adjacent deities, devotion as a mechanic, and a run of enchantment creatures that still show up in cube and casual decks today. This list looks at the 20 most expensive cards from the set, ranked by their current Australian dollar price.

Each card in the set appears once on this list. Where a card has more than one printing within Theros, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card below has a single tracked printing, so there is no internal tie-breaking to walk through this time, though the rule still applies to the methodology as a whole.

Prices throughout are given in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so a figure quoted here today may not match what you see on a card page or an eBay Australia listing next month. Buy singles through the card pages on this site or through eBay Australia, and treat every number below as current rather than fixed.

Theros released on the 27th of September 2013, and it has been available on the singles market ever since.

Each card appears once, ranked by its highest priced printing within the set, and any two printings sharing an exact price are noted as a tie rather than being silently separated. Bow of Nylea and Thassa, God of the Sea are the one pair on this list that share a price exactly.

**Which is the single most expensive card in Theros?**
Nykthos, Shrine to Nyx is the most expensive card in the set at AU$77.16, sitting well ahead of the rest of the list.
