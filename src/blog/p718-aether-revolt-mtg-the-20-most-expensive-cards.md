Aether Revolt is the small set that followed Kaladesh, released on the 20th of January, 2017, and it slotted into the same plane of invention and artifice. It is remembered for a tight run of efficient rares and mythics that saw serious competitive play, and that history shows up clearly in this list of its most expensive cards by current Australian dollar price. This ranking runs from number 1, the most expensive card in the set, down to number 20.

Every card here appears once, even where a card had more than one printing in Aether Revolt. In cases with multiple printings, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the higher price, and where two printings land on exactly the same figure that tie is called out rather than being quietly resolved. As it happens, every card in this particular top twenty has just a single priced printing in the set, so there is no split pricing to untangle this time around.

Prices are shown in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move, sometimes by a few cents and sometimes more, so treat the figures here as a point in time rather than a fixed number.
