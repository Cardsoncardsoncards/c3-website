The Brothers' War landed on 18 November 2022, bringing with it an artifact-heavy set that leaned hard into the Urza and Mishra storyline, along with a slab of reprinted lands and staples that Commander players have been happy to slot into their decks ever since. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card turns up in more decklists relative to the rest of the field.

Some of these cards have more than one printing within The Brothers' War itself, whether through borderless treatments, retro frames or other variant slots. Where that happens, the card is ranked once using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of its printings carries the higher price. Any other printing's price is shown alongside it, and where two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly smoothed over.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at one point in time. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat the figures as a read on where things stood rather than a permanent fact. For buying, singles from this set are available through the card pages on our own site and through eBay Australia.
