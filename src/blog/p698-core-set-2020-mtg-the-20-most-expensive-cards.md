Core Set 2020 arrived on 12 July 2019 as one of Wizards of the Coast's last dedicated Core Sets, mixing new mythics with returning staples and a full complement of planeswalkers. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20, giving a snapshot of where collector value sits across the set's rare and mythic slots.

Every card in this list has only a single priced printing within Core Set 2020, so there is no need to hunt down alternate frames or promotional versions to find the number that matters. Where our methodology notes call for it, cards with several printings would be ranked by their highest priced version, with ties flagged rather than quietly resolved, but that scenario does not come up here since each entry sits on exactly one printing.

Field of the Dead sits alone at the top of the set, priced well clear of everything beneath it, while the rest of the field descends in a fairly even curve down to Mystic Forge at the bottom of this list. Rarity plays out as expected, with mythics and rares dominating the upper and middle reaches and only one uncommon, Veil of Summer, breaking into the top twenty.

Prices throughout are quoted in Australian dollars and are accurate as of when this list was compiled. Card prices move constantly with market conditions, so treat these figures as a snapshot rather than a fixed reference, and check current listings before buying or selling.
