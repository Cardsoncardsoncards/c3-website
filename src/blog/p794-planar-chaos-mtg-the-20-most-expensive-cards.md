Planar Chaos is the small-set companion to Time Spiral, released on the 2nd of February 2007 as part of Wizards of the Coast's time-travel themed block. It is best remembered for reprinting Damnation and for reshuffling colour pie expectations, printing familiar effects in unfamiliar colours. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by current Australian dollar price, drawing on singles pricing rather than any assumption about what players are hunting.

Every card in this set that carries a listed price has just one printing, so there is no averaging or picking a "best" version. Where the methodology calls for ties to be noted rather than broken silently, none arise here, each of the 20 entries sits at a distinct price point from its neighbours. The order runs from number 1, the most expensive card in the set, down to number 20.

Prices below are a snapshot only. Singles pricing shifts with supply, listings and time, so treat these figures as a point-in-time read rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia.
