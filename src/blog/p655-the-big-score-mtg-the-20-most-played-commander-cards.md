The Big Score released on 19 April 2024, and this list works through its 20 most played cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, from number 1 down to number 20. Lower EDHREC rank means a card turns up in more decklists, so the card sitting at rank 250 is doing far more work at Commander tables than one sitting past rank 8000, even if the price tag tells a different story.

Several of these cards have multiple printings within The Big Score itself. Where that happens, the card is ranked using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, but priced using whichever printing carries the highest price. The other printings for that card are listed alongside it, and where two printings happen to land on the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly folded into the ranking.

Every price here is a snapshot in Australian dollars. Singles markets move week to week, and a figure that holds true today may look quite different in a month, so treat these numbers as a point of reference rather than a fixed value.

The cards themselves are all mythic rarity, which flattens one axis of comparison and leaves EDHREC rank and price as the two figures doing the talking throughout this list.
