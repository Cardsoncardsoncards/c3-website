Ikoria: Lair of Behemoths arrived on 24 April 2020, bringing giant monsters, the mutate mechanic and a stack of Companion creatures that reshaped early deckbuilding conversations. It also gave the format its five Triomes, lands that have quietly held value across several years of reprints. This list looks back at that set through a single lens: current Australian dollar pricing for the 20 most expensive cards.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Ikoria itself, whether through showcase treatments, borderless frames or godzilla-style alternate art. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, it is ranked here by whichever printing carries the highest price, and the other printings are listed alongside it rather than left out. Where two printings land on the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly broken one way or the other.

Prices below are a snapshot only. Magic singles move constantly with supply, reprints in later sets and shifts in what's being played, so the figures here reflect one point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers in Australia can check current listings through our own card pages or through eBay Australia if they want to compare against what's shown here.
