Dominaria arrived on 27 April 2018 as a return to Magic's oldest plane, mixing new legends with a slate of efficient lands and value creatures that Commander players adopted almost immediately. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from that set in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means a card shows up in more decks. It is a snapshot of play patterns, not a price guide, though we have paired each entry with current pricing for context.

Every card here appears once, even where Dominaria gave it more than one printing. Where a card has several printings in the set, the rank shown is its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, and the price shown is the highest priced of them. If two printings happen to land on the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved. In this particular list, every card carries just a single priced printing, so the arithmetic stays simple, but the rule is applied uniformly regardless.

Prices are in Australian dollars and reflect a point in time. Singles markets move constantly, sometimes by cents, sometimes by a lot more, so treat every figure here as a snapshot rather than a fixed number. Readers looking to buy can do so through our own card pages or via eBay Australia, and both venues will show current asking prices that may differ from what is listed here.

The most played card from the set, by a wide margin over the rest of the field, is Llanowar Elves, sitting at EDHREC rank 58. From there the list moves through cycle lands, artifacts and a run of Dominaria's signature legends, ending with cards that still see reasonable Commander play despite ranking well outside the top few hundred.
