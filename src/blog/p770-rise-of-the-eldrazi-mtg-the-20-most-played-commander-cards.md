Rise of the Eldrazi landed on the 23rd of April 2010, a set best remembered for cracking open the colourless corner of Magic with the Eldrazi titans and a swathe of ramp and support pieces that Commander tables have leaned on ever since. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander decks today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where the lower the number, the more decks a card turns up in.

Each card on this list appears once, even if it has more than one printing within Rise of the Eldrazi. Where a card does have multiple printings in the set, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever printing is currently most expensive. In cases where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved in the text below it. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just one priced printing in the set, so there are no ties to flag here.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a single snapshot in time. The market for Rise of the Eldrazi singles shifts as supply, reprints elsewhere and general demand ebb and flow, so treat every figure here as a point-in-time reading rather than a fixed value. For current pricing and to buy singles, readers can check the card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia.

Evolving Wilds sits at the top by a wide margin, a testament to how often a cheap, colourless land fixer finds a home regardless of what else is in a deck. From there the list moves into the set's rarer and pricier corners, mixing the Eldrazi titans and their support cast with a handful of enchantments and utility pieces that have carved out smaller but persistent followings.
