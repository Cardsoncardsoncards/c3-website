Future Sight closed out the Time Spiral block when it released on 2007-05-04, and it remains one of the strangest, most experimental sets Magic has produced. Its cards previewed mechanics and card frames that wouldn't see full use for years, and that peculiar design brief left it packed with cards that have found long, quiet careers in Commander. This list ranks the twenty most played Future Sight cards in the format, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play.

Every card here is drawn from a single printing within Future Sight itself. Our methodology, for cards with multiple printings in a set, is to rank by the best (lowest) EDHREC rank and price by the highest priced printing, noting explicitly any case where two printings share an identical price rather than quietly picking one. For this particular set that rule stays in the background, since none of these twenty cards have more than one priced printing within Future Sight to compare.

Prices are listed in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, sometimes sharply, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value. Readers looking to buy can check our own card pages or shop through eBay Australia, where availability and pricing will vary from what's printed below.
