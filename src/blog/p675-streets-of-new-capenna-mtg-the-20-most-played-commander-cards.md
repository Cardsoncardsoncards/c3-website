Streets of New Capenna landed on 29 April 2022, bringing five criminal families and a stack of triome lands to Commander tables everywhere. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set, using EDHREC rank as the measure of play, where a lower rank number means the card turns up in more decks. The most played card by this measure is An Offer You Can't Refuse, sitting at EDHREC rank 35.

Several of these cards have more than one printing within Streets of New Capenna itself, whether through borderless treatments, showcase frames or other variants. Where that happens, the card is ranked by its single best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever of its printings costs the most. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars, and card prices move over time with supply, demand and reprints. Treat the figures as a point of reference rather than a fixed number, and check current listings before buying. Singles can be sourced through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia.

What follows is the countdown from twenty up to the single most played card in the set, with a short note on each entry beyond the numbers already shown above it.
