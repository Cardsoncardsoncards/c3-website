Shadowmoor arrived in May 2008 as the darker, dourer half of a two-set block, its sibling Eventide following soon after with the same hybrid mana mechanics turned to different ends. It is a set built around shifting colour identities, the pushed hybrid cards that let a deck lean into two colours at once, and a run of build-around rares that have kept a foothold in cube, cEDH and legacy circles ever since. That long tail of formats is part of why, sixteen years on, the set still has cards worth tracking rather than a flat curve of bulk commons.

This list ranks the twenty most expensive Shadowmoor cards by their current Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20. Where a card has more than one printing within the set, we take its highest priced printing as the ranking figure, and we note in the line above each entry how many printings exist and what those other printings cost. Where two printings share an identical price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other, so nothing is hidden behind a single headline number.

Every figure here is a snapshot. Magic singles prices move with supply, with reprints in other sets, with format bans and with plain market drift, so the ordering and the numbers you see today will not be exactly the ordering and numbers of a year from now. Painter's Servant sits at the top of this particular snapshot, well clear of the field, and the rest of the list runs down through a mix of rares, one uncommon and a single common that has punched above its rarity in price. Readers looking to buy any of these singles in Australia can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, both of which carry current listings rather than the fixed figures quoted below.

The spread across this top twenty is worth noting on its own terms. The gap between the most expensive card and the twentieth is large in relative terms but the bottom half of the list is tightly bunched, with barely a dollar or two separating several consecutive entries. That bunching says more about how flat the demand curve is for the set's mid tier than any single card's story.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Shadowmoor release?**
Shadowmoor released on the 2nd of May 2008 as the first set in the Shadowmoor and Eventide block.

**Which card is the most expensive in Shadowmoor?**
Painter's Servant is the most expensive card in the set on this snapshot, priced at AU$77.62 with a single tracked printing.

**How does the ranking handle cards with multiple printings?**
Each card appears once on the list, ranked by whichever of its printings within the set carries the highest price. Where two printings share exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently.

**Do all cards on the list have more than one printing?**
No. Most of the twenty cards here have a single printing in the set, and only Reflecting Pool has more than one printing recorded, though it too has just one printing that carries a tracked price.
