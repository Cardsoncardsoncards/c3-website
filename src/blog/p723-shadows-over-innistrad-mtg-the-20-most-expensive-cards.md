Shadows over Innistrad returned Magic: The Gathering to its gothic horror plane in 2016, folding werewolves, vampires, spirits and a slow creeping madness into a set that has since become a fixture of Innistrad-themed collections. It sits alongside its parent set, Innistrad, and its later companion, Eldritch Moon, as one of the defining horror-flavoured releases in the game's history, and it introduced mechanics and cards that are still discussed years after release.

This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to the twentieth spot. Cryptolith Rite sits at the very top of the list. Where a card has been printed more than once within Shadows over Innistrad, it is ranked by its single highest priced printing, not by an average, and any other printings with tracked prices are noted alongside it. When two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is recorded rather than quietly resolved one way or another.

Almost every card in this list carries just one priced printing in the set, which makes the ranking a simple ordering exercise for most of the field. Tamiyo's Journal is the exception, appearing across six printings in the set with four of them carrying a tracked price, so its position reflects the highest of those figures while the others trail behind it.

Keep in mind that these are snapshot prices. Singles markets move constantly with supply, demand and time since release, so the figures here reflect a moment rather than a fixed value.
