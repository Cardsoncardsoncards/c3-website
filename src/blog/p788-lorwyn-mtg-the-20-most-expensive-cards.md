Lorwyn, released on 2007-10-12, was the set that introduced Magic's tribal focus and its first proper look at planeswalkers. It gave players a plane built around five core creature types, and it has held a curious mix of chase rares and quietly priced commons ever since. This list ranks the 20 most expensive Lorwyn cards by current Australian dollar price, from the single most costly card down to the twentieth.

Each card in this ranking appears once. Where a card has more than one printing in the set, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and any printings that share an identical price have that tie noted rather than quietly resolved. In this particular list, every one of the 20 cards happens to have only a single priced printing in the set, so there are no internal ties or secondary printings to weigh up.

Prices shown throughout this post are a snapshot only. Magic singles move constantly with supply, reprints elsewhere and general market drift, so the figures here reflect a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers looking to buy or sell can check current listings on our own card pages or search eBay Australia for comparison.
