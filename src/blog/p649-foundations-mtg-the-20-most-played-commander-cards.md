Foundations landed on 15 November 2024 as a deep, evergreen-flavoured set built to sit at the middle of the constructed and casual landscape for years to come, and that breadth shows up clearly in Commander play. This list ranks the twenty Foundations cards most played in EDHREC's Commander data, ordered from number 1 down to number 20 by EDHREC rank, where a lower rank means the card turns up in more decklists. It is a play-frequency list, not a price list, so a card can sit near the top while costing next to nothing, or further down while carrying a heavier price tag.

Several of these cards were printed more than once within Foundations itself. Where that happens, the card is ranked once, using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, and priced using whichever printing currently costs the most. Any other printings with a price are listed alongside it, and where two printings happen to share an identical price, that coincidence is called out rather than quietly smoothed over.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars, exactly as recorded at the time of writing. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so treat these figures as a point of reference rather than a permanent fact. Singles can be sourced through the card pages on Cards on Cards on Cards or through eBay Australia, and either venue is worth checking against the numbers here before buying.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Foundations release?**
Foundations released on 15 November 2024, and it had already been out for some time by the point this ranking was compiled.

**What does this list actually measure?**
It ranks the twenty Foundations cards most played in Commander decks according to EDHREC rank, where a lower rank number means the card appears in more decklists. It is not a list of the most expensive or most valuable cards.

**How are cards with multiple Foundations printings handled?**
Each card appears only once on the list. It is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across all its Foundations printings, and priced using whichever of those printings currently costs the most, with any other priced printings noted separately.

**What happens when two printings of a card are priced exactly the same?**
That tie is stated plainly rather than being broken or hidden, so readers can see when two printings genuinely share a price rather than one simply rounding to match the other.

**Which card is the most played Foundations card in Commander?**
Swiftfoot Boots holds the top spot, with an EDHREC rank of 12 and a price of AU$4.61 for its highest priced printing.

**Are the prices in this article fixed?**
No. Every price given here is a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at one point in time, and card prices move afterwards, sometimes considerably, so current listings may differ from the figures quoted.
