Legends released back on 1994-06-01, and three decades later it remains one of the most closely watched sets from Magic's earliest years. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by Australian dollar price, running from the single priciest card at number one down to number twenty. It is a list built for browsing, whether you are chasing a specific rare for a cube or simply curious about where the set's value sits today.

The methodology is simple but worth stating plainly. Each card appears once on this list, even if it had more than one printing within Legends. Where a card carries several printings, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and the other printings are listed alongside it. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly broken by some arbitrary rule. For every card here, the data shows a single printing carrying a price, so there is no splitting of printings to worry about, but the method still stands for future updates.

Legends is remembered for its legendary creatures, its two card names joined by an ampersand, and a run of powerful rares and uncommons that have aged into some of the most expensive cardboard from the early 1990s. That reputation shows in this list. Rares dominate, though a couple of uncommons punch well above their rarity in price. The gap between the top of the list and the bottom is enormous, a reminder of how unevenly value is spread even within a single old set.

One last note before the list itself: card prices move constantly. What follows is a snapshot taken at a point in time, not a fixed record. Anyone buying or selling should check current listings before treating any figure here as gospel.
