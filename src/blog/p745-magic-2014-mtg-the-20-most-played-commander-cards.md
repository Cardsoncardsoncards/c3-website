Magic 2014 was the last of Wizards of the Coast's core sets to arrive as a yearly, standalone release before core sets took a hiatus, landing on 19 July 2013 with a mix of reprints and new designs aimed at drafters and constructed players alike. Years on, its Commander legacy is what keeps a lot of these cards in circulation, and this list ranks the 20 most played Magic 2014 cards in the format using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks.

Each card on this list appears only once, even where Magic 2014 printed it more than once. Our methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across a card's printings in the set, and prices it at whichever printing carries the highest price. Where two printings land on the same price, we note that tie rather than quietly picking one over the other. As it happens, every card here has a single priced printing in this set, so there's no splitting of hairs this time around.

The order below runs from number 1, the most played card, down to number 20, the least played of this particular group. Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only, singles markets move constantly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. ## Frequently asked questions

**When did Magic 2014 release?**
Magic 2014 released on 19 July 2013, as one of Wizards of the Coast's yearly core sets.

**What does this list measure?**
It ranks the 20 most played Magic 2014 cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower rank number means the card appears in more decks.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears once on the list, ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across its printings and priced at its highest priced printing. Where two printings share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently, though in this particular list every card had only one priced printing.

**Why do some higher-ranked cards cost less than lower-ranked ones?**
