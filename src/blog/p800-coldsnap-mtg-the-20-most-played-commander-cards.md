Coldsnap arrived on 21 July 2006 as a small, self-contained set finishing off a storyline that had been left on ice since the original Ice Age and Alliances blocks. It never had the print run of a modern set, and it carries no reprints within its own borders, so every card here has exactly one Coldsnap printing to its name. Despite that slim footprint, several of its rares and uncommons have carved out a real presence in Commander decks, which is what this list tracks.

The ranking below runs from number 1 down to number 20, ordered purely by EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card is played in more decks. Because Coldsnap has no duplicate printings to weigh against each other, this edition of the methodology is simple in practice: each card's single printing supplies both its rank and its price, with no best-of-several-printings adjustment needed and no ties to flag. That said, the underlying method still allows for it, cards with multiple printings would be ranked by their lowest EDHREC number and priced by their highest listed price, with any exact price tie between printings called out rather than quietly resolved.

Rite of Flame sits at the top with an EDHREC rank of 660, the strongest showing on this list by a wide margin. From there the list spreads out across common, uncommon and rare Coldsnap cards, with prices that owe more to individual collector interest than to any pattern of rarity. Keep in mind that every price quoted here is a snapshot; card prices move constantly, and what you see today on our site or on eBay Australia may differ from the figures printed above.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Coldsnap release?**
Coldsnap released on 21 July 2006, and it is a completed set with no further printings expected.

**What does this list measure?**
It ranks the twenty Coldsnap cards most played in Commander decks according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card appears in more decks.

**How are cards with multiple printings or tied prices handled?**
Each card is ranked by its lowest, meaning best, EDHREC rank across its printings in the set, and priced by its highest priced printing, with any exact price tie between two printings noted rather than silently resolved. In this particular list every card has only one Coldsnap printing, so no such adjustment was needed.

Rite of Flame is the most played card from Coldsnap, sitting at EDHREC rank 660 and priced at AU$8.32.
