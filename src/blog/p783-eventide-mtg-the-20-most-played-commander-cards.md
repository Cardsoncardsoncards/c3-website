Eventide arrived on 25 July 2008 as the second half of the Shadowmoor block, closing out a set built around hybrid mana and the strange, gothic-fairytale creatures of that plane. It never got a Commander-specific reprint push the way some later sets did, so most of what circulates today is the original small-set stock, which shapes both the pool of playable cards and the prices attached to them. This list looks at the twenty Eventide cards with the most play in Commander decks right now, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decklists relative to the rest of the format.

The methodology is simple but worth stating plainly. Each card appears once on this list even if it has more than one printing within Eventide itself. Where a card does have multiple printings in the set, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC number, and priced using whichever printing costs the most. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved one way or the other. As it happens, every card on this particular list has just one priced printing in Eventide, so the tie-breaking rule is mentioned here for transparency rather than because it was needed twenty times over.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week and sometimes day to day, so a figure quoted here is accurate to the moment it was recorded and not a promise about what a card will cost when you go looking for it. Readers on this side of the world can find these cards through the card pages on our own site or by searching eBay Australia, and checking both is generally worthwhile given how much a price can shift between listings.

What follows is the ranking itself, from the most played Eventide card in Commander down to the twentieth, with the rank, price and printing detail sitting above each entry and a short note underneath adding whatever context the numbers alone do not spell out.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Eventide release?**
Eventide released on 25 July 2008 as part of the Shadowmoor block, and it is a set that has already fully released.

**Which Eventide card is played the most in Commander?**
Bloom Tender is the most played card from the set by EDHREC rank, ranked at 260, priced at AU$28.97, well ahead of every other card on this list.

**How does the ranking handle cards with more than one printing in the set?**
A card appears once on the list regardless of how many Eventide printings it has, ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC number, and priced using its highest priced printing, with any exact price ties between printings noted rather than resolved silently.

**Are the prices on this list fixed?**
No, every price given is a snapshot in Australian dollars taken at one point in time, and singles prices move after that point, sometimes by a noticeable amount.

**Where can Australian buyers purchase these singles?**

**Does a low EDHREC rank number mean a card is played less?**
No, on this list a lower EDHREC rank number means a card is played more, so Bloom Tender's rank of 260 reflects more play than Sapling of Colfenor's rank of 6643 at the bottom of the list.

**Why do some cards on this list have a high price despite a low play rank?**
Price and play rank measure different things, one is a market figure and the other is a measure of decklist inclusion, so a card like Sapling of Colfenor can rank last for play while still pricing near the top of the list.
