Magic 2013, one of the last of Wizards of the Coast's annual "Core Set" releases, hit shelves on 13 July 2012. It sits in an odd spot in Magic's history, a set built to reintroduce fundamentals to the game while also carrying a handful of mythic rares that have held collector interest for over a decade. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set, by current price in Australian dollars.

Each card on this list appears once. Where a card has more than one printing within Magic 2013, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card here has a single printing in the set, so there is no multi-printing arithmetic to untangle this time around, just a clean run from the most expensive card down to the twentieth.

Akroma's Memorial sits at the top of the list at AU$52.28, comfortably clear of everything else in the set. From there the list drops fairly quickly into single digits and then into a long, close cluster of cards in the AU$3 to AU$5 range, which makes up most of the back half of the ranking.

Prices in this list are a snapshot only. Magic singles move with supply, reprints and general market drift, so figures quoted here reflect a point in time rather than a fixed value.
