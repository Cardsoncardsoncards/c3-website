Fifth Dawn closed out the Mirrodin block back in June of 2004, and it remains one of the more curious sets in Magic's history thanks to its focus on artifacts and the introduction of mechanics like entwine. Nearly two decades on, it still supplies a surprising number of cards that see regular play in Commander, from cheap card draw to artifact engines that have found homes in dozens of archetypes. This list ranks the 20 most played Fifth Dawn cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks.

Every card on this list appears only once, even if it happens to have more than one printing within Fifth Dawn itself. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, it is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of those printings is currently the most expensive. If two printings happen to land on the exact same price, that is called out rather than smoothed over. As it turns out, every card in this particular list has just a single priced printing in Fifth Dawn, so there is no tie-breaking to speak of this time around.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are current as of when this article was written. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure here as a snapshot rather than a permanent fact. For buying singles in Australia, our own card pages carry current listings, and eBay Australia is another common place to check for both graded and ungraded copies.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Fifth Dawn release?**
Fifth Dawn released on 2004-06-04, closing out the original Mirrodin block.

**How is this list ranked?**
Cards are ordered by EDHREC rank, from most played at number one down to number 20, with a lower rank meaning a card appears in more Commander decks.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears only once on the list, ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing within Fifth Dawn. If two printings share an identical price, that tie is noted rather than resolved silently, though no such tie occurs among these 20 cards.
