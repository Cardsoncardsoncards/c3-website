Innistrad, released 30 September 2011, was the set that took Magic's Gothic horror flavour and turned it into one of the most enduring Commander toolboxes ever printed. Its check lands alone reshaped two-colour manabases for a decade, and it produced a run of value creatures and effects that still show up in decks well outside their original block. This list ranks the twenty most played Innistrad cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card sees more play across the format.

Every card here is drawn straight from Innistrad, ordered from number 1, the most played, down to number 20. Where a card has more than one printing within the set, its rank is taken from whichever printing plays best on EDHREC, and its listed price is the highest of those printings. Any case where two printings share an identical price is called out rather than quietly smoothed over, though as it happens every card on this particular list has just one priced printing in the set.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, so treat every figure as accurate at the time of writing rather than a permanent number.
