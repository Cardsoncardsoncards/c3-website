Tempest is one of the older sets in Magic: The Gathering's history, released on 1997-10-14, and its cards have had decades to settle into whatever place the secondary market has found for them. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by Australian dollar price, drawing from card pages on our own site and current listings. Every card here has a single tracked printing within the set, so the ranking runs cleanly from the top price down without needing to average or combine multiple versions.

Where the methodology matters is in how ties are handled: if two printings shared an identical price, that would be noted explicitly rather than quietly broken by some other factor. None of the twenty cards below required that treatment, since each has just one priced printing in Tempest. The order is fixed by that single number, from the most expensive card at number one down to the twentieth spot.

Prices are a snapshot, not a permanent record. Magic singles move in Australian dollars over time depending on availability and print runs, and figures quoted here reflect current listings at the time of writing. Readers looking to buy or check current figures can do so through our card pages or through eBay Australia.
