Magic 2010 was the first of Wizards of the Coast's annual "Core Set" era releases, landing on 17 July 2009 with a mix of reprints and new designs aimed squarely at long-term playability. More than a decade on, it remains a quiet source of Commander staples, the kind of cards that show up in decklists without much fanfare but keep getting slotted in regardless. This list ranks the twenty most played Magic 2010 cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play across the format.

Every card below appears once on the list. Where a card had multiple printings within Magic 2010, its rank is taken from the best (lowest) EDHREC figure among those printings, and its price is the highest priced version of the card in the set. If two printings landed on the same price, that tie is called out explicitly rather than glossed over. As it happens, every card here had only a single printing carrying a price, so there are no ties to report this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a fixed value. Singles markets shift week to week, so treat every figure here as accurate at time of writing rather than a permanent number. Readers looking to buy can do so through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it's worth checking both before settling on a purchase.
