Journey into Nyx closed out the Theros block back in May 2014, wrapping up the god-heavy storyline with a set built around devotion, the Underworld, and a final wave of gold and mono-coloured mythics. It is a smaller set than its predecessors, but it carries a dense cluster of constructed and cube staples, which keeps a decent spread of its cards relevant on the secondary market a decade on. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by current price in Australian dollars, from the priciest single card down to the twentieth.

The methodology is simple. Each card is listed once, even if it has more than one printing within Journey into Nyx itself. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, it is ranked according to whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two of those printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out explicitly rather than one being arbitrarily placed above the other. In this particular set, every card on the list happens to have a single priced printing, so there is no internal shuffling to account for, but the rule stands for any set where that changes.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Card markets move, sometimes by a lot and sometimes by very little, depending on reprints, format shifts, and general supply. Nothing here is a guarantee of what a card will cost by the time you read it, only a record of where things stood at the time this list was compiled.
