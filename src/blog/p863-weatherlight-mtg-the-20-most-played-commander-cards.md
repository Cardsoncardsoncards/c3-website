Weatherlight arrived on 9 June 1997, the set that named the whole Weatherlight Saga and gave early Magic players a ship full of legendary crew and a lot of odd little utility cards that have quietly stuck around in Commander decks ever since. This list ranks the 20 most played Weatherlight cards in the format, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card turns up in more decklists. It is a play-frequency list, not a price list, so the order below reflects how often each card is chosen for a deck rather than how much it costs.

Every card in Weatherlight only ever had one printing within this set, so the methodology note about tying and best-printing selection is mostly a formality here. Where a card had appeared with several printings across Magic's history the rule would be to rank by the lowest EDHREC rank achieved by any version and to price by the highest priced version, with any exact price ties between printings called out rather than resolved arbitrarily. For this particular twenty, each entry carries a single tracked price, so the figures printed above each blurb are simply that card's number.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at one point in time. Singles markets move week to week, sometimes sharply for older uncommons and rares from a set this old, so treat every figure here as a moment captured rather than a fixed value.
