March of the Machine: The Aftermath arrived as a small epilogue set on 2023-05-12, following on from the main March of the Machine release and wrapping up some loose narrative threads. It is a compact list, but it still produced a handful of cards that found real homes in Commander decks, and this post ranks the twenty most played of them according to EDHREC rank, from the most played at number 1 down to number 20 at the bottom.

Because several cards in this set were printed more than once, whether as regular versions, borderless treatments or other variants, we needed a consistent way to handle that before ranking anything. Each card appears only once on this list. Where a card has multiple printings within the set, we use its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank to place it, and we price it using its highest priced printing as the headline figure. Any other priced printings are listed alongside it, and where two of those printings land on the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved one way or another.

Training Grounds tops the list, sitting at EDHREC rank 1052, some way ahead of second place. From there the ranks climb gradually rather than in big jumps, and rarity is mixed throughout, with uncommons like Gold-Forged Thopteryx and Filter Out sitting alongside mythics such as Nissa, Resurgent Animist and Ob Nixilis, Captive Kingpin.

All prices here are a snapshot and will move over time, in both directions, as supply and demand shift. Readers in Australia can find singles through the card pages on our own site, or search for the same cards on eBay Australia if they want to compare listings.
