Time Spiral Timeshifted is the timeshifted companion sheet released alongside Time Spiral on 2006-10-06, a set built entirely of reprints presented with the distinctive black-bordered, silver-styled frame that marks every card in it as "special" rarity. It draws from decades of Magic history, pulling cards that never had a modern reprint back into circulation, which is a large part of why the sheet still shows up in Commander decks today. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from that set in Commander, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play.

Every card in Time Spiral Timeshifted appears here just once. Where a card had more than one printing within the set, our methodology takes its best (lowest) EDHREC rank to decide placement, and its highest priced printing to decide the number shown. If two printings of a card land on the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card on this particular list has only a single tracked printing in the set, so no ties or multi-printing comparisons come into play this time around.

Prices below are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Card markets move, sometimes by a lot and sometimes within days, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value. Singles can be bought through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it's worth checking both before buying.
