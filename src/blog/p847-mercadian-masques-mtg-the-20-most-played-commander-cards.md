Mercadian Masques arrived back on 4 October 1999, a set now remembered less for constructed staples and more for the handful of eternal pieces it slipped into Commander decks. This list ranks the 20 most played cards from that set in Commander, ordered by EDHREC rank, where a lower number means more decks are running the card. Counterspell sits at the top of the pile, and from there the list works down through cards that see far less table time but still carry their own price stories.

Every card here appears once, even if a card had more than one printing within the set. Where that happened, the ranking uses the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and the price shown is the highest of the available printings. Had two printings landed on the same price, that tie would be flagged rather than smoothed over, though as it happens none of the twenty needed that treatment this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at time of writing. Singles markets shift constantly, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a permanent value. Anyone chasing a copy can check current listings on our own card pages or search eBay Australia for further options.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did Mercadian Masques release?**
Mercadian Masques released on 4 October 1999, and the set has been available for singles purchase ever since.

**What does this list measure?**
It ranks the 20 most played Mercadian Masques cards in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number indicates more decks running that card.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears once on the list. If a card had several printings within Mercadian Masques, its rank uses the best (lowest) EDHREC rank among them, and its listed price is the highest priced printing. Where two printings shared an identical price, that tie would be noted explicitly rather than resolved silently.

**Which card is the most played?**
Counterspell is the most played card from Mercadian Masques in Commander, sitting at EDHREC rank 16.

**Are the prices listed fixed?**

**Does higher rarity mean a higher price on this list?**
Not consistently. Several commons and uncommons on this list are priced below rares, and rank position does not track rarity in a simple way across the twenty entries.
