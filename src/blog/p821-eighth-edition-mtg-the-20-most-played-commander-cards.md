Eighth Edition landed on the 28th of July 2003 as one of Magic's core sets, the kind of release built to reprint proven cards rather than introduce a new mechanical identity. That reprint focus is exactly why it still shows up in Commander decks today, and this list ranks the twenty most played Eighth Edition cards in that format using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card sees more play. The order runs from number 1, the most played card, down to number 20.

Several of these cards have more than one printing within Eighth Edition itself, so the methodology needs a rule for that. Each card appears once on this list. Where a card has multiple printings in the set, it is ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using whichever of its printings carries the higher price tag. If two printings happened to share a price exactly, that tie is noted rather than quietly resolved.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot over a short stretch, so a figure quoted here reflects one point in time rather than a permanent value. Readers in Australia can track down these cards through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and it's worth checking current listings rather than assuming the numbers below still hold by the time you're reading this.

The list itself spans the full range of Eighth Edition's rarities, from commons like Rampant Growth and Wood Elves through to uncommons and a run of rares. The spread of EDHREC ranks, from 26 at the top down to 1546 at the bottom, gives a sense of how play frequency drops off even within a fairly tight group of twenty cards.
