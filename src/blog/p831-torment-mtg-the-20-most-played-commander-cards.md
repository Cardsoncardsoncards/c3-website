Torment arrived on 4 February 2002 as the second chapter of the Odyssey block, a set remembered for leaning hard into black mana and graveyard strategies. This list ranks the 20 Torment cards most played in Commander today, using EDHREC rank as the measure, from number 1 down to number 20, with the lower rank always denoting the more played card.

Each card on this list appears once, even if a name shares design space with cards elsewhere in Torment. Where a card has more than one printing within this set, it is ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, and priced using its highest priced printing. As it happens, every card here has just one priced printing within Torment, so there are no ties to note this time, though the methodology accounts for that possibility.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes quickly, so treat every figure below as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers can compare these figures against listings on our own card pages or on eBay Australia when looking to buy.
