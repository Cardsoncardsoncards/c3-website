The Big Score is the Magic: The Gathering set that released on 19 April 2024, and this list looks at its twenty most expensive cards by Australian dollar price. Many cards in the set exist in more than one printing, whether that is a straight version and a showcase or borderless variant, and where that happens this list uses the highest priced printing to set the rank, with the other printings listed alongside it for comparison.

Every card on this list is mythic, and several carry three or even four printings within the set. Where two printings land on the same price, that tie is noted rather than quietly broken by some other factor, so readers can see exactly where the numbers sit rather than have them smoothed over.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move day to day, and a card's position here reflects pricing at the time this list was put together rather than a fixed value.

Vaultborn Tyrant sits at the top of the list, and the rest of the field falls away from there, some sharply and some by only a few cents from their nearest neighbour.
