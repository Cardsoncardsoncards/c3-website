Eldritch Moon arrived on 22 July 2016 as the smaller, sharper follow up to Shadows over Innistrad, folding double-faced cards, meld pairings and a heavier dose of the eldritch into a set built around Emrakul's arrival on the plane. It is remembered for a run of mythic bombs and a handful of rares that found homes well outside limited play, and that mix of scarcity and playability is a large part of why the list below looks the way it does. This post ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price, from the priciest down to the twentieth spot.

Each card on the list appears only once, even where a card was printed more than once within Eldritch Moon. When a card has multiple printings here, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out explicitly rather than being broken by some arbitrary secondary rule. As it happens, every card in this particular top twenty has just a single priced printing in the set, so there are no ties or secondary printings to untangle this time round.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move constantly, sometimes by a few cents in a week and sometimes by a lot more, so treat every figure here as accurate to the moment it was recorded rather than as a fixed value. Readers in Australia can chase these cards down through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and comparing listings across both is usually worth the extra minute.

What follows is a rundown of all twenty cards, in order, with a short note on each covering what its price and printing pattern imply next to its neighbours on the list, rather than repeating the numbers already shown above each entry.
