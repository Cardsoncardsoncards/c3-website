Dissension closed out the Ravnica block back in 2006, and nearly two decades later it still turns up constantly in Commander decks thanks to its run of guild lands and mana rocks. This post ranks the twenty most played Dissension cards in the format, using EDHREC rank as the sole measure of how often a card sees play, with a lower number meaning more decks include it. Breeding Pool sits at the top of that list, and the rest of the field trails off through a mix of rares, uncommons and a long tail of guild-themed commons.

Every card in Dissension appears only once on this list even if it happens to have more than one printing within the set. Where a card does have multiple Dissension printings, its rank is drawn from the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank among those printings, and its price is taken from whichever printing is currently most expensive. Any case where two printings land on exactly the same price is called out rather than quietly resolved, though as it happens every card here has just a single priced printing in this set.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and reflect a snapshot at the time of writing. Singles markets shift week to week, sometimes sharply, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed number. Readers looking to buy can check our own card pages or search eBay Australia, where Dissension singles turn up regularly across a wide price spread.

The list itself runs from the shockland trio at the very top down through signets, bounce lands, and a scattering of build-around rares that never cracked the format's upper tiers. Read on for the full ranking, with commentary on how each card sits against its neighbours.

Each card appears once on the list, ranked by its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across its Dissension printings, and priced using whichever of those printings is currently most expensive.
