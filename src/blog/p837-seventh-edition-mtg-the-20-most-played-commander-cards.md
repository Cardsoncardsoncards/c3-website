Seventh Edition landed on 11 April 2001 as one of the core sets that reprinted staples for a new generation of players, and it remains a common sight in Commander decks today thanks to that reprint job. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set in Commander according to EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. The order runs from number 1, the most played card, down to number 20.

Every card in Seventh Edition was printed twice within the set, but each card appears only once on this list. Where a card has two printings, we rank it by whichever printing has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and price it using whichever printing carries the higher price. For every card below, only one of the two printings actually carries a price, so there is nothing to compare on the pricing side this time, though the methodology still applies if that ever changes. Where two printings happen to share a price exactly, that tie is noted rather than silently broken in one direction.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Card prices move constantly with supply, demand and the broader market, so treat every figure here as accurate at the time of writing rather than a permanent number.

Seventh Edition released on 11 April 2001.

**How is this list ordered?**
Cards are ranked by EDHREC rank in Commander, from number 1 as the most played card down to number 20 as the least played of the twenty featured.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
Each card appears once on the list even if it has several printings in Seventh Edition. It is ranked using its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and priced using its highest priced printing, and any exact price tie between printings is noted rather than broken silently.
