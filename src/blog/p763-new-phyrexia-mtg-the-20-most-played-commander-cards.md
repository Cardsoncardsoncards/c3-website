New Phyrexia arrived on the 13th of May 2011, closing out the Scars of Phyrexia block with a mechanically dense set built around Phyrexian mana and the completion of the plane's corruption. It is a small set by card count, but it punches well above its weight in Commander, contributing planeswalkers, praetors and a handful of format staples that have stuck around for well over a decade. This list ranks the 20 most played New Phyrexia cards in Commander, using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means a card sees more play. Number one is the most played card, and the list counts down to number twenty.

Every card here has a single printing in New Phyrexia, so there is no averaging or best-of-multiple-printings logic to apply, though the methodology still calls for using the best EDHREC rank and the highest priced printing where a card does have more than one appearance in the set, and noting ties explicitly rather than resolving them silently. That approach keeps the list honest to how EDHREC itself treats reprint equity, and it means the prices you see attached to each entry reflect a single real printing rather than an artificial blend.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. The singles market moves week to week, and a card sitting at AU$5 today can be meaningfully different in price by the time you read this, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value. Readers wanting to buy any of these can do so through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, where New Phyrexia singles trade regularly given the set's age and continued relevance to Commander decks.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did New Phyrexia release?**
New Phyrexia released on the 13th of May 2011 as the final set in the Scars of Phyrexia block.

**What does this list measure?**
It ranks the 20 most played New Phyrexia cards in Commander using EDHREC rank, where a lower rank number means a card is played more often.

**How are cards with multiple printings in the set handled?**
A card with several printings in New Phyrexia is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing, with ties between printings noted rather than resolved silently. In this particular list every card has a single priced printing.

**Which card is the most played New Phyrexia card in Commander?**
Beast Within holds the top spot, sitting at EDHREC rank 25 and priced at AU$3.46.

**Are the prices listed fixed?**
No, every price given is a snapshot in Australian dollars, and singles prices move over time, so figures will shift after publication.

**Why does this list include mythics priced lower than some rares or uncommons?**
Price and rarity do not move together in a fixed way, price reflects market demand for that specific printing while rarity simply describes how the card was distributed at print, so a mythic can sit below a rare or uncommon in price despite its rarity.
