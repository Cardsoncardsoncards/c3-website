Battle for Zendikar arrived on 2 October 2015, returning players to the plane of Zendikar as the Eldrazi titans tightened their grip on the land. It is a set built around the Awaken mechanic and a heavy Eldrazi presence, and it produced a run of colourless threats and mythic planeswalkers that have stayed part of the conversation since release. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from the set by their current Australian dollar price.

Each card appears once on the list. Where a card has more than one printing within Battle for Zendikar, the ranking uses whichever printing carries the highest price, and any case where two printings land on exactly the same figure is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card here has a single priced printing, so there are no ties or secondary prices to weigh up this time around.

Prices are shown in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at the time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes by a few cents and sometimes more, so treat the figures as a point in time rather than a fixed number. Readers looking to buy can check individual card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia.
