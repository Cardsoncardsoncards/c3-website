Legions arrived on the 3rd of February 2003 as one of the more unusual Magic sets ever printed, a creature-only affair that leaned hard into tribal mechanics and cycles built around each colour's identity. Two decades on, it still has a foothold in Commander, and this list ranks the 20 Legions cards played most often in that format, using EDHREC rank as the measure, from number 1 down to number 20.

Where a card has more than one printing within Legions itself, the methodology takes the best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank across those printings, and prices by whichever printing costs the most. If two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is noted rather than smoothed over. As it happens, every card on this particular list has only a single Legions printing with a listed price, so the multiple-printing logic sits quietly in the background this time.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot, not a fixed value. Singles markets move with supply, demand and time, so treat every figure here as accurate to the moment it was recorded rather than a permanent number.

The list itself spans rares, uncommons and commons, and the spread between the top of the list and the bottom is wide, both in EDHREC rank and in price. Seedborn Muse sits well clear of the rest of the field on played rate, while several commons near the bottom trade for well under a dollar.
