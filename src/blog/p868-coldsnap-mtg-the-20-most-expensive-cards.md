Coldsnap is the small, mechanically dense expansion released on the 21st of July 2006, a set built as a delayed follow up to the original Ice Age block. It never had the print run of a full core set, and that history shows up clearly once you start sorting its cards by current price. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from Coldsnap by Australian dollar price, from the single most costly card down to the twentieth.

Every card here has just one printing within Coldsnap, so this particular list has no genuine multi-printing spread and no ties to note. Where a set does carry cards with several printings, our method is to rank by whichever printing carries the highest price, and to call out explicitly any two printings that land on exactly the same figure rather than silently choosing one over the other. That policy is worth stating even here, since it is the same rule that would apply if this set's card pool were larger.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes by a little and occasionally by a lot, so treat every figure below as a point in time rather than a fixed number. For current pricing and to buy, readers can check individual card pages on our own site or search listings on eBay Australia.
