Arabian Nights holds the distinction of being the first expansion ever released for Magic: The Gathering, arriving on 1993-12-17 with a desert theme that has never been repeated in quite the same way. It is a small set by modern standards, but it carries an outsized reputation among collectors and Commander players alike, and its age means every card in it now sits in a strange space between playable curiosity and antique.

This list ranks the 20 most played cards from the set in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decklists. Some cards in Arabian Nights were printed more than once within the set itself, thanks to the "corrected" and "uncorrected" versions of certain arts and texts that were produced during its original run. Where that happens, we have taken the single lowest EDHREC rank for that card and paired it with the highest price among its printings, and if two printings happen to land on exactly the same price we have said so rather than pretending one edges out the other.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars, quoted to the cent, and it will not stay fixed. Cards from a set this old move with the broader collectable market as much as with anything happening in Commander, and a figure that is accurate today can look quite different in a few months. Readers can track current pricing and buy singles through the card pages on our own site, or through eBay Australia, where listings for cards this old tend to fluctuate more than most.

## Frequently asked questions

**When was Arabian Nights released?**
Arabian Nights released on 1993-12-17, making it the first expansion set ever produced for Magic: The Gathering.

**Which card is the most played from Arabian Nights in Commander?**

**How does this list handle cards with more than one printing in the set?**
Each card appears once on the list. When a card has multiple printings in Arabian Nights, it is ranked using its best, meaning lowest, EDHREC rank, and priced using its highest priced printing, with ties between printing prices noted rather than silently resolved.

**How is played rank determined for this list?**
Rank comes from EDHREC, where a lower number means the card appears in more Commander decklists relative to other cards.
