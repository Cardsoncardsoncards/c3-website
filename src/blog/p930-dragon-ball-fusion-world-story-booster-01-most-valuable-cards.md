Story Booster 01 landed in English on 21 August 2026 and does something Dragon Ball Fusion World has not done before: every card in the set carries a holographic foil finish, and the alt-art Leaders get a dual-sided foil treatment that is a first for the game. Five days in, the singles market has started to sort itself, and the shape of it is worth understanding before you buy.

**Data checked: 26 August 2026.** The set is five days old. Prices on a set this fresh rest on thin listing pools and move sharply. Australian dollar figures are live C3 Card Vault prices, which convert market prices at the daily rate and track the broader market rather than Australian shopfronts.

## Methodology

- **Basis:** highest priced printing per card on C3 data at 26 August 2026.
- **Printings are separate entries.** A card's standard and alternate art printings are priced individually and have not been averaged.
- **Sealed products are listed separately** from single cards.
- **These are market prices**, not individually verified completed sales. On a five day old set, a top-end figure can rest on very few listings and move substantially on a handful of transactions.

## What the data actually shows

**Every single entry is an alternate art printing.** All twenty. The standard printings of these same cards do not appear anywhere near this list, which tells you unambiguously that this set's value is a treatment story rather than a card-power story.

**Rarity is close to irrelevant.** A Rare at number six, [Vegeta ST01-066](/cards/dbsfusionworld/story-booster-01-vegeta-st01066-alternate-art-st01-066) at AU$134.59, outprices Super Rares at seven, eight and nine. Two more Rares appear at 13 and 17. In a set where everything is foil and the differentiator is alternate art, the printed rarity symbol has stopped carrying pricing information.

The practical consequence when buying: filter by alternate art, not by rarity. A listing advertising a Super Rare tells you very little about what it is worth here, and [current Story Booster 01 Secret Rare listings on eBay Australia](https://www.ebay.com.au/sch/i.html?_nkw=dragon+ball+fusion+world+story+booster+01+secret+rare&campid=5339146789&customid=dbfw-sb01-secretrare&mkevt=1&mkcid=1&mkrid=705-53470-19255-0&toolid=10001) are worth comparing against the AU$277 pair at ranks three and four rather than assumed to be dearer.

**The two Secret Rares are priced within 45 cents of each other.** [Vegito at AU$277.68](/cards/dbsfusionworld/story-booster-01-vegito-st01070-alternate-art-st01-070) and [Vegeta at AU$277.23](/cards/dbsfusionworld/story-booster-01-vegeta-st01071-alternate-art-st01-071). A gap that small five days after release is not the market forming distinct views on two cards. It is a placeholder level applied to a tier, and it is likely to separate once real transaction volume arrives.

**Character concentration is extreme.** Vegeta appears five times in the top 20, Son Goku four times, Broly three, Majin Buu in three forms. That is the Buu Saga and Broly theme working exactly as designed, and it means your ability to complete this set cheaply depends heavily on which characters you care about.

## The double-sided foil Leaders

The set's genuine novelty, and the top two cards in it. The alt-art Leader reprints, Vegito and Broly, carry a dual-sided foil treatment described as a first for Fusion World.

[Broly FB08-073](/cards/dbsfusionworld/story-booster-01-broly-fb08073-alternate-art-fb08-073) at AU$419.05 leads the set. [Vegeta ST01-053](/cards/dbsfusionworld/story-booster-01-vegeta-st01053-alternate-art-st01-053) at AU$401.64 and [Son Goten ST01-001](/cards/dbsfusionworld/story-booster-01-son-goten-st01001-alternate-art-st01-001) at AU$232.00 complete the Leader tier, with the [Majin Buu three-form Leader](/cards/dbsfusionworld/story-booster-01-majin-buumajin-buu-pure-evil-majin-buu-evil-alternate-art-st01-027) at AU$97.06.

First-of-a-kind treatments carry two opposing forces. They attract concentrated early attention, which supports price. They also have no track record, so nobody knows whether the treatment holds value once it stops being novel. If double-sided foil becomes a regular feature of future sets, these lose their distinction. If it stays rare, they keep it.

We have no information either way, and neither does anyone quoting you a confident forecast. If you have decided you want one regardless, [current Story Booster 01 alternate art Leader listings on eBay Australia](https://www.ebay.com.au/sch/i.html?_nkw=dragon+ball+fusion+world+story+booster+01+leader+alternate+art&campid=5339146789&customid=dbfw-sb01-leaders&mkevt=1&mkcid=1&mkrid=705-53470-19255-0&toolid=10001) are the check against the AU$419.05 and AU$401.64 tracked figures, and on a five day old set that comparison matters more than usual.

## Competitive versus collector

Worth separating, because they behave differently.

**Collector demand** is what the table above measures, and it is clearly the dominant force in this set. Alternate art, a first-time treatment and the most recognisable characters in the franchise.

**Competitive demand** is a separate question this data does not answer. The standard printings play identically to the alternate arts and cost a fraction. If you want to play the new Leaders, buy the standard versions.

Story Booster 01 is a story-themed product built around the Buu Saga climax and Broly, and its design intent is visibly collector-first. Treat the prices above as a collector market, because that is what they are.

## How this set fits the wider Fusion World picture

Some context that changes how you should read the numbers above.

Story Booster 01 is a story-themed product rather than a main numbered booster, and that distinction matters for expectations. Main sets carry the competitive weight and the largest print runs. Story-themed products sit alongside them, usually with a tighter focus and a collector-first design brief. Story Booster 01's Buu Saga and Broly theme, all-foil structure and first-of-a-kind Leader treatment are all consistent with that brief.

The practical consequence is that this set should not be judged against a main booster on competitive impact, and its cards should not be expected to define the format. It is doing a different job.

It also sits inside a busy release run. [Brightness of Hope](/cards/dbsfusionworld) follows on 16 October 2026 and Reach the God on 11 December 2026, which means Story Booster 01 has roughly seven weeks in the spotlight before attention moves. Sets with short attention windows tend to see their release-week premiums erode faster than sets with a long runway, and that is worth factoring into any decision to buy at current levels.

**The Dragon Ball naming trap, briefly.** Dragon Ball has three separate card games and people conflate them constantly. This set belongs to Dragon Ball Super Card Game Fusion World, tracked at [/cards/dbsfusionworld](/cards/dbsfusionworld). It is not the older Dragon Ball Super CCG, and it is not the Panini-era Dragon Ball Z game. If you are searching for prices, make sure you are looking at the right game, because the card numbering conventions overlap enough to mislead.

## What we would do

**If you want to play**, buy standard printings. They are the same cards.

**If you collect**, the double-sided foil Leaders are the defensible targets, because they are the set's genuine novelty rather than another alternate art.

**If you are buying sealed**, wait. Release-week sealed pricing on an all-foil set with a first-of-a-kind treatment is the most inflated it will be.

**If you are chasing a specific character**, note the concentration. Vegeta and Goku cards make up nearly half the top 20, so those are the expensive collecting paths and Gotenks or Trunks are considerably cheaper. Because the character determines the price far more than the rarity does in this set, searching by character rather than by rarity is the efficient approach: [current Story Booster 01 Vegeta listings on eBay Australia](https://www.ebay.com.au/sch/i.html?_nkw=dragon+ball+fusion+world+story+booster+01+vegeta&campid=5339146789&customid=dbfw-sb01-vegeta&mkevt=1&mkcid=1&mkrid=705-53470-19255-0&toolid=10001) cover five of the top twenty entries on their own.
