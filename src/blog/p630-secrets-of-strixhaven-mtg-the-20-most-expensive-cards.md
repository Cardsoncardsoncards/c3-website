Secrets of Strixhaven, released 24 April 2026, is the latest set to send Wizards of the Coast's students back through the five colleges, and with it comes a fresh wave of cards for Australian collectors to track. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards in the set by Australian dollar price, from the top spot down to number twenty.

Where a card has more than one printing in the set, the ranking uses only the highest priced version to decide its place on the list. The lower priced printing, or printings, are still shown for context, and any two printings that happen to share an exact price are noted as a tie rather than being separated arbitrarily. Every card on this list carries at least two priced printings, so the gap between headline and secondary printing is worth watching card by card.

All prices below are given in Australian dollars and are accurate to the cent at the time of writing. Card prices move, sometimes quickly, so treat this as a snapshot of the market rather than a fixed reference. Readers can buy singles through our own card pages or through eBay Australia.

The list runs from Emeritus of Woe // Demonic Tutor at the top through to Emeritus of Conflict // Lightning Bolt at number twenty, covering a mix of mythic and rare cards across the full run of the set.
