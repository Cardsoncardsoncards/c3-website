Saviors of Kamigawa closed out the original Kamigawa block when it released on 2005-06-03, and it remains one of the smaller, more contained sets from that era of Magic. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set in Australian dollars, from the priciest down to the twentieth spot, using the highest priced printing of each card as its entry into the ranking.

Where a card has more than one printing in the set, only the top price counts for its position on the list, and if two printings land on exactly the same figure that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, every card in this particular top 20 has a single priced printing in Saviors of Kamigawa, so the methodology is simple in practice even though the rule exists for sets where it is not.

Every price below is a snapshot rather than a fixed value. Singles markets move with supply and interest over time, so the figures here reflect where things sit right now rather than a permanent ranking. For buying and selling in Australia, our own card pages and eBay Australia are the two channels worth checking against these numbers.
