Dissension closed out the Ravnica block back in 2006, bringing the guild story to its conclusion with a set built around the ten two colour factions of the plane. It gave Magic some of its most enduring dual lands and a handful of build-around rares that still show up in cube and Commander lists today. This countdown looks at the twenty most expensive cards from that set, ranked purely by Australian dollar price.

The methodology is simple but worth stating plainly. Every card on this list appears once, even if it had more than one printing within Dissension. Where a card had multiple printings, we ranked it by whichever printing carries the highest price, and if two printings ever land on the exact same figure, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. In this particular set, none of the twenty cards below have more than one priced printing, so the numbers you see are each card's only listed price.

Prices for a set this old move constantly, shaped by print runs, foil demand and whatever else is happening in the wider singles market at any given time. What follows is a snapshot taken at one point in time, not a permanent ranking. Treat the figures as a guide to where things stood when this list was compiled, not as a fixed hierarchy.

Singles for Dissension can be picked up through the card pages here on Cards on Cards on Cards, as well as through eBay Australia, where listings for this set are common given how long it has been in circulation.
