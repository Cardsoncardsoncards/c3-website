Rise of the Eldrazi landed on the 23rd of April 2010, closing out the Zendikar block with a set built almost entirely around the enormous, alien Eldrazi titans and the colourless strategies that support them. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards from that set by their current Australian dollar price, from the single costliest card down to the twentieth.

Every card on this list has just one printing in Rise of the Eldrazi, so there are no reprint variants to weigh against each other here. Where our methodology talks about tied prices or multiple printings, it is worth noting up front that this particular set does not throw up any such ties among its top 20, each card sits at its own price point. The ranking itself simply orders each card by that single printing's current price, highest to lowest.

Prices throughout are in Australian dollars and are a snapshot at the time of writing. Singles markets shift constantly with supply, so treat these figures as a point of reference rather than something fixed. The set's split of mythic, rare and uncommon cards across this list gives a reasonable picture of where collector interest in Rise of the Eldrazi currently sits, price wise.
