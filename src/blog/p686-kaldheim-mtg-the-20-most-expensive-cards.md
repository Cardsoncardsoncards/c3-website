Kaldheim brought Norse-flavoured legends, snow-covered lands and a run of double-faced god cards to Magic: The Gathering, releasing on the 3rd of February 2023. This list ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by their current price in Australian dollars, drawing on the card pages tracked on our own site alongside listings from eBay Australia.

Several of these cards have more than one printing within Kaldheim itself, whether that's a showcase treatment, a borderless version or another variant. Where a card has multiple printings, it is ranked by whichever printing carries the highest price, and the other printing's price is noted alongside it. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same figure, that tie is called out rather than glossed over.

Prices below are a snapshot. Singles markets shift constantly with supply, reprints and general market movement, so the figures here reflect one point in time rather than a fixed value. Treat the ranking as a guide to how these 20 cards sit relative to one another right now, not as a permanent order.
