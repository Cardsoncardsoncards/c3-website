Mercadian Masques arrived on 4 October 1999, the first set of Magic's Masques block, and it has spent the years since settling into the quieter end of the secondary market. This list ranks the twenty most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price, from the top single card down to twentieth place. It is a snapshot of one moment in a market that never really sits still.

Every card here appears once, even where a card had more than one printing in the set. When that happens, the card is ranked by whichever printing carries the higher price, and if two printings land on exactly the same figure that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, none of the twenty cards below have a second priced printing in this set, so every entry stands on a single figure.

Rishadan Port sits alone at the top, well clear of everything below it, and the gap between first and second place is the widest single step on the whole list. From there the prices ease down through a run of rares before flattening out at the bottom, where the last two entries are separated by nothing at all despite one being a rare and the other a common.

Read the numbers as a point in time rather than a fixed reading. Prices for a set this old drift with supply, with reprints elsewhere, and with ordinary market movement, so treat everything below as current rather than permanent.
