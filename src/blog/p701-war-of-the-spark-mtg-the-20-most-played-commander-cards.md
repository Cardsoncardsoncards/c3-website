War of the Spark landed on 3 May 2019 as one of the more unusual sets Magic has produced, built around a board full of planeswalkers and a Ravnica-sized war to match. It gave Commander players a strange mix of small, efficient uncommons and splashy mythic planeswalkers, and years later that mix still shows up across a huge range of decks. This list ranks the 20 War of the Spark cards played most in Commander, using EDHREC rank as the measure, where a lower rank number means the card turns up in more decklists.

Every card here appears once. Where a card has more than one printing within War of the Spark, we rank it by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank and price it by whichever printing costs the most. If two printings land on exactly the same price, we say so rather than pretending one is somehow ahead of the other. In this particular set, that situation barely arises: nearly every card on the list has a single priced printing, so the headline figure is simply what that printing costs.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot only. Singles markets move week to week, sometimes day to day, so treat every figure here as a point in time rather than a fixed value. If you're chasing any of these for a Commander deck, our own card pages and eBay Australia are both reasonable places to compare current asking prices against what's written below.

## Frequently asked questions

**When did War of the Spark release?**
War of the Spark released on 3 May 2019, and it has been available for purchase ever since.

**Which card is the most played War of the Spark card in Commander?**
Karn's Bastion is the most played, sitting at EDHREC rank 206, the lowest (best) rank of any card in this set.

**How does the methodology handle cards with multiple printings?**
Each card appears once on the list, ranked by its best (lowest) EDHREC rank across all its War of the Spark printings, and priced by whichever printing of it costs the most. Where two printings share an identical price, that tie is stated rather than resolved arbitrarily.

**Do any of the cards on this list have multiple printings within the set?**
In this particular list, every card has just one priced printing within War of the Spark, so there are no ties or split prices to report.

**What does EDHREC rank actually measure?**
