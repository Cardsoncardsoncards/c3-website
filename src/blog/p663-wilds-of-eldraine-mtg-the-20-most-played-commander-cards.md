Wilds of Eldraine landed on 2023-09-08, mixing a fairytale-flavoured Standard set with a handful of cards that immediately found homes in Commander decks. This list ranks the 20 most played cards from the set in that format, using EDHREC rank as the measure of play, from number 1, the most played, down to number 20.

Some of these cards were printed more than once within Wilds of Eldraine itself, whether as a bonus showcase treatment, a promotional version, or a normal frame alongside a foil or borderless equivalent. Where a card has several printings in the set, it appears once on this list, ranked by whichever printing has the best (lowest) EDHREC rank, and priced by whichever printing carries the highest price. If two printings happen to land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than smoothed over.

Every price below is a snapshot in Australian dollars, and singles pricing shifts with supply and demand over time. What is listed here reflects the figures on hand at the time of writing, not a live feed, so expect some drift if you check again later. For buying, Cards on Cards on Cards lists these singles on our own card pages, and eBay Australia is another common source for the same printings.
