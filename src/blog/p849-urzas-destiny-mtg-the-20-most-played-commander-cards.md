Urza's Destiny closed out the Urza block back on 1999-06-07, and it remains one of the more heavily mined sets from that era when it comes to Commander play. This list ranks the twenty most played cards from the set using EDHREC rank, where a lower number means the card shows up in more decks. It is a measure of play frequency, not price, so a cheap common can easily outrank an expensive rare.

Every card here appears once, even where a card had multiple printings in the set. In those cases the rank shown is the best (lowest) EDHREC rank across those printings, and the price shown is the highest priced printing among them. Where two printings land on exactly the same price, that tie is called out rather than quietly resolved. As it happens, none of the twenty cards below had more than one priced printing in this set, so the maths stays simple here.

Prices are quoted in Australian dollars and are a snapshot taken at time of writing. Singles markets move, sometimes by a lot, so treat every figure as a point in time rather than a fixed number. You can buy the cards on this list through the card pages on our own site or through eBay Australia, and the two markets will not always agree with each other on price.
