Limited Edition Beta is the second printing of Magic: The Gathering's original card pool, released on 4 October 1993 as a direct follow up to Limited Edition Alpha. It is one of the oldest and most closely watched sets in the game's history, and its cards form the backbone of many Vintage and Commander price lists to this day. This post ranks the 20 most expensive cards from the set by current price in Australian dollars, from number 1 down to number 20.

Each card on this list appears once, even where a card had more than one printing within the set. Where that happens, the card is ranked by its single highest priced printing, and any printings sharing an identical price are noted as a tie rather than quietly resolved. For this particular list, every entry has exactly one priced printing in the set, so no such ties or multi-printing gaps appear here, but the methodology stays the same for future sets where they do.

Every figure quoted below is a snapshot taken at one point in time. Magic card prices, especially for a set as old as Beta, move constantly with supply, condition grading and general market activity, so treat these numbers as a picture of where things stood when this list was compiled rather than a fixed reference point.
