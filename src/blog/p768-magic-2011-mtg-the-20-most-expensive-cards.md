Magic 2011 was released on 2010-07-16 as one of Wizards of the Coast's core sets, the kind of release built to reintroduce fundamentals rather than lean on a single block story. Its card pool spans the full rarity spread, from commons that saw heavy limited play to mythics that still turn up in cube and constructed conversations today. This list looks at the 20 most expensive cards from that set by current Australian dollar price.

Every card in this set appears here only once, and each one is ranked by its single highest priced printing. Where two printings share an identical price, that tie is called out directly rather than resolved by some arbitrary tiebreaker. In this particular list, every card has just one printing carrying a price, so no ties or multi-printing gaps appear, but the methodology stays the same for consistency across our set coverage.

Serra Ascendant sits at the top of the list at AU$45.19, comfortably clear of everything below it. From there the list runs down through a mix of mythic and rare staples before settling into uncommons and commons near the bottom. As with any singles market, these figures are a snapshot only, and prices on both our own card pages and eBay Australia will shift over time.
