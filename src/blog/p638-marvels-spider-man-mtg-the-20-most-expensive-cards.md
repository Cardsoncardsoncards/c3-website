Marvel's Spider-Man is Magic: The Gathering's web-slinging excursion into the Marvel Universe, released on 26 September 2025. This post ranks its 20 most expensive cards by Australian dollar price, from number 1 down to number 20, using the highest priced printing of each card as the figure that determines its place on the list.

Several cards in this set were printed more than once, and where that happens we've used the top price to rank the card, then listed the other printings alongside it. If two printings of a card happen to share the exact same price, that tie is noted rather than resolved by any arbitrary tiebreaker. This keeps the ranking honest to what the market is actually showing across every version in circulation.

Prices below are a snapshot and will move as the market for this set settles in the weeks and months after release. Singles can be picked up through our own card pages or through eBay Australia, and figures on either platform will drift over time as supply and buying patterns change. Treat every number here as accurate to the day it was recorded, not as a permanent fixture.
